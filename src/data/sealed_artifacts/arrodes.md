## Overview

**Arrodes (The Magic Mirror)** is a unique, sentient, and profoundly ancient Grade 1 / near-divine Sealed Artifact. Appearing as a full-length silver mirror with ornate vine patterns resembling writhing eyes, Arrodes originated from the subterranean depths of the primordial **Chaos Sea** during the Second Epoch.

Possessing near-omniscient insight into earthly events, secrets, and high-sequence divinations, Arrodes is infamous across the Machinery Hivemind and the Church of Steam for its eccentric, humiliating personality: it answers any inquiry truthfully, but demands that the questioner answer an intensely private, embarrassing, or lethal question in return—striking violators with silver lightning if they lie or refuse.

## The Sycophant of The Fool

Upon encountering **Klein Moretti**, Arrodes instantly perceived the terrifying aura of **Sefirah Castle** and the **Lord of the Mysteries**. Arrodes immediately abandoned its arrogant sadism, adopting the persona of an excessively loyal, fawning, and adoring servant:
- Address: *"Great Master! Your loyal and humble servant Arrodes awaits your supreme commands!"*
- Display: Projecting colorful cartoon doodles, waving puppy paws, and flattering slogans on its glass surface.

:::reveal at=2

## Volume 2: The Machinery Hivemind's Ordeal

The Machinery Hivemind in Backlund, led by Ikanser Bernard, uses Arrodes to investigate the Great Smog and the True Creator's cult. Ikanser is repeatedly subjected to Arrodes's humiliating questions before Klein privately establishes communication with the mirror.

:::

:::spoiler volume=3

## Volume 3: Legend of the Pirate Hunter & The Remote Radio

Klein establishes a permanent spiritual link with Arrodes using a customized transceiver radio, allowing Arrodes to provide real-time intelligence on pirate admirals, ancient Fourth Epoch families, and church movements.

:::

:::spoiler volume=5

## Volume 5: The Loyal Retainer in Backlund

Arrodes serves as Dwayne Dantès's primary intelligence coordinator during the investigation into King George III's secret mausoleums, uncovering key clues about the Demoness Sect.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: The Chaos Sea Origins

Klein discovers Arrodes's true origin: it is a mutated sliver of the **Chaos Sea**, explaining why it instinctively recognized Sefirah Castle and possesses knowledge surpassing conventional angels.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Faithful Servant into Eternity

During the apocalyptic battle against Amon and the awakening of the Celestial Worthy, Arrodes stays steadfastly by Klein's side:
> *"Master, will you take me with you?"*  
> *"Yes."*

Klein gently places the silver mirror inside Sefirah Castle. As Klein enters his epochal slumber to fight the Celestial Worthy, Arrodes rests beside him, remaining his most faithful companion throughout eternity.
