## Overview

**Leymano's Travels** is an extraordinary Grade 2 / Grade 1 magical spellbook artifact created by an ancient demigod of the **Abraham Family (Door Pathway)**. Bound in supple goatskin parchment with shimmering bronze clasps, it was gifted by Dorian Gray Abraham to **Fors Wall (The Magician)** to assist her rapid growth as a **Scribe**.

## Powers & The Art of Recording

- **Spell Recording**: Allows a Scribe to record Beyonder abilities, spells, and mythical phenomena witnessed in real-time or demonstrated by allies. Once recorded, the ability can be reproduced later at a fraction of the original caster's spiritual cost.
- **Versatile Combat Arsenal**: Pages can hold diverse spells from all 22 pathways, from low-level flame tricks to devastating saint-tier divine strikes.
- **Page Degradation**: Each recorded spell can typically only be cast once or a limited number of times before the inked handwriting fades, requiring re-recording.
