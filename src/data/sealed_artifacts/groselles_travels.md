## Overview

**Groselle's Travels** is an extraordinary Grade 1 / Grade 0 magical book artifact authored in the Second Epoch by the ancient Dragon of Imagination **Ankewelt (Spectator Pathway)**. Bound in coarse, dark dragon-hide parchment, the book is not merely a record of stories; it is an entire, living pocket dimension that sucks anyone who spills blood on its cover into its narrative reality.

## The World Inside the Book

Those trapped inside find themselves living alongside legendary historical figures from across thousands of years who were sucked into the story at different epochs:
- **Groselle**: A brave giant warrior from the Second Epoch Giant King's Court.
- **Mobet Medici**: A Fourth Epoch human Viscount and noble scion.
- **Siatas**: An elven singer from the ancient Elven Kingdom.
- **Longman**: An ascetic priest of the Church of the Lord of Storms.
- **The King of the North**: A ferocious mutated frost dragon who serves as the eternal final boss of the book's narrative loop.

:::reveal at=3

## Volume 3: Legend of the Pirate Hunter & The Frozen Expedition

Aboard the *Future*, Klein Moretti, Edwina Edwards, and Danitz are pulled into Groselle's Travels. Klein organizes the diverse historical heroes into an expedition, slaying the King of the North and unlocking the mechanism to escape back to the real world.

:::

:::spoiler volume=4

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past & The Passing of Heroes

Upon exiting the book, the ancient heroes, whose natural lifespans had long expired thousands of years ago, rapidly age and peacefully pass away in the real world. Klein reverently collects their remains and Beyonder characteristics, honoring their timeless bravery.

:::

:::spoiler volume=6

## Volume 6: Light of the City of Silver & The Giant Court Clues

Klein utilizes the memories and ancient Jotun dialogue shared by Groselle to decipher the layout, defensive traps, and secret entrances of the **Giant King's Court** in the Forsaken Land of the Gods.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Hall of Imagination

In Volume 8, Groselle's Travels rests in the Church of the Fool's archives, commemorated as a testament to the enduring human spirit across the epochs of history.
