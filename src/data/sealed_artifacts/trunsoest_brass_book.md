## Overview

The **Trunsoest Brass Book** is an apocalyptic Grade 0 Sealed Artifact that manifests the living conceptual **Uniqueness of the Justiciar Pathway**. Bound in heavy, cold brass with razor-sharp metal pages, it originates from Emperor **Trunsoest (The Night Emperor)** of the Fourth Epoch.

The book possesses autonomous, tyrannical consciousness: it automatically writes new legal statutes into reality with shimmering golden ink, immediately executing anyone within its territory who violates its arbitrary laws.

## The Living Laws of Tyranny

- **Absolute Legal Proclamations**: Decrees bizarre, escalating laws over entire cities (e.g., *"No Speech," "No Movement," "No Spellcasting," "No Breathing"*).
- **Automated Execution**: The moment a law is broken, the book channels invisible conceptual guillotines that decapitate or crush the offender without requiring physical presence.

:::reveal at=7

## Volume 7: The Belltaine City Catastrophe

In Volume 7, the Trunsoest Brass Book awakens in Belltaine City along the Loen-Feysac border. It systematically imprisons and executes thousands of citizens and soldiers with escalating draconian laws. 

Klein Moretti (as Merlin Hermes) and the Church of the Evernight Goddess execute a daring coordinated strike, using concealments and legal loopholes to restrain the book and seal the Justiciar Uniqueness inside Saint Samuel Cathedral.

:::

## Volume 8: The Law of the Cosmos

In Volume 8, the Trunsoest Brass Book is safely contained under the joint oversight of the Church of Evernight and the Tarot Club, awaiting a worthy candidate (such as **Xio Derecha**) to accommodate its authority during post-war reconstruction.
