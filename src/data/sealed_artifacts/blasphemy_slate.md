## Overview

The **Blasphemy Slate** refers to two legendary, supreme stone tablets that document the absolute metaphysical laws of the universe. Inscribed in ancient mystical script, they reveal the complete 22 divine pathways, potion formulas from Sequence 9 to Sequence 0, acting methods, ritual requirements, neighboring pathway compatibility, Sefirot connections, and the ascension paths to **Great Old Ones / Pillars**.

- **First Blasphemy Slate**: Formed in the Second Epoch from the shattered remnants of the primordial **Original Creator**, discovered and interpreted by the **Ancient Sun God**.
- **Second Blasphemy Slate**: Formed in the Third Epoch upon the tragic fall and cannibalization of the Ancient Sun God, capturing his final divine revelations before being recovered by **Adam**.

:::reveal at=6

## Volume 6: Light of the City of Silver & Chernobyl

Klein Moretti discovers the truth of the Blasphemy Slates after journeying through the Forsaken Land of the Gods and uncovering Chernobyl, realizing that human history and mysticism were systematically decoded through these divine tablets.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: Adam's Masterwork

**Adam** wields the Second Blasphemy Slate during the World War and the Battle of Gods. Using its primordial authority, Adam orchestrates the fusion between himself and the True Creator to awaken **God Almighty**.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Blueprint of Creation

In Volume 8, the Blasphemy Slate stands as the foundational scientific manual for Earth's defense against the Outer Deities, providing the exact pathways and Sefirot configurations Klein Moretti and the Orthodox Gods use to safeguard humanity.
