## Overview

**Artificial Death** was a catastrophic, semi-sentient Grade 0 divine anomaly engineered over centuries by the radical **Numinous Episcopate** in the Southern Continent. Believing that the ancient **Death (Salinger)** could be resurrected through mortal engineering, the cult fused high-sequence **Death Pathway** characteristics with the collective unconsciousness of millions of deceased believers, the sacred **Feather of the Death Bird**, and the primordial whispers of the **River of Eternal Darkness**.

However, lacking the accommodation of a genuine True God, Artificial Death developed into an erratic, slumbering nightmare entity whose subconscious discharges periodically triggered mass dying events across the Balam Empire.

## Disasters & Underworld Radiance

- **Spiritual Necrosis Wave**: Periodically emits a silent, conceptual frequency that instantly extinguishes the vitality of thousands of living organisms within a continental radius.
- **Mutated Underworld Domain**: Transforms physical cities into spectral graveyards where living citizens become wandering soulless zombies.
