## Overview

The **Cards of Blasphemy** are 22 transcendent mythical artifacts crafted by Emperor **Roselle Gustav** during the Fifth Epoch. Using high-grade mystical materials and his authority as a Crafting demigod (Savant Pathway), Roselle inscribed the absolute truths of the **First Blasphemy Slate** into 22 tarot cards, preserving the complete potion formulas, ritual requirements, acting methods, and neighboring pathway connections for all 22 pathways from Sequence 9 to Sequence 0.

Disguised by powerful anti-divination wards, the cards appear as ordinary bookmarks or parchment sheets until infused with specific spiritual frequencies.

## The Cards & Their Divine Sovereign Mantles

Each Card of Blasphemy grants its bearer immense supernatural advantages:
- **Complete Sequence Blueprint**: Reveals every secret ingredient, mythical ritual, and acting principle of the corresponding pathway.
- **Anti-Divination & Fate Concealment**: Shielding the bearer from high-sequence tracking and astrological scrying.
- **Direct Characteristic Attraction**: Subtly bending probability to draw the pathway's high-sequence Beyonder characteristics and Uniqueness toward the holder.
