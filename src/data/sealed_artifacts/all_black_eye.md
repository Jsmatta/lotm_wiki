## Overview

The **All-Black Eye** is a hazardous, deeply corrupted high-tier Beyonder relic retrieved by Klein Moretti from the underground ruins of Backlund. It originates from the high-sequence characteristic of the **Nimble Right Hand**, an ancient warrior corrupted by the divine blood of the **True Creator**.

Appearing as a jet-black eyeball that constantly leaks foul, shadowy fluid, it radiates an oppressive aura of depravity and fallen divinity.

## Abilities & Dark Corruption

- **Shadow Manipulation & Corruption**: Allows the user to manipulate physical shadows, forge shadowy spikes, and inflict psychological degradation and blasphemous madness upon targets.
- **The True Creator's Ravings**: Radiates the continuous, deafening murmurs of the True Creator. Anyone who gazes directly into the eye without high spiritual protection experiences spontaneous mental breakdown or physical mutation.

:::reveal at=2

## Volume 2: Purification Above the Gray Fog

Klein brings the All-Black Eye to **Sefirah Castle**. Using the isolating power of the gray fog, he suppresses the True Creator's corruptive aura and utilizes the eye to forge the mystical **Sun Emblem** and craft high-grade shadow charms.

:::

:::spoiler volume=3

## Volume 3: The Core of the Marionette

Klein embeds the purified shadow essence of the All-Black Eye into his marionettes, granting them shadowy stealth, night vision, and resistance to mental hexes during naval skirmishes.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: The Chaos Sea Resonance

In Volume 7, as the connection between the True Creator, Adam, and the **Chaos Sea** deepens, Klein recognizes the All-Black Eye as a direct physical manifestation of the Chaos Sea's primordial spiritual magma.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Shadow Reclaimed

Following the divine fusion of Adam and the True Creator into the reconstituted **God Almighty**, the ancient shadow corruption of the All-Black Eye is fully harmonized, resting as an ancient historical relic above Sefirah Castle.

:::
