## Overview

The **Master Key** is a valuable Grade 3 utility artifact of the **Door Pathway (Sequence 9: Apprentice)**. Appearing as a tarnished, antique brass skeleton key with intricate astrological engravings, it was obtained by Klein Moretti in Backlund from the deceased rogue Beyonder "Mr. Eye" (Aura).

## Mystical Abilities & Flaws

- **Universal Phasing & Unlocking**: Can open any mundane physical lock, padlock, vault, or mechanical barrier. In the hands of a Beyonder, it can even pierce through thin brick walls and conceptual barriers by opening an ephemeral dimensional door.
- **Spatial Disorientation Curse**: The user suffers from a chronic, uncontrollable sense of direction loss, constantly taking wrong turns and wandering into bizarre, unintended alleys or dangerous supernatural encounters.
- **The Cosmos Whisper**: During the Full Moon, anyone holding the key hears distant, agonizing murmurs originating from the cosmos where **Bethel Abraham (Mr. Door)** is sealed.
