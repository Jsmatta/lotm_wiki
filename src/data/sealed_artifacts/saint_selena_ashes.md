## Overview

**Saint Selena's Ashes (Sealed Artifact 1-42)** is a sacred Grade 1 holy relic belonging to the **Church of the Evernight Goddess**, sealed beneath the Chanis Gate of the Tingen City diocese. Contained within an ornate silver urn, the ashes belong to Saint Selena, a high-sequence saint of the **Sleepless Pathway (Darkness Pathway)** who lived during the Fourth Epoch.

## Powers & Sacred Authority

- **Absolute Pacification & Eternal Slumber**: Emanates a powerful aura of peace that pacifies raging evil spirits, soothes mental madness, and forcibly induces deep slumber in living targets and undead creatures.
- **Netherworld Suppression**: Emits a conceptual darkness barrier that neutralizes high-tier death curses and banishes wandering wraiths.
- **Negative Rebound**: Prolonged unshielded exposure causes human handlers to gradually lose consciousness, fall into eternal comas, or experience irreversible cessation of bodily functions.

## Volume 1: Clown & The Tingen Betrayal

Former Archbishop **Ince Zangwill**, guided by the script of **0-08**, infiltrates the underground vault of Tingen's Blackthorn Security Company. Slaying Captain Dunn Smith and Klein Moretti, Ince steals Saint Selena's Ashes alongside the Antigonus Family Notebook to perform forbidden demigod ascension rituals.

:::

:::spoiler volume=4

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past & The Final Rest

In the ruins of East Balam in the Southern Continent, Klein Moretti, Leonard Mitchell, and Daly Simone corner Ince Zangwill. Following Ince's execution, Klein and Leonard recover Saint Selena's Ashes, reverently returning the sacred relic to the Church of the Evernight Goddess to honor Dunn Smith's sacrifice.

:::

## Volume 8: The Goddess's Eternal Night

In Volume 8, with the Evernight Goddess accommodating the Death and Twilight Giant pathways toward the Great Old One **Eternal Darkness**, the holy soul of Saint Selena rests peacefully in the divine Astral Kingdom, remembered forever by the Nighthawks.
