## Overview

**Pale Death** is a catastrophic Grade 0 Sealed Artifact of the **Death Pathway (Sequence 1: Pale Emperor)**. Manifesting as a skeletal mask carved from the calcified skull of an ancient underworld demigod and veiled in rotting funerary silk, it channels the primordial chill of the **River of Eternal Darkness**.

## Terrors of Pale Extinction

- **The Aura of Extinction**: Radiates a chilling pale aura that extinguishes biological cellular metabolism, causing trees to wither, rivers to freeze into spectral ice, and human bodies to dissolve into ash within miles.
- **Underworld Dominion**: Commands the spirits of ancient Fourth Epoch kings and forces wandering wraiths into fanatical servitude.

:::reveal at=7

## Volume 7: The Battle of Gods

Pale Death is utilized by high-ranking conspirators in the astral conflict attempting to weaken the Orthodox Gods. Klein Moretti and the Evernight Goddess intercept the artifact, suppressing its deathly chill with the holy waters of the Underworld.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The River's Embrace

Following the Evernight Goddess's accommodation of the Death Pathway Uniqueness and the River of Eternal Darkness, Pale Death is permanently pacified, integrated into the tranquil nocturnal majesty of the Astral Kingdom.

:::
