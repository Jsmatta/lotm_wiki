## Overview

The **Sea God Scepter (Staff of the Sea God)** is a powerful demigod-tier divine artifact created from the Sequence 3 / Sequence 4 characteristic of the ancient serpent **Kalvetua** and deep-sea white coral. Wielded exclusively by **Klein Moretti** from within **Sefirah Castle**, it serves as Klein's primary conduit to answer prayers and manifest meteorological miracles across the oceans.

Resembling a majestic staff forged from calcified white bone and shimmering aquamarine gemstones, it crackles continuously with arcs of blue celestial lightning.

## Divine Authorities & Powers

- **Storm & Hurricane Summoning**: Generates devastating oceanic tempests, localized typhoons, and water spouts across nautical leagues.
- **Celestial Lightning Barrage**: Calls down concentrated blue thunderbolts that vaporize warships and incinerate high-sequence spirits.
- **Deep-Sea Mastery**: Manipulates oceanic water pressure, grants water breathing, and commands oceanic creatures.
- **Prayer Projection**: Intercepts and projects answers to thousands of prayers directed toward the Sea God or The Fool.

## Volume 3: Legend of the Pirate Hunter

Klein claims the scepter following Kalvetua's demise in the Rorsted Archipelago. Transporting it above the gray fog, Klein uses Sefirah Castle to suppress the serpent's lingering madness and begins answering the desperate prayers of indigenous islanders, establishing the legend of The Fool.

:::

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past & Divine Retribution

Klein unleashes the scepter from Sefirah Castle to smite slave traders, corrupt colonial administrators, and sea monsters threatening innocent maritime travelers.

While trapped in the Forsaken Land of the Gods, Klein leverages his connection to Sefirah Castle and the Sea God Scepter to channel distant divine lightning, assisting Derrick Berg and the Tarot Club during critical moments.
