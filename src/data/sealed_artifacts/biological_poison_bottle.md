## Overview

The **Biological Poison Bottle** is a lethal Grade 2 tactical artifact of the **Chained Pathway (Zombie / Werewolf)** and **Corpse Collector Pathway**. Encased within a frosted glass bottle wrapped in thick lead foil, it was retrieved by Klein Moretti after eliminating assassins tied to the Intis diplomatic conspiracy in Backlund.

## Abilities & Corrosive Mist

- **Lethal Toxic Emanation**: Upon opening the lead stopper, the bottle releases an invisible, rapidly expanding cloud of necrotic poison gas that causes respiratory failure, muscle liquefaction, and cellular rot in living targets within seconds.
- **Biological Infection**: Corrodes physical armor, dulls spiritual perception, and inflicts persistent rotting curses on Beyonders below demigod rank.
- **Negative Backlash**: If kept unstoppered for more than five minutes, the poison will permanently seep into the user's skin, turning them into a rotting ghoul.
