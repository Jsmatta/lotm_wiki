## Overview

The **Antigonus Family's Notebook** is a notorious, cursed historical manuscript of extreme danger (Grade 1 / near Grade 0 hazard). Bound in dark, weathered leather and written in ancient Hermes, it originates from the **Antigonus Family**—one of the foundational demigod bloodlines of the Fourth Epoch's Solomon and Tudor Empires who held divine authority over the **Seer Pathway**.

The notebook contains the complete potion formulas and acting secrets of the Seer pathway from Sequence 9 up to Sequence 2, but carries a terrifying mental curse: anyone who reads it without demigod protection is driven into suicidal delirium, spiritual possession, or violent transformation into a mutated puppet.

## Abilities & Curvilinear Curse

- **Occult Knowledge Repository**: Details the mystical rituals, potion ingredients, and acting methods of the Seer, Clown, Magician, Faceless, Marionettist, Bizarro Sorcerer, and Scholar of Yore sequences.
- **Psychic Contagion & Suicide Compulsion**: Radiates a subtle, irresistible mental corruption that manipulates coincidences to draw seekers together and forces them to commit bizarre suicides (such as shooting oneself while smiling).

## Volume 1: Clown & The Tingen Tragedy

The notebook is purchased by student Welch McGovern in Tingen, ensnaring **Klein Moretti** (Zhou Mingrui) and causing Welch and Naya's horrific deaths. Stolen by the traitorous Archbishop **Ince Zangwill** under the direction of **0-08**, the notebook serves as the catalyst for the climactic battle that destroys the Tingen Nighthawks.

:::

:::spoiler volume=4

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past & Saint Samuel's Vault

Following the Tingen crisis, the notebook is secured inside the underground Chanis Gate of **Saint Samuel Cathedral** in Backlund. 

Klein Moretti, disguised as the wealthy philanthropist **Dwayne Dantès**, uses his connection with the Church of the Evernight Goddess and the Spirit World to secretly inspect the notebook behind Chanis Gate, copying the formula for **Sequence 3: Scholar of Yore** and **Sequence 2: Miracle Invoker**.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & The Hornacis Peak

Klein learns the ultimate truth of the Antigonus Family: the notebook was the subconscious beacon of the **Half-Fool Antigonus**, who lost control and fell into eternal slumber on the peak of the Hornacis mountain range.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Awakening of The Fool

Klein reaches the Hornacis peak and accommodates the Fool Uniqueness from the slumbering Antigonus. With the ancient demigod pacified and returned to a peaceful state by the Evernight Goddess, the notebook's malicious curse permanently dissipates, preserved as a historical relic of the Fifth Epoch.

:::
