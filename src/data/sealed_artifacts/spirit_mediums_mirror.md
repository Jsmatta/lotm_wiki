## Overview

**Sealed Artifact 3-0271 (The Spirit Medium's Mirror)** is a Grade 3 utility relic preserved behind Chanis Gate by the Tingen Nighthawks. Manifesting as a silver-rimmed handheld vanity mirror crafted from polished obsidian and enchanted silver, it channels the supernatural powers of the **Death Pathway (Sequence 6: Spirit Medium)**.

## Mystical Properties & Divination Rules

- **Spiritual Channeling**: Allows the user to communicate with lingering souls, project questions into the spirit world, and display illusory photographic scenes of past crimes.
- **Negative Backlash**: If an improper or prohibited question is asked, or if the mirror is held for more than ten minutes, the user's reflection will step out of the glass to strangle the handler, or attract malicious wandering wraiths.
