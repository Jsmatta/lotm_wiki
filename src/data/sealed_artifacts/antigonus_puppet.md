## Overview

**Sealed Artifact 2-049 (The Antigonus Family Puppet)** is a Grade 2 hazardous relic guarded by the **Church of the Evernight Goddess**. Appearing as an antique, jointed wooden marionette dressed in Fourth Epoch aristocratic attire, it is a dangerous physical manifestation of the **Seer Pathway (Sequence 5: Marionettist)** created from ancient Antigonus family lineage remnants.

## Abilities & Sealing Rules

- **Spirit-Body Thread Control**: Within a 30-meter radius, 2-049 passively seizes the invisible spirit-body threads of all nearby warm-blooded organisms. Targets experience gradual muscle stiffness, slowed cognitive processing, and complete paralysis within minutes, transforming permanently into marionettes.
- **Continuous Motion Sealing Rule**: The only way to disrupt 2-049's thread-control mechanism is continuous, rhythmic physical motion. Guardians transporting the artifact must constantly swing their limbs, jump, dance, or jog without pausing.
