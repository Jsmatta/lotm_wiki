## Overview

The **Die of Probability (Sealed Artifact 0-08 equivalent of Fate)** is a terrifying Grade 0 Sealed Artifact embodying the supreme authority of the **Wheel of Fortune Pathway (Monster Pathway)**. Manifesting as an ancient silver-cast die with six distinct faces numbered 1 through 6, it directly alters the probabilistic fabric of reality.

## The Six Faces of Destiny

Whenever the die is rolled or left unsealed:
- **Roll 1 (Absolute Calamity)**: Unleashes catastrophic misfortune upon everyone in the vicinity, triggering sudden heart attacks, fatal structural collapses, or devastating meteor strikes.
- **Roll 2–3 (Adverse Misfortune)**: Induces persistent bad luck, equipment failures, and minor accidents.
- **Roll 4–5 (Favorable Fortune)**: Enhances luck, allowing attacks to hit critical spots and plans to succeed smoothly.
- **Roll 6 (Absolute Miracle)**: Grants unfathomable good fortune where improbable coincidences resolve insurmountable crises instantaneously.
