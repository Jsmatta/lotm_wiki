## Overview

The **Devil's Oil Painting** is a horrifying Grade 0 Sealed Artifact of the **Abyss Pathway (Devil Pathway)**. Painted in dark, viscous pigments blended with human blood and sulfur, the canvas depicts an abyssal hellscape filled with flayed souls, boiling magma rivers, and towering horned demonic entities.

## Psychic Torment & Abyssal Corruption

- **Visual Contagion**: Anyone who gazes upon the painting—even through photographs or spiritual reflections—is struck with immediate homicidal mania, depraved sexual sadism, and spontaneous bodily combustion.
- **Abyssal Gate Opening**: The painting serves as a dimensional anchor, allowing high-level devils from the subterranean abyss to project their malicious claws into physical reality.

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & The Backlund Hunt

Klein Moretti and Miss Justice (Audrey Hall) uncover the Devil's Oil Painting hidden inside a secret aristocratic vault in Backlund, neutralizing its psychic infection through advanced Spectator dream therapy and Sefirah Castle's purification.
