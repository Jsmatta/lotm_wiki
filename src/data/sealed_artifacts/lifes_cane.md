## Overview

**Life's Cane** is a magnificent Grade 1 divine healing relic of the **Mother Pathway (Sequence 3: Fecundity Bishop / Sequence 2: Desolate Matron)**. Crafted from the petrified branch of an ancient world tree and entwined with fresh, blossoming white lilies that never wither, it radiates an overwhelming aura of biological renewal, fertility, and life essence.

## Healing Miracles & Side Effects

- **Miraculous Regeneration**: Instantly knits severed limbs, cures lethal physical trauma, reverses terminal organ failure, and purges virulent biological blights across an entire hospital ward.
- **Vitality Overgrowth Curse**: Living targets exposed to the cane's unchecked radiation experience hyperactive cellular division, causing flowers, vines, and fungal blooms to sprout uncontrollably from their skin, ears, and eyes.

:::reveal at=5

## Volume 5: Red Priest & The Backlund Relief

During the devastating airship bombings of Backlund in the World War, Klein Moretti (acting as Dwayne Dantès and the legendary healing Clown) uses Life's Cane to heal thousands of mangled civilians and impoverished East Borough victims, accelerating his digestion of the **Sequence 3: Scholar of Yore** potion.

:::

:::spoiler volume=6

## Volume 6: Historical Summoning

Even after returning the physical cane, Klein frequently summons **historical projections** of Life's Cane from the historical void to resurrect fallen allies and cleanse extreme physical injuries during deep dungeon expeditions.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Earth Mother's Blessing

In Volume 8, with the Earth Mother (Lilith) standing as an ally of The Fool, Life's Cane is enshrined within the Backlund Cathedral of Healing, continuing to provide miraculous relief to wartime survivors.
