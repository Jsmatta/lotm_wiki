## Overview

The **Flower of Blood** is a sinister Grade 2 combat artifact of the **Chained Pathway (Sequence 5: Wraith / Werewolf)** associated with the bloodthirsty **Indulgence Faction** of the **Rose School of Thought**. Manifesting as a crystalline, crimson rose whose petals appear to be formed from coagulated living arterial blood, it exudes a sickeningly sweet fragrance of decay.

## Abilities & Thirst

- **Vampiric Vitality & Regeneration**: Ingesting or grafting the flower onto one's flesh grants explosive physical strength, supernatural agility, and rapid cellular regeneration that knits severed muscles and bones in moments.
- **Blood Spike Eruption**: The user can cause the blood of injured adversaries to burst into sharp, thorned crimson spikes from within their own veins.
- **Negative Backlash**: Inflicts insatiable, frenzied bloodlust. Handlers who hold the flower for prolonged periods lose cognitive reason and turn into rabid predatory monsters.

Klein Moretti and Sharron encounter the Flower of Blood during their operations against Rose School of Thought assassins in Backlund. Sharron utilizes her Temperance techniques to neutralize the artifact's maddening temptations.

## Volume 5: Red Priest & Tactical Suppression

Klein stores the Flower of Blood inside Sefirah Castle, preventing its bloodlust from contaminating the mortal world while studying its biological resonance with the Chained pathway.

## Volume 8: The Cleansing of the South
