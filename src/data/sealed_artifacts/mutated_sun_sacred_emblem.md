## Overview

The **Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem** is a sentimental and practical Grade 2 purification artifact crafted by Klein Moretti during his early days in Tingen. Originating from an ordinary silver holy emblem of the **Eternal Blazing Sun**, it underwent bizarre mystical mutation after being exposed to anomalous spiritual discharges and purified above Sefirah Castle.

Appearing as a warm, polished silver sun amulet surrounded by eight radiant rays, it constantly radiates soothing solar warmth.

## Powers & Solar Blessings

- **Purifying Solar Radiance**: Unleashes concentrated beams of holy light that incinerate low-tier wraiths, dissolve corrosive zombie poisons, and banish lingering curses.
- **Holy Water Creation**: Immersing the emblem in pure spring water blesses it with potent purification properties suited for exorcisms.
- **Negative Backlash**: The bearer experiences mild, continuous praise-singing urges and suffers minor spiritual fatigue if worn in pitch-black environments.
