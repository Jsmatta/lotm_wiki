## Overview

The **Fate Siphon** is a devious Grade 1 Sealed Artifact of the **Error Pathway (Sequence 4: Parasite / Sequence 3: Mentor of Deceit)**. Resembling an exquisite, pocket-sized silver stopwatch whose gears turn counterclockwise, it commands the treacherous authority of **Theft** over abstract metaphysical concepts.

## Abilities & Conceptual Thievery

- **Fate & Identity Theft**: Siphons the good fortune, identity, spiritual perceptions, or planned actions of an adversary, transferring them temporarily to the wielder.
- **Time Distortion**: Delays the onset of lethal injuries or hostile spells by "stealing" five seconds of cause-and-effect.
- **Negative Curse**: The wielder's own destiny becomes porous and fragile, attracting the attention of **Amon's avatars** who can detect the siphon's temporal vibrations across vast distances.
