## Overview

The **Magic Wishing Lamp** is one of the most terrifying, reality-warping Grade 0 Sealed Artifacts in existence. Appearing as an ordinary, tarnished brass oil lamp of Fourth Epoch design, it houses the sealed consciousness and core authority of the **Son of Chaos**, a primordial **Outer Deity / Great Old One** who was defeated and sealed by the **Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth** in the pre-epoch era.

## The Three Wishes & Malicious Distortion

The lamp houses the **Genie (Son of Chaos)**, who is bound by ancient cosmic rules to grant three wishes to whoever rubs the lamp:
- **Reality-Warping Miracles**: The lamp can fulfill almost any wish—curing impossible divine curses, destroying demigods, materializing immense wealth, or altering spatial geometry.
- **The Malicious Twist**: Every wish is fulfilled with ruthless, ironic malevolence designed to cause the wielder's horrific demise (e.g., wishing for a cure turns the user into an immortal, brainless stone statue).
- **The Third Wish Trap**: The third wish invariably grants the Genie an opportunity to shatter the seal and reclaim his terrifying cosmic body.

## Volume 5: Red Priest & Klein's Gamble

Klein acquires the Magic Wishing Lamp and takes it into Sefirah Castle. Using the supreme authority of the gray fog, Klein communicates directly with the Genie, exploiting his desire for liberation to obtain crucial cosmic intelligence while strictly refusing to make careless wishes.

:::

## Volume 7: The Cosmic Bargain

During the apotheosis crisis and the Battle of Gods, Klein negotiates with the Genie, promising to return his physical seal to the cosmos once Klein stabilizes the astral barrier, securing the Genie's reluctant assistance against Amon.
