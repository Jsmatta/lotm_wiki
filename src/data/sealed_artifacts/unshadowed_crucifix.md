## Overview

The **Unshadowed Crucifix** is an extraordinary Grade 1 Sealed Artifact of the **Sun Pathway (Sequence 4: Unshadowed)**. Crafted from pure, fossilized divine bone and radiating blinding white luminescence, it embodies the ultimate conceptual authority of **Purification** and **Unshadowing**.

## The Miracle of Characteristic Separation

- **Characteristic Cleansing**: When the crucifix is driven into a living Beyonder or placed in contact with a corrupted mystical item, it violently purges all foreign, excess, or incompatible Beyonder characteristics, expelling them as pure, uncorrupted crystals without killing the target.
- **Evil Annihilation**: Vaporizes high-level spirits, shadows, and demonic curses within seconds.
- **Negative Backlash**: Prolonged physical contact gradually purges the user's *own* Beyonder characteristics, eventually stripping them down to an ordinary mortal.

## Volume 5: Red Priest & The Cleansing of Creeping Hunger

Klein Moretti and Derrick Berg utilize the Unshadowed Crucifix to purify **Creeping Hunger**, permanently eliminating its terrifying daily requirement to devour human souls and transforming it into a safe, obedient glove.

:::

## Volume 6: Light of the City of Silver & The Tarot Club Exchange

When Klein is captured by Amon in the Forsaken Land of the Gods, Alger Wilson (The Hanged Man) requests the Unshadowed Crucifix through the Tarot Club, using its solar authority to purify marine artifacts and resist the blasphemous corruption spreading across the sea.

Klein employs the crucifix during high-sequence demigod battles, utilizing its unshadowing properties to strip corrupted characteristics from hostile saints of the Rose School of Thought.
