## Overview

**Madam Arianna (The Servant of Concealment)** is the supreme Matriarch of the **Church of the Evernight Goddess** and a supreme **Sequence 2: Servant of Concealment Angel** of the **Darkness Pathway (Sleepless Pathway)**. As the personal emissary and strongest terrestrial agent of the Evernight Goddess, Arianna embodies absolute **Concealment**, **Erasure**, **Dark Pacification**, and **Night Dominion**.

Dressed in simple, unadorned black nun robes, walking barefoot across the snow and stone, and possessing an ethereal, serene beauty, Arianna speaks in gentle, sparse sentences while wielding unfathomable concealment powers capable of hiding entire cities, memories, and divine phenomena from cosmic detection.

## Concealment Authority & Divine Interventions

- **Concealment Domain**: Isolating spaces into pocket dimensions where no sound, light, divination, or historical resonance can leak out.
- **Erasure of Existence**: Temporarily or permanently concealing concepts, identities, physical objects, and life forces.
