## Overview

Leonard Mitchell is a charming, poetic, and observant Beyonder who began his career in the Tingen City Nighthawks squad alongside Klein Moretti. Characterized by his dark wavy hair, vivid green eyes, casual aristocratic flair, and the demeanor of a dramatic theatrical protagonist, Leonard conceals a monumental secret beneath his relaxed exterior: his body houses the parasitic astral projection of **Pallez Zoroast**, an ancient Sequence 1 King of Angels from the Marauder / Error Pathway's Zoroast family.

## Beyonder Pathway: Darkness (Sleepless)

Leonard traverses the **Darkness Pathway** (Church of Evernight Goddess), wielding the soothing and terrifying powers of the night.

### Sequence 9: Sleepless
- **Abilities**: Requires only 3–4 hours of sleep per day, granting immense stamina, keen perception in total darkness, enhanced physical reflexes, and spiritual intuition.

### Sequence 8: Midnight Poet
- **Abilities**: Reciting occult poetry infused with supernatural spirituality to induce drowsiness, pacify hostile targets, stir emotional courage in allies, or unleash razor-sharp acoustic sonic attacks.

## Volume 1: Tragedy in Tingen

In Tingen, Leonard considers himself "the protagonist" of the unfolding mystical drama, though he recognizes Klein Moretti's bizarre luck and swift advancements. Following the cataclysmic treason of Ince Zangwill and the death of Captain Dunn Smith, Leonard's romantic delusions shatter. Filled with grief and determination for vengeance, he requests a transfer to the Church's elite rapid-response strike unit: the **Red Gloves**.

:::reveal at=2

## Volume 2: The Faceless & Red Glove Investigations

Leonard trains relentlessly in Saint Selena Cathedral, advancing to **Sequence 7: Nightmare** (entering dreams, interrogating subconscious minds, and trapping enemies in nightmares). He is dispatched to Backlund to investigate the Great Smog and the serial killings in the capital.

:::

:::spoiler volume=4

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past & The Reunion

### Sequence 6: Soul Assurer
- **Abilities**: Pacifying vengeful spirits, commanding undead entities, communicating with the deceased, and severing hostile spiritual threads.

Operating as a Red Glove Captain in Backlund, Leonard investigates the elusive detective Sherlock Moriarty and pirate hunter Gehrman Sparrow, gradually deducing that Klein Moretti never died in Tingen. Through Pallez Zoroast's guidance, Leonard confronts Dwayne Dantès (Klein), forging a secret alliance to hunt Ince Zangwill in the Southern Continent and avenge Dunn Smith and Daly Simone.

:::

:::spoiler volume=5

## Volume 5: The Star of the Tarot Club

### Sequence 5: Spirit Warlock
- **Abilities**: Contracting and commanding a retinue of wraiths, shadows, and spirit-world creatures, employing them for espionage, defensive shields, and soul possession.

Leonard is officially inducted into the Tarot Club by Mr. Fool, receiving the Major Arcana title **The Star**. He serves as the bridge between Pallez Zoroast and Klein, coordinating the urban sweep across Backlund to hunt and purge parasitic avatars of **Amon**.

:::

:::spoiler volume=6

## Volume 6: Light of the City of Silver

### Sequence 4: Nightwatcher (Demigod)
- **Abilities**: Commanding deep nighttime concealment, summoning domain-wide tranquility, extracting characteristics from dead souls, and wielding the terrifying **Sword of Misfortune**.
- **Mythical Creature Form**: A shadowy darkness entity draped in crimson starlight and slumbering feathers.

Leonard achieves demigod rank within the Church of Evernight, using his authority to protect civilians during the World War and assist the Tarot Club's covert demigod operations.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Fool's Slumber

Following the ascension of The Fool and the departure of Pallez Zoroast to recover his godhood, Leonard remains a high-ranking demigod of the Church of Evernight and an active Major Arcana pillar of the Tarot Club, guarding Backlund against the encroaching cosmos.

:::
