## Overview

**Benson Moretti** is the eldest sibling of the Moretti family, the elder brother of **Klein Moretti (Zhou Mingrui)** and **Melissa Moretti**. Working as a diligent clerical employee at the **Tingen Import and Export Company** and later studying for the Loen Kingdom Civil Service Examinations, Benson is the steady, responsible patriarch of the household who sacrificed his own educational opportunities to financially support Klein through university and Melissa through technical school.

Distinguished by his gentle self-deprecating humor about his receding hairline, Benson provides the warm, mundane human grounding that anchors Klein's humanity throughout his supernatural journey.

## Family Life & Sibling Bond

- **Financial Sacrifice**: Managing the household's tight budget of pennies, shillings, and pounds, ensuring his siblings had meat, bread, and decent clothing.
- **Relocation to Backlund**: Following the tragedy in Tingen and Klein's staged death, Benson and Melissa relocate to Backlund on the generous pension provided by the Church of Evernight.
- **Civil Service Triumph**: Benson passes the kingdom's competitive examinations, securing a respectable, stable position in the Ministry of Transportation.
