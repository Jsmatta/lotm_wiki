## Overview

**Xio Derecha (Miss Judgment)** is a skilled bounty hunter, high-ranking military intelligence officer in **MI9**, and a core member of the **Tarot Club**. Walking the **Justiciar Pathway (Arbiter Pathway)**, Xio is a master of martial combat, judicial restriction, verdict execution, and legal enforcement.

Despite her petite physical stature (standing barely 1.5 meters tall) and her spiky golden hair, Xio commands immense aura, dignity, and ferocity. Motivated by an unyielding desire to uncover the truth behind the wrongful execution of her father, former royal guard captain **Mason Derecha**, Xio rises from an impoverished bounty hunter in East Borough to an influential Colonel in Loen's national intelligence agency.

- **Major Arcana Title**: **Judgment** (Seat representing strict law, order, impartial execution, and punishment).
- **Sequence Progression**:
  - **Sequence 9: Arbiter** (Enhanced physique, commanding aura, dispute resolution).
  - **Sequence 8: Sheriff** (Enhanced combat intuition, arresting authority, tracking).
  - **Sequence 7: Interrogator** (Psychic interrogation, truth compulsion, psychic piercing).
  - **Sequence 6: Judge** (Imposing prohibitions, decrees, verdicts, and spatial constraints).
  - **Sequence 5: Disciplinary Paladin** (Punishing rule-breakers with amplified retaliatory force).
  - **Sequence 4: Imperative Mage / Chaos Hunter** (Demigod authority, creating absolute regional laws).
  - **Sequence 3: Silver Knight / Balancer** (Manipulating justice balances and legal contradictions).
  - **Sequence 2: Hand of Order** (Angel of Justiciar, enforcing cosmic laws and order).

## Key Relationships

- **Fors Wall (The Magician)**: Her closest friend and roommate, providing mutual protection and emotional support.
- **Mason Derecha**: Her late father, whose framed execution was part of King George III's Black Emperor mausoleum coverup.
