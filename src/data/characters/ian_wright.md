## Overview

**Ian Wright** began his journey as a sharp-witted, resilient, red-haired street youth and errand runner in the impoverished alleys of Backlund's **East Borough**. Possessing natural investigative instincts, streetwise survival skills, and unyielding courage, Ian was framed for a diplomatic murder before being rescued and cleared by **Detective Sherlock Moriarty (Klein Moretti)**.

Mentored by Klein, Maric, and Xio Derecha, Ian grew into a formidable private investigator and intelligence operative.

:::reveal at=2

## Volume 2: The Fugitive of East Borough

Sherlock Moriarty takes Ian under his wing, shielding him from corrupt police hitmen and solving the mystery of the Intis diplomatic conspiracy that nearly cost Ian his life.

:::

:::spoiler volume=5

## Volume 5: Red Priest & The Backlund Underground

Ian operates as a senior intelligence liaison for Detective Maric and Xio, tracking royal supply convoys and gathering vital clues regarding King George III's secret construction sites.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: Master Investigator

Walking the **Hunter Pathway (Sequence 8: Provoker / Sequence 7: Pyromaniac)**, Ian coordinates a nationwide network of grassroots informants, providing actionable intelligence to the Church of Evernight.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Chief Investigator

In Volume 8, Ian Wright serves as Chief of Civil Investigations in Backlund, working closely with Archangel Xio Derecha and the Church of the Fool to safeguard ordinary citizens from supernatural crime.

:::
