## Overview

**Colin Iliad (The Demon Hunter)** was the revered Chief of the **Six-Member Council of the City of Silver**. Standing as the legendary protector of the last surviving human civilization inside the eternal darkness of the **Forsaken Land of the Gods**, Colin embodied the pinnacle of martial discipline, tactical wisdom, and selfless sacrifice.

Walking the **Twilight Giant Pathway (Warrior Pathway)** as a **Sequence 4: Demon Hunter** and later **Sequence 3: Silver Knight**, Colin spent centuries hunting mutated abominations in the cursed wasteland, dedicating his life to leading his people toward the light.

## Leadership & The Search for Light

For over 2,500 years, the City of Silver lived in constant peril, facing the monstrous curse that turns deceased citizens into evil spirits. Colin Iliad:
- Identified **Derrick Berg's** anomalous rapid growth and covertly nurtured his connection to **The Fool**.
- Formulated the grand expeditionary strategy to breach the **Giant King's Court**.

## Volume 6: Light of the City of Silver & The Chief's Sacrifice

Advancing to **Sequence 3: Silver Knight**, Colin Iliad joins Klein Moretti, Elder Lovia, and Derrick Berg in assaulting the **Giant King's Court**. 

In the climactic battle against the mutated ancient Silver Knight Chief, Colin sustains fatal wounds to deliver the decisive strike. Handing his swords to Derrick, Colin looks toward the opening doors of dawn, speaking his immortal final words:
> *"Light... has come."*
