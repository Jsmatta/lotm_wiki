## Overview

**Colin Iliad (The Demon Hunter)** is the revered Chief of the **Six-Member Council of the City of Silver** (Berg). Standing as the legendary protector of the last surviving enclave of humanity inside the eternal darkness of the **Forsaken Land of the Gods**, Colin embodies the pinnacle of martial discipline, tactical wisdom, and selfless sacrifice.

Walking the **Warrior Pathway (Twilight Giant Pathway)**, Colin spent centuries hunting mutated Second and Third Epoch abominations in the cursed wasteland, advancing to **Sequence 4: Demon Hunter** and later **Sequence 3: Silver Knight**.

## Leadership & The Search for Light

For over 2,500 years, the City of Silver lived in constant peril, subsisting on black-patterned grass and facing the monstrous curse that turns every deceased citizen into an evil spirit. Colin Iliad dedicated his entire existence to finding an exit to the outside world:
- He identified **Derrick Berg (The Sun)** as the key link to an unknown benevolent deity (**The Fool**).
- Rather than executing Derrick for heresy, Colin discreetly protected Derrick from fanatical elders, using Mr. Fool's revelations to guide city policy.

Through Derrick Berg's Tarot Club reports, Colin Iliad is introduced as the shrewd, scar-faced Chief who tests Derrick's mental stability while quietly studying the mysterious purification spells gifted by Mr. Fool.

## Volume 4: Exploration of the Cursed Temple

Colin leads the exploration team into the Fallen Creator's ruined temple, utilizing holy silver swords, dawn armor, and demon hunting potions to purge ancient shadow beasts.

:::

:::spoiler volume=6

## Volume 6: Light of the City of Silver & The Supreme Sacrifice

Colin receives the blessing of The Fool and advances to **Sequence 3: Silver Knight**. He organizes the final expedition into the **Giant King's Court** alongside Derrick, Elder Lovia Tiffany, and Gehrman Sparrow (Klein Moretti).

During the climactic battle against the evil spirit of Dark Angel **Sasrir**, Colin heroically impales himself onto Sasrir's black sword to lock the entity in place, enabling Derrick to deliver the final purifying strike with the Sun God's Unshadowed Spear.

With his dying breath, Colin promotes Derrick to the Council of Elders, commands him to open the bronze gates of the Giant King's Court, and smiles as he gazes upon the radiant golden sunlight of the outside world for the first time in 2,500 years.

:::
