## Overview

Derrick Berg is an earnest, courageous young warrior from the **City of Silver**, a surviving fortress of human civilization trapped inside the perilous **Forsaken Land of the Gods**. Standing over two meters tall with pure golden hair and an athletic physique, he is chosen to be a sacrificial exploration warrior for his city. Despite the eternal darkness and horrors of the cursed land, Derrick possesses an unwavering heart, deep loyalty to his people, and a burning desire to bring true sunlight to the City of Silver.

## Tarot Club: The Sun

Derrick is pulled into Sefirah Castle by Mr. Fool in Volume 2, receiving the Major Arcana title **The Sun**. Because the City of Silver was cut off from the Northern and Southern Continents during the Cataclysm of the Second/Third Epochs, Derrick brings invaluable ancient knowledge: the myths of the Ancient Sun God, the Eight Kings of Angels, and the original names of the Beyonder Pathways.

In return, the Tarot Club provides Derrick with uncontaminated potion formulas, mental counseling from Miss Justice, and divine warnings from Mr. Fool that allow him to navigate the internal corruptions and lurking horrors threatening his homeland.

## Beyonder Pathway: The Sun

Derrick walks the **Sun Pathway** (also known as the Eternal Blazing Sun Pathway), the ultimate banisher of darkness, decay, undeath, and depravity.

### Sequence 4: Unshadowed (Demigod)
- **Abilities**: **Unshadowed Spear** (a concentrated lance of solar destruction capable of purifying demigod-level corruption and forcing Beyonder characteristics out of living bodies), **Flawless Purification**, and total immunity to darkness curses.
