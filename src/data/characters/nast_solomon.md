## Overview

**Nast Solomon (The King of the Five Seas)** is the undisputed sovereign ruler of the pirate world, commanding the Four Pirate Kings from his terrifying colossal flagship, the **Black Emperor**. Tracing his direct ancestry back to the Fourth Epoch's **Solomon Empire**, Nast walks the **Black Emperor Pathway (Lawyer Pathway)** as a **Sequence 3: Frenzy Mage / Sequence 2: Duke of Entropy Angel**.

Towering, majestic, wearing an ornate black crown and cape, Nast enforces his absolute legal decrees across the high seas, distorting and exploiting rules to establish order amidst pirate anarchy.

## The Black Emperor Flagship & Royal Legacy

- **The Black Emperor**: A legendary ghost battleship constructed from dark iron and ancient shadow wood, possessing immense distortion barriers capable of deflecting naval broadsides and high-sequence spells.
- **Solomon Bloodline**: Safeguarding the ancestral legal charters, potion formulas, and historical secrets of Emperor Solomon and Emperor Roselle.
