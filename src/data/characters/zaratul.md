## Overview

**Zaratul (The Leader of the Secret Order)** is an ancient **Sequence 1: Attendant of Mysteries Angel** of the **Fool Pathway (Seer Pathway)** and the enigmatic patriarch of the **Zaratul Family**. Having served as the mysticism mentor to Emperor **Roselle Gustav** in the Fourth and Fifth Epochs, Zaratul is Klein Moretti's primary direct pathway rival for the divine authority of The Fool.

A master of historical fog summoning, miracle creation, marionette puppetry, and realm grafting, Zaratul lost control in the late Fifth Epoch before recovering his sanity to pursue Sefirah Castle.

## Pathway Supremacy & Historical Fog

- **Sequence 1: Attendant of Mysteries**: Controlling spirit-body threads across entire cities, grafting spatial and temporal concepts, summoning historical projections of ancient angels from the fog of history.
- **The Secret Order**: A clandestine occult organization hunting down the Antigonus Notebook and Seer characteristics across the Northern Continent.
