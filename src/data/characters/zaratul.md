## Overview

**Zaratul** was the ancient, enigmatic leader of the **Secret Order**, an ancient Fourth Epoch demigod organization guarding the secrets of the **Seer Pathway**. Walking the Seer Pathway as a **Sequence 1: Attendant of Mysteries** (and previously Sequence 2 Miracle Invoker), Zaratul was Emperor Roselle Gustav's close friend, mystical advisor, and eventual rival during the late Fourth and early Fifth Epochs.

Mastering historical void summoning, marionette manipulation, and bizarre fortune-telling, Zaratul served as Klein Moretti's primary pathway rival for the divine throne of The Fool.

:::reveal at=1

## Volume 1: Clown & The Secret Order's Shadow

Zaratul's agents infiltrate Tingen and Backlund seeking the Antigonus Notebook, clashing with the Nighthawks and monitoring Klein's early progress.

:::

:::spoiler volume=5

## Volume 5: Red Priest & The Historical Clashes

In Backlund, Zaratul appears as a wandering historical projection, engaging in complex psychological games and supernatural skirmishes against Klein Moretti (Dwayne Dantès).

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: The Battle in Utopia & Zaratul's Demise

Zaratul conspires with Adam and the Church of Storms to dismantle Klein's phantom city **Utopia**. 

In an earth-shattering Sequence 1 confrontation amidst historical void projections, Klein outwits Zaratul, utilizing paper figurine substitutions, historical void traps, and Sefirah Castle's suppression to slay Zaratul. Klein consumes Zaratul's **Attendant of Mysteries** characteristic, successfully completing his advancement to Sequence 1.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Dissolved Secret Order

In Volume 8, following Zaratul's fall and Klein's ascension as **The Fool**, the Secret Order is absorbed under the Church of the Fool, ending centuries of covert pathway rivalry.
