## Overview

**Maric** is a melancholic, devoted member of the **Temperance Faction** and the loyal combat partner of **Miss Sharron**. Walking the **Chained Pathway (Sequence 6: Zombie)**, Maric is characterized by his pale, corpse-like complexion, sunken eyes, heavy black woolen coat, and constant struggle to restrain his inner bloodlust by carrying bound, compliant zombie companions and playing card games to distract his mind.

A steadfast friend to **Klein Moretti**, Maric provides reliable physical muscle, underworld connections, and underground reconnaissance in Backlund.

:::reveal at=2

## Volume 2: The Zombie Master of Bravehearts Bar

Detective Sherlock Moriarty meets Maric at Bravehearts Bar in East Borough, coordinating underground skirmishes against Rose School of Thought pursuers.

Maric advances to **Sequence 5: Wraith**, mastering mirror traversal and spectral possession to support Sharron during the wartime defense of Backlund's civilian quarters.

Maric travels to the Southern Continent alongside Sharron and Klein, commanding squads of purified spirit guardians to liberate enslaved Temperance communities.
