## Overview

**Maric (The Zombie)** is a dedicated member of the **Temperance Faction** of the **Chained Pathway (Sequence 6: Zombie / Sequence 5: Wraith)** and the closest operational partner of **Sharron** in **Backlund**. Operating covertly out of the underground boxing rings and backrooms of the **Bravehearts Bar** in the Iron Gate Street district, Maric serves as the primary liaison between Sharron and the city's private investigators.

Distinguished by his perpetually pale, sickly complexion, dark eye bags, tense demeanor, and habit of playing solitaire card games with living corpses sitting silently around his table, Maric struggles constantly against the bloodthirsty urges of the Zombie sequence through mental discipline.

## Pathway & Zombie Abilities

- **Sequence 9: Prisoner & Sequence 8: Lunatic**: Frenzied physical attacks and emotional outbursts.
- **Sequence 6: Zombie**: Superhuman physical strength, invulnerable iron-like skin, immunity to physical pain, controlling legions of living corpses, and emitting frost aura.
