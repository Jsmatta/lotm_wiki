## Overview

**Fors Wall (The Magician)** is a prominent author, former physician, and core member of the **Tarot Club**. Walking the **Door Pathway (Apprentice Pathway)**, Fors is an adopted disciple of the ancient **Abraham Family**, eventually ascending to become the supreme guardian and Sequence 2 Angel of the family.

Known for her humorous cynicism, procrastination when writing fantasy novels, fondness for salted butter rolls, and deep loyalty to her best friend **Xio Derecha (Miss Judgment)**, Fors plays an indispensable logistical and combat role in the Tarot Club through inter-dimensional teleportation and mystical recording.

## Tarot Identity & Sequence Progression

- **Major Arcana Title**: **The Magician** (Seat representing resourcefulness, spatial manipulation, and illusion).
- **Sequence Progression**:
  - **Sequence 9: Apprentice** (Passing through solid walls, opening locks).
  - **Sequence 8: Trickmaster** (Spelling illusions, electric shocks, freezing spells).
  - **Sequence 7: Astrologer** (Divination, astromancy, danger perception).
  - **Sequence 6: Scribe** (Recording Beyonder powers and divine spells).
  - **Sequence 5: Traveler** (Blink teleportation, astral navigation, spirit world traveling).
  - **Sequence 4: Secrets Sorcerer** (Spatial exile, sealing dimensions, creating pocket spaces).
  - **Sequence 3: Wanderer** (Wandering historical dimensions and the cosmos).
  - **Sequence 2: Key of Stars** (Angel of the Door Pathway, commanding celestial portals).

## Key Relics & Abilities

- **Leymano's Travels**: A legendary enchanted spellbook gifted by Dorian Gray Abraham, capable of recording and unleashing hundreds of high-tier demigod spells and angel attacks provided by Mr. Fool and Tarot Club members.

## Volume 2: The Faceless & Tarot Club Induction

Plagued by terrifying full-moon ravings, Fors attends a private gathering where Audrey Hall invites her to pray to Mr. Fool. Brought above the gray fog, Fors discovers that Sefirah Castle completely cleanses the cosmic madness, leading to her initiation as **The Magician**.

:::

:::spoiler volume=4

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past & Leymano's Travels

Fors acts as the liaison between the Tarot Club and Dorian Gray Abraham, receiving *Leymano's Travels*. She records Gehrman Sparrow's deadly attacks, aiding in the execution of Backlund cultists.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & Mr. Door's Legacy

Following Bethel Abraham's self-sacrifice, Fors inherits the sacred mantle and characteristics of the Door Pathway. She advances to Demigod Sequences, lifting the thousand-year curse on the Abraham Family.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Key of Stars & Major Tarot Commission

Fors advances to **Sequence 2: Key of Stars**. Before entering slumber, Mr. Fool appoints Fors as a senior angel guide to wander the world, protect the Abraham Family, and oversee the gathering of mystical cards for the new era.

:::
