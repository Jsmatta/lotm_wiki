## Overview

**Melissa Moretti** is the youngest sibling of the Moretti family, the beloved younger sister of **Klein Moretti (Zhou Mingrui)** and **Benson Moretti**. A brilliant student at the **Tingen Technical School** with an innate genius for steam machinery, clockwork gears, chemistry, and alchemical engineering, Melissa represents the sharp, pragmatic, and unyielding spirit of the industrial working class.

Frugal, fiercely independent, and constantly scolding her brothers for unnecessary expenditures while secretly cherishing every moment with them, Melissa is Klein's most profound emotional anchor to ordinary human life.

## The Steam Engineer & Family Anchor

- **Machinery Talent**: Fixing clocks, repairing gas lamps, building steam-driven models, and earning scholarships at the technical institute.
- **Relocation to Backlund**: Moves to Backlund with Benson following the Tingen tragedy, advancing her studies at the prestigious Backlund Institute of Technology.
- **The Mysterious Revelation**: Over the years, Melissa gradually pieces together clues about Klein's extraordinary survival, recognizing his presence behind the miracles that protect her family.

## Volume 8: The Prayer to The Fool

Ten years after the world war and Klein's cosmic slumber, Melissa brings her young niece **Alice Moretti** to their home alter. Drawing the sacred symbol of the Pupil-less Eye and Contorted Fog, Melissa guides Alice in reciting the honorific name of **The Fool**. 

When a translucent, gentle spiritual tentacle playfully taps Alice's forehead and strokes Melissa's cheek, Melissa smiles with tears in her eyes, knowing that her brother Klein is still watching over them from above the gray fog.
