## Overview

**Pallez Zoroast (Old Man)** is the ancient patriarch and ancestor of the **Zoroast Family**, one of the prominent angel bloodlines of the Fourth Epoch (Solomon Empire and Tudor Dynasty). Walking the **Error Pathway (Marauder Pathway)** as a supreme **Sequence 1: Worm of Time Angel**, Pallez survived the catastrophic destruction of his family by residing as a parasitic spirit within the mind of Nighthawk officer **Leonard Mitchell (The Star)**.

Cautious, calculating, profoundly knowledgeable in Fourth Epoch history, and possessing a dry, weary humor, Pallez serves as Leonard's invaluable mystical mentor while maintaining constant vigilance against his mortal nemesis: **Amon (The Angel of Time)**.

## The Parasite Symbiosis

Following a devastating battle in the early Fifth Epoch where Amon shattered Pallez's physical body:
- Pallez divided his remaining *Worms of Time* into a severely injured, dormant state.
- He parasitized young Leonard Mitchell in Tingen, covertly shielding Leonard from mental attacks and loss of control in exchange for a warm biological vessel to recuperate.
- Throughout the series, Pallez teaches Leonard advanced Beyonder secrets, potion acting tricks, and the terrifying rules of dealing with high-sequence demigods.
