## Overview

**Will Auceptin (The Snake of Fate / Snake of Mercury)** is an ancient King of Angels of the **Monster Pathway (Wheel of Fortune Pathway)**. Walking the path of **Sequence 1: Snake of Mercury**, Will commands absolute mastery over **Probability**, **Luck**, **Cycles of Reincarnation**, **Misfortune Redirection**, and **Destiny Perception**.

To avoid the lethal hunting of his rival **Ouroboros (The Tail-Devourer)** and prepare for accommodation of the **Wheel of Fortune Uniqueness**, Will repeatedly resets his life cycle, reincarnating as a vulnerable infant. Possessing the personality of a precocious, sarcastic, ice-cream-addicted child, Will forms a crucial strategic partnership with **Klein Moretti (The Fool)**.

## The Paper Crane & Reincarnation

- **The Paper Crane**: A blessed origami paper crane gifted to Klein Moretti; by folding or whispering into it, Klein can establish direct one-on-one divination and communication with Will above the gray fog.
- **The Ceres Family Reincarnation**: In Volume 2, Will chooses the womb of **Dr. Aaron Ceres's** wife for his latest reincarnation, being born into Backlund as their beloved infant son.
- **Ice Cream Bribes**: Despite his terrifying status as an angel who can rewrite probability, Will's favorite mortal treat is luxury ice cream, which Klein frequently brings to bribe him for destiny divinations and high-level mystical consultations.
