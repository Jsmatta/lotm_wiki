## Overview

**Lanevus** was a charismatic, psychopathic swindler, con artist, and cultist walking the **Marauder Pathway (Sequence 8: Swindler)**. As one of the core instigators of the **Tingen Tragedy**, Lanevus orchestrated the fraudulent Tingen Steel and Iron Company investment scam, seduced the innocent young woman **Megose**, and used her body as the physical vessel to cultivate the descending fetus of the **True Creator**.

Greedy, cunning, devoid of moral conscience, and possessing an extraordinary ability to manipulate human greed and trust, Lanevus fled Tingen with tens of thousands of pounds before resurfacing in the slums of Backlund.

## The Tingen Con & Megose Tragedy

- **The Steel Factory Scam**: Embezzling tens of thousands of pounds from Tingen's middle class, driving families to suicide.
- **The Vessel of Evil**: Feeding Megose corrupted occult items and blood charms to implant the fallen divine fetus, directly causing the deaths of Captain Dunn Smith, Kenley, and Klein Moretti.

## Volume 2: The Faceless & The Vengeance in the Sewers

Sherlock Moriarty (Klein Moretti) tracks Lanevus to the sewers of East Borough, Backlund, where Lanevus is hiding with the lingering biological corruption of the True Creator. 

Klein confronts him in the dark, disabling Lanevus's limbs with tarot cards and spirit bullets before shooting him dead through the head, completing the first step of Klein's vengeance for Tingen.
