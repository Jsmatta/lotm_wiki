## Overview

**Lanevus** was a cunning, psychopathic swindler, serial fraudster, and rogue Beyonder walking the **Marauder Pathway (Error Pathway)** as a **Sequence 8: Swindler**. Possessing charismatic eloquence and total moral bankruptcy, Lanevus engineered massive steel factory investment scams in Tingen, seducing the young woman Megose and offering her unborn child as a sacrificial vessel for the descent of the **True Creator**.

His schemes served as the primary biological catalyst for the catastrophic climax of Volume 1.

:::reveal at=1

## Volume 1: Clown & The Tingen Fraud

Lanevus embezzles tens of thousands of pounds and flees Tingen, leaving behind the pregnant Megose whose demonic fetus nearly destroys the city and claims the life of Captain Dunn Smith.

:::

:::spoiler volume=2

## Volume 2: The Faceless & Justice in Backlund

Lanevus flees to Backlund, posing as a wealthy investor while secretly working with Aurora Order cells. Detective Sherlock Moriarty (Klein) discovers Lanevus's presence and coordinates with Miss Sharron to corner him in East Borough. 

Klein executes Lanevus with a silver bullet through the forehead, avenging Dunn Smith and carving a tarot card upon his corpse.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: Historical Closure

In Volume 8, the justice delivered to Lanevus stands as a crucial early milestone in Klein Moretti's growth, proving that even as a lone rogue Beyonder, Klein never abandoned his commitment to moral justice.
