## Overview

**Ludwell (Admiral Hell)** was one of the Seven Pirate Admirals ruling the **Five Seas**, captain of the legendary ghost galleon **The Black Tulip**, and a powerful **Sequence 5: Gatekeeper** of the **Death Pathway (Corpse Collector Pathway)**. Bound to the ancient imperial bloodlines of the Southern Continent's **Balam Empire** and the **Numinous Episcopate**, Ludwell wielded absolute authority over the underworld, bone spirits, and ghost fleets.

Distinguished by his pale skull-like mask, tattered black admiral uniform, and enchanted **Ring of Death**, Ludwell terrorized maritime trade routes before being defeated by **Gehrman Sparrow (Klein Moretti)**.

## Pathway & Underworld Authority

- **Sequence 5: Gatekeeper**: Opening spectral gates directly into the Underworld, summoning hordes of vengeful spirits, skeletal warhorses, and controlling the deceased souls of sunken sailors.
- **Gehrman's Marionette**: Converted into one of Klein's elite combat marionettes, providing Klein with instant access to the spirit underworld and death-pathway spells.

## Volume 3: Legend of the Pirate Hunter & The Boarding of the Black Tulip

Gehrman Sparrow and Mr. Azik track Ludwell to obtain historical records concerning the Fourth Epoch's Death (Salinger). In a ferocious naval duel, Gehrman severs Ludwell's spirit threads, turning the pirate admiral into his personal Underworld Gatekeeper marionette.
