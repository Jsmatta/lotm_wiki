## Overview

Audrey Hall is the aristocratic daughter of Count Hall, one of the wealthiest and most influential nobles in the Loen Kingdom. Revered in Backlund's high society as the "Dazzling Gem of Backlund," she combines aristocratic elegance with profound empathy, fierce intellect, and an insatiable curiosity for the occult world.

## Tarot Club: Miss Justice

Audrey is one of the founding members of the Tarot Club. During Klein Moretti's initial luck enhancement ritual above the gray fog, she was accidentally summoned alongside Alger Wilson. Intrigued by the mysterious existence who called himself **Mr. Fool**, she chose the Major Arcana title **Justice** and suggested the official name "The Tarot Club."

Throughout the series, Miss Justice serves as the club's financial pillar and emotional anchor. Leveraging her family's vast wealth, banking connections, and social status, she purchases critical potion formulas, Beyonder ingredients, and intelligence for members like Derrick Berg and Alger Wilson in exchange for mysticism knowledge and Roselle's diary pages.

## Beyonder Pathway: The Spectator

Audrey embarks on the **Spectator Pathway** (also known as the Visionary Pathway), dedicated to observation, psychological insight, subconscious manipulation, and the collective unconscious.

### Sequence 9: Spectator
- **Abilities**: Exceptional visual perception, reading subtle micro-expressions, body language, vocal inflections, and emotional auras. Spectators can infer a person's inner thoughts and hidden motives while remaining detached.
- **Acting Method**: "The audience always remains calm and detached; only an observer; never lets oneself become part of the play."
- **Companion**: Her golden retriever, **Susie**, accidentally drinks a portion of the Spectator potion, awakening high intelligence and becoming an indispensable scouting partner.

## Volume 2: The Faceless & Backlund Undercurrents

### Sequence 8: Telepathist
- **Abilities**: Direct reading of surface thoughts, sensing emotional shifts, perceiving spiritual auras, and projecting calming telepathic cues.

Audrey infiltrates the **Psychology Alchemists**, an underground academic Beyonder organization originating from the Hermes lineage. She uncovers political conspiracies involving Prince Edessak, Capim's human trafficking ring, and Ince Zangwill.

Witnessing the devastation of the Great Smog of Backlund and the destitution of East Borough deeply alters Audrey's worldview. She realizes the cruelty underlying high society's luxury and resolves to use her powers to heal the suffering.

:::

:::spoiler volume=3

## Volume 3: Legend of the Pirate Hunter

### Sequence 7: Psychiatrist
- **Abilities**: **Frenzy** (disrupting emotional equilibrium), **Placate** (soothing mental distress and delaying loss of control), **Psychological Cue** (guiding thoughts subtly), and dream exploration.

Audrey aids the Tarot Club by diagnosing members' psychological conditions, soothing Derrick Berg's traumas from the Forsaken Land, and analyzing intelligence regarding the royal family and military factions.

:::

:::spoiler volume=4

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past

### Sequence 6: Hypnotist & Sequence 5: Dreamwalker
- **Hypnotist**: Implanting deep subconscious commands, erasing or altering memory fragments, and inducing total psychological compliance.
- **Dreamwalker**: Entering the dreams of others, navigating the subconscious sea, uncovering hidden memories, and creating shared dreamscapes.

Audrey becomes Klein Moretti's (as Dwayne Dantès) personal psychological counselor. She discovers that the mysterious adventurer Gehrman Sparrow suffers from extreme mental stress and trauma, providing regular Placate therapy while maintaining absolute discretion.

:::

:::spoiler volume=5

## Volume 5: Red Priest & Royal Conspiracy

Audrey advances in the Psychology Alchemists, navigating high-level internal struggles against Hvin Rambis, a dragon elder of the Hermes circle. She participates in disrupting George III's apotheosis ritual and unmasks the crown's treason.

:::

:::spoiler volume=6

## Volume 6: Light of the City of Silver

### Sequence 4: Manipulator (Demigod)
- **Abilities**: **Virtual Persona** (creating independent psychic clones), **Mental Plague** (infectious emotional states across vast battlefields), **Consciousness Roaming**, and total mastery of the collective subconscious mind.
- **Mythical Creature Form**: A majestic mind dragon constructed of translucent light scales and spiritual thought-currents.

Audrey coordinates with the Tarot Club in the operation against Saint of Secrets Botis, orchestrating tactical misdirections and psychological warfare.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos

### Sequence 3: Dream Weaver
- **Abilities**: Weaving collective subconscious dreams into physical reality, isolating enemies inside psychological mazes, and stabilizing high-sequence mental balance.

Audrey leaves her noble family under a fabricated cover identity to protect them from divine retaliation, choosing the path of a wandering occult psychiatrist.

:::

## Volume 8: The Fool's Slumber

Audrey performs the ultimate psychological stabilization on Klein Moretti before his fateful battle against Amon and the awakening Celestial Worthy. When The Fool enters deep slumber, Miss Justice receives the long-term commission to oversee the mental stability of the Tarot Club and protect Earth from outer deity corruption.
