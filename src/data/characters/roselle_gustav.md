## Overview

**Emperor Roselle Gustav (Huang Tao)** is a legendary transmigrator from pre-epoch Earth who awakened during the late Fourth / early Fifth Epoch. Revered as the **Father of the Industrial Revolution**, the **Caesar of the Intis Republic**, and the **Consul of Intis**, Roselle revolutionized the world by inventing the steam engine, steam locomotives, modern firearms, textiles, constitutional legal codes, and card games.

In the mystical world, Roselle created the **22 Cards of Blasphemy** (embodying the complete potion formulas and acting secrets of all 22 pathways) and authored hundreds of diary pages written in modern simplified Chinese, which serve as Klein Moretti's greatest source of ancient occult history.

## Pathway & Ascension

- **Initial Pathway**: **Savant Pathway (Paragon / Mystery Pryer)**, advancing to Sequence 1: Knowledge Emperor under the Church of Steam.
- **Pathway Switch**: Switching to **Sequence 0: Black Emperor (Lawyer Pathway)** to escape divine containment.
- **The Crimson Moon Tragedy**: During an expedition to the primitive island and cosmic astral space, Roselle gazed upon the Crimson Moon and was contaminated by the Outer Deity **Mother Goddess of Depravity**, corrupting his apotheosis ritual and forcing his assassination in his secret mausoleum.
