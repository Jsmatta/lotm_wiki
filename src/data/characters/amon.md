## Overview

Amon, known throughout the cosmos as the **Angel of Time**, the **God of Mischief**, and the **Blasphemer**, is a mythical King of Angels who was born as a direct biological son of the Ancient Sun God during the Third Epoch. Born as a mythical creature of the **Error Pathway** (Marauder Pathway), Amon possesses innate Sequence 1 Beyonder characteristics and the Uniqueness of Error.

Characterized by his signature crystal monocle worn over his right eye, a pointed black wizard hat, and a playful yet terrifyingly cold demeanor, Amon treats all of existence as an elaborate game where he exploits loopholes in physical, mental, and divine laws.

## Beyonder Pathway: Error & Marauder

Amon is the ultimate embodiment of the Error Pathway, wielding authority over:
- **Parasitization**: Planting conceptual avatar clones into humans, demigods, animals, bacteria, thoughts, and even metaphysical concepts.
- **Theft**: Stealing physical objects, thoughts, memories, lifespan, Beyonder abilities, identities, distances, time, and even the fate of gods.
- **Deceit & Loopholes**: Finding flaws in the rules of reality, bypassing rituals, and altering cause-and-effect sequences.

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past

Amon's shadow looms over the Beyonder world. The ancient angel **Pallez Zoroast** of the Zoroast family (who is parasitizing Leonard Mitchell) warns Klein Moretti that Amon has noticed Backlund and is actively hunting remaining Marauder characteristics. Even speaking or thinking Amon's true name invites immediate avatar parasitization.

## Volume 5: Red Priest & Backlund Hunting Grounds

Amon avatars infiltrate Backlund through the Jacob family's descendants. Klein, Leonard, Pallez Zoroast, and Will Auceptin coordinate a desperate high-sequence purge to eliminate Amon's clones. However, in the volume's climax, an Amon avatar outmaneuvers Klein and captures him following the disruption of George III's ritual.

:::

:::spoiler volume=6

## Volume 6: Light of the City of Silver

Amon drags Klein across the desolate **Forsaken Land of the Gods**, attempting to force Klein to willingly surrender Sefirah Castle without triggering the ancient slumbering consciousness of the Celestial Worthy. 

During this psychological and intellectual duel, Amon escorts Klein past the ruins of **Chernobyl**, revealing the devastating truth that their world is Earth following an ancient pre-epoch apocalypse. Klein barely escapes Amon's grasp by choosing suicide with the divine intervention of the Evernight Goddess.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos

Amon cooperates with his brother Adam to orchestrate the demise of Bethel Abraham (Mr. Door). As Bethel descends from the cosmos tainted by the Mother Goddess of Depravity, Amon steals Bethel's ritual and characteristics, becoming a **Dual-Pathway True God** holding the Uniquenesses of both **Error** and **Door**.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Fool's Slumber

Amon infiltrates Sefirah Castle during Klein's Fool Apotheosis Ritual, initiating the final battle for the position of **Lord of the Mysteries**. Klein risks mutually assured destruction by allowing the Celestial Worthy's will to awaken inside himself. Fearing madness and losing his self-identity, Amon hesitates, allowing Klein to land the decisive blow, shattering Amon's main body and forcing his surviving avatars to flee into the cosmos.

:::
