## Overview

**Captain Dunn Smith (The Sleepless Guardian)** was the beloved captain of the **Tingen Nighthawks squad** and the managing director of the **Blackthorn Security Company** (36 Zouteland Street). Walking the **Sleepless Pathway (Darkness Pathway)** as a **Sequence 7: Nightmare**, Dunn is remembered as Klein Moretti's foundational mentor, spiritual father figure, and the quintessential embodiment of the Nighthawks' tragic code of honor.

Distinguished by his deep gray eyes, receding hairline, black trench coat, and habit of drinking strong coffee without sugar, Dunn bore the heavy mental burden of consuming the Beyonder characteristics of fallen teammates to preserve their memories and powers, leading to his chronic, endearing forgetfulness.

## Pathway & Nightmare Abilities

- **Sequence 9: Sleepless**: Minimal sleep requirement (2–3 hours), enhanced nocturnal vision, heightened spiritual sensitivity.
- **Sequence 8: Midnight Poet**: Chanting soothing poems that pacify unrest, inflict psychological sleep, or unleash concussive sonic strikes.
- **Sequence 7: Nightmare**: Entering and manipulating the dreams of living targets, dragging them into terrifying psychological illusions, and extracting buried truths.

## Volume 1: Clown & The Sacrifice of Tingen

When Ince Zangwill and Megose orchestrate the horrific assault on Tingen, Captain Dunn Smith makes the ultimate sacrifice: he consumes the characteristics of Kenley and his fallen comrades, using his nightmare authority to protect Tingen. Dying with a gentle, tranquil smile in Klein's arms, Dunn entrusts the safety of the world to his remaining teammates.

:::

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past & The Reunited Dance

In the Southern Continent, Klein Moretti and Leonard Mitchell avenge Dunn by slaying Ince Zangwill. In her final moments, Daly Simone consumes a spirit medium potion to dance one last romantic waltz with Dunn's gentle soul, granting the lovers eternal peace.

## Volume 8: The Anchor in the Divine Slumber

As Klein Moretti ascends to **Sequence 0: The Fool** and prepares to enter his epochal slumber against the Celestial Worthy, his thoughts return to Captain Dunn Smith, his bitter coffee, and the words that guided his entire divine journey:
> *"We are guardians, but also a bunch of miserable wretches that are constantly fighting against threats and madness."*

Dunn Smith remains the immortal moral compass anchoring the Lord of the Mysteries to his humanity.
