## Overview

Alger Wilson is a shrewd, battle-hardened sailor and a seasoned Beyonder of the Church of the Lord of Storms. Holding the rank of Captain of the ghost ship *Blue Avenger*, he possesses mixed human-elvish ancestry, characterized by dark blue coarse hair and weathered features. He is deeply ambitious, calculating, and exceptionally knowledgeable in maritime mysticism and secret faction politics.

## Tarot Club: The Hanged Man

Alger is one of the founding members of the Tarot Club. Summoned accidentally during Klein Moretti's initial ritual above the gray fog, he chose the Major Arcana title **The Hanged Man**. Seeing Mr. Fool manipulate divine spatial laws effortlessly, Alger became convinced that Mr. Fool was an ancient, recovering true deity.

As the Tarot Club's primary senior occult advisor in the early volumes, Alger provides vital intelligence on the Beyonder world, the orthodox churches, pirate factions, and ancient folklore in exchange for sequence formulas, potion ingredients, and the favor of Mr. Fool.

## Beyonder Pathway: The Sailor

Alger traverses the **Sailor Pathway** (also known as the Tyrant Pathway), reigning over wind, lightning, water currents, and maritime warfare.

### Sequence 9: Sailor
- **Abilities**: Extraordinary balance on any surface, enhanced muscle strength, night vision underwater, supreme swimming capabilities, and the ability to breathe underwater for extended periods.
