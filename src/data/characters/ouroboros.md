## Overview

**Ouroboros (The Angel of Fate / The Tail-Devourer)** is one of the ancient Eight Kings of Angels who served the **Ancient Sun God** in the Third Epoch. Walking the **Monster Pathway (Wheel of Fortune Pathway)** as a supreme **Sequence 1: Snake of Mercury**, Ouroboros commands absolute mastery over **Probability**, **Reincarnation**, **Fate Loops**, **Calamity Redirection**, and **Destiny Perception**.

Taking the visual form of a divine youth with pure silver hair, golden eyes, and an infinitely coiling silver snake eating its own tail, Ouroboros remained eternally loyal to the Ancient Sun God, becoming the chief divine protector and Angel of the **True Creator** and the **Aurora Order**.

## The War of Fate: Ouroboros vs. Will Auceptin

To accommodate the **Wheel of Fortune** Uniqueness and ascend to Sequence 0, a Snake of Mercury must consume all three Sequence 1 characteristics. Throughout the Fifth Epoch, Ouroboros has relentlessly hunted his rival **Will Auceptin (The Snake of Fate)** across the continent, orchestrating terrifying probability anomalies and cyclical time loops to corner Will's infant reincarnations.
