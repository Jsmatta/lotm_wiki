## Overview

**King George Augustus III (George III)** was the reigning monarch of the **Loen Kingdom**, the supreme secular ruler of Backlund, and the sovereign of the House of Augustus. Known publicly as a progressive, reform-minded king who oversaw the expansion of steam technology, the growth of the Royal Navy, and the restructuring of colonial trade, George III projected an aura of imperial majesty, national pride, and stern dedication to the Loen Empire.

Behind his public facade, the House of Augustus harbored high-sequence demigods inheriting the **Black Emperor Pathway (Lawyer Pathway)** and **Justiciar Pathway (Arbiter Pathway)**.

:::reveal at=2

## Volume 2: The Faceless & The Monarch in the Palace

Following the devastation of the **Great Smog of Backlund**, King George III delivers patriotic speeches to Parliament, rallying the nation to endure hardships and authorizing increased military expenditure, while intelligence investigations hint at royal involvement in Prince Edessak's mysterious death.

:::

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past & Dwayne Dantès's Infiltration

Klein Moretti (as Dwayne Dantès) enters Backlund high society, attending royal galas and interacting with the nobility. Klein discovers that vast sums of money and thousands of impoverished laborers from East Borough are mysteriously vanishing into state-sponsored secret construction projects.

:::

:::spoiler volume=5

## Volume 5: Red Priest & The Fall of the King

The full horrifying conspiracy is exposed: George III secretly advanced to **Sequence 1: Prince of Abolition** and formed clandestine pacts with the **Demoness Sect**, **Aurora Order**, and **Adam** to execute **George III's Apotheosis Ritual**. 

The Great Smog and the outbreak of the World War were engineered to cover up the secret construction of **nine pyramid-shaped mausoleums** across the empire. 

During the grand patriotic military parade in Backlund, George III drinks the Sequence 0 potion. However, Klein Moretti, Miss Judgment (Xio), Miss Justice (Audrey), Trissy, and Queen Mystic Bernadette coordinate a daring sabotage of the secret mausoleums. With the ritual anchors shattered, George III suffers catastrophic divine backlash, and Klein Moretti delivers the fatal strike, executing the monarch and avenging the victims of the Great Smog.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Aftermath of the Tyrant

In Volume 8, the fall of George III triggers deep constitutional reforms across the Loen Kingdom. The House of Augustus is forced to surrender despotic authority, while Backlund commemorates the secret heroes who saved the empire from a corrupted Black Emperor.
