## Overview

**King George Augustus III (George III)** was the reigning monarch of the **Loen Kingdom**, the supreme secular ruler of Backlund, and the primary overarching antagonist of Volume 5 (Red Priest). Walking the **Lawyer Pathway (Sequence 1: Prince of Abolition)** of the Black Emperor Pathway, George III secretly orchestrated catastrophic mass sacrifices, human trafficking networks, and imperialist wars to complete his **Sequence 0: Black Emperor Apotheosis Ritual**.

Cold, calculating, utterly ruthless, and viewing millions of his mortal subjects as expendable fuel for his divine ascension, George III formed clandestine pacts with the **Demoness Sect**, the **Aurora Order**, and the **Twilight Hermit Order (Adam)**.

## The 9 Secret Mausoleums & The Great Smog

To ascend to Sequence 0: Black Emperor:
- The candidate must construct **nine secret pyramid-shaped mausoleums** across the kingdom with deep historical architectural resonance.
- The candidate must deeply bind their reign to the nation's political and legal structure, distorting national laws while inciting national fervor.
- George III permitted and utilized the **Great Smog of Backlund** and the outbreak of the **World War** to cover up the disappearance of tens of thousands of laborers used to build his mausoleums.

## Volume 5: Red Priest & The Fall of the King

During the national victory parade celebrating the war effort, George III attempts to activate his nine mausoleums to ascend to Sequence 0: Black Emperor.

Klein Moretti, Miss Judgment (Xio), Miss Justice (Audrey), Trissy, and Queen Mystic Bernadette coordinate a multi-pronged assault on the mausoleums. With the mausoleum anchors shattered, George III's ritual collapses in divine backlash, and Klein delivers the fatal blow, ending the tyrant's reign.
