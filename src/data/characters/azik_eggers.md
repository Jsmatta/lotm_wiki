## Overview

**Mr. Azik Eggers (The Death Consul)** is an ancient angel and biological son of the Fourth Epoch's **Salinger (The Death / Balam Emperor)**. Walking the **Death Pathway (Corpse Collector Pathway)** as a supreme **Sequence 2: Death Consul Angel**, Azik commands absolute dominion over the **Underworld**, **Spirits of the Dead**, **Underworld Fire**, and **Reanimation**.

When first encountered in Tingen, Azik lived as a gentle, melancholic history instructor at **Khoy University**, plagued by severe amnesia and recurring cycle-amnesia every few decades. Serving as Klein Moretti's most loyal mentor, guardian, and parental figure, Azik's steadfast protection rescues Klein from countless fatal crises.

## The Cycle of Memory & The Soul Cleave

During the Fourth Epoch's Cataclysm and the death of Salinger:
- Azik's soul was cleaved into two halves, with one half bound to the River of Eternal Darkness / Underworld and the other half reincarnating across mortal history.
- Every several decades, Azik falls into a deep slumber, losing all memories and awakening with a completely new mortal identity (a teacher, a painter, a physician).
- **Azik's Copper Whistle**: An enchanted relic gifted to Klein Moretti that summons a colossal bone messenger from the Underworld to deliver letters across any distance.

## Volume 1: The Mentor of Tingen

Mr. Azik notices Klein's mysterious resurrection and teaches him ancient Fourth Epoch history. Sensing Klein's danger, Azik bestows the **Copper Whistle**, helping Klein identify Ince Zangwill's treason.

## Volume 3: Legend of the Pirate Hunter & The Battle of Bayam

Azik awakens portions of his Angelic memory, descending in Bayam with underworld bone dragons and skeletal armies to rescue Gehrman Sparrow from the demigods of the Rose School of Thought.

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past & The Slumber of Healing

Azik assists Klein in avenging Dunn Smith and executing Ince Zangwill. To heal his fragmented soul and recover his full Sequence 2 Angelic authority, Azik enters a century-long restorative slumber inside a golden coffin.

## Volume 8: The Awakening of the Consul

Klein Moretti (as Lord of the Mysteries) cleanses the River of Eternal Darkness, fully restoring Azik Eggers's soul. Azik awakens with complete memories, smiling proudly as he witnesses Klein's divine ascension.
