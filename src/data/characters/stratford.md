## Overview

**Viscount Stratford** was the Captain of the **Loen Royal Guards**, a prominent aristocratic noble in Backlund, and a **Sequence 6: Judge / Sequence 5: Disciplinary Paladin** of the **Justiciar Pathway (Arbiter Pathway)**. Serving as a trusted confidant and dirty-work enforcer for King George III, Stratford was responsible for silencing whistleblowers, hunting rogue demonesses, and executing loyal royal guards who discovered the Black Emperor mausoleums.

Stratford was the officer who directly framed and murdered **Mason Derecha** (the father of **Xio Derecha**).

:::reveal at=5

## Volume 5: Red Priest & Avenging Mason Derecha

Xio Derecha (Miss Judgment) targets Viscount Stratford to uncover the truth behind her father's execution. Working alongside Miss Justice and Gehrman Sparrow, Xio tracks Stratford to a secret meeting with Demoness Saint Katarina Pelle.

During the confrontation, Xio corners Stratford and forces a full confession regarding the framing of Mason Derecha and the royal conspiracy. To prevent higher-level royal secrets from leaking through spirit channeling, a concealed magical suicide curse triggers, detonating Stratford's body into bloody fragments.

:::
