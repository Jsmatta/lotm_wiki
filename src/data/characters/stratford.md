## Overview

**Colonel Stratford** was an elite senior officer of the **Loen Royal Guard**, serving as a trusted confidant and security enforcer for King **George Augustus III**. Walking the **Justiciar Pathway (Sequence 5: Disciplinary Paladin)**, Stratford was a disciplined, ruthless operative tasked with eliminating loose ends, silencing witnesses, and executing clandestine royal directives in Backlund.

He served as the primary liaison between the royal palace, corrupt officers in **MI9**, and the **Demoness Sect**.

:::reveal at=5

## Volume 5: Red Priest & The Downfall of the Colonel

Klein Moretti and Miss Judgment (**Xio Derecha**) track Stratford as the key link to the disappearance of East Borough laborers and the death of Prince Edessak. 

After ambushing Stratford in his carriage using paper figurine tricks and psychic pierces, Klein and Xio extract his memories, exposing the exact coordinates of King George III's secret mausoleums before executing him for treason against humanity.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Record of Justice

In Volume 8, Stratford's interrogation confession remains preserved in the historical archives of the Tarot Club, serving as absolute proof of the royal family's atrocities and justifying their downfall.
