## Overview

**Danitz (Blazing Danitz / Oracle Danitz)** is the charismatic fourth mate of the **Golden Dream Pirates**, serving under Vice Admiral Iceberg **Edwina Edwards**. Walking the **Hunter Pathway (Red Priest Pathway)**, Danitz begins as a **Sequence 8: Provoker** who uses razor-sharp insults and fireballs to confound enemies, while harboring a deeply loyal heart and a secret admiration for his scholarly captain.

After being captured by **Gehrman Sparrow (Klein Moretti)** in Bayam, Danitz is reluctantly conscripted as Gehrman's personal valet, laundry-doer, and tactical assistant, embarking on an unintended journey that leads him to become the revered **Oracle of The Fool**.

:::reveal at=3

## Volume 3: Legend of the Pirate Hunter & Gehrman's Valet

In Bayam, Danitz is held hostage by Gehrman Sparrow at the Wind of Azure bar. Terrified by Gehrman's cold-blooded demeanor and relentless pirate hunting, Danitz learns to anticipate Klein's every command, advancing to **Sequence 7: Pyromaniac** while assisting in the defeat of pirate champions.

:::

:::spoiler volume=5

## Volume 5: Red Priest & The Appointment as Oracle

Klein tasks Danitz with representing The Fool in the Rorsted Archipelago. Clad in fine gentleman's suits and wielding Sea God blessings, Danitz preaches The Fool's gospel to native resistance fighters and pirate crews, establishing the first public congregation of the **Church of the Fool**.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: The Grand Oracle of Bayam

Danitz advances to **Sequence 5: Reaper** and later **Sequence 4: Iron-Blooded Knight**, leading legions of faithful believers. He receives direct divine revelations from Sefirah Castle, commanding deep reverence from pirate admirals and imperial diplomats alike.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Supreme Oracle in Slumber

In Volume 8, as Klein Moretti enters divine slumber as **The Fool**, Danitz stands as the supreme terrestrial authority of the Church of the Fool in Bayam. Proudly wearing his holy vestments, Danitz coordinates global relief efforts, protects the faithful, and eagerly awaits the day his Lord awakens.

:::
