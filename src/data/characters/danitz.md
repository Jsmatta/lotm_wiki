## Overview

**Danitz (The Blazing / Flaming Danitz)** is the Fourth Boatswain of the **Golden Dream Pirates**, an experienced pirate officer walking the **Hunter Pathway (Sequence 7: Pyromaniac)**, and the official mortal **Oracle of The Fool**.

Comical, boastful, cowardly when facing unfathomable horrors, yet unexpectedly loyal and courageous in critical moments, Danitz is one of the most beloved comedic and operational characters in the series. Initially captured by **Gehrman Sparrow (Klein Moretti)** in Bayam, Danitz involuntarily becomes Gehrman's valet, translator, and maritime liaison, watching in terror and awe as his bounty skyrockets alongside Gehrman's legend.

## Pathway Progression & Abilities

- **Sequence 9: Hunter**: Exceptional tracking, trap setting, physical agility, and environmental survival.
- **Sequence 8: Provoker**: Masterful verbal and psychological taunting that disrupts enemy focus and provokes emotional blunders.
- **Sequence 7: Pyromaniac**: Controlling roaring flames, hurling explosive fireballs, forging fire ravens, and flame armor.
- **Sequence 5: Reaper / Conspirer**: Advanced close-quarters lethal assassination and tactical war planning.

## From Pirate Boatswain to Oracle of The Fool

Following the exodus of the **City of Silver** and the establishment of the **Church of the Fool** in Bayam:
- Mr. Fool appoints Danitz as his official earthly **Oracle**.
- Equipped with holy divine amulets and formal clerical robes, Danitz manages daily diplomacy with the Loen Kingdom, Feysac Empire, and Orthodox Churches, frequently sweating in panic while maintaining an outwardly serene, omniscient holy demeanor.

## Volume 3: Legend of the Pirate Hunter & The Bayam Captivity

Danitz is captured by Gehrman Sparrow in the Sweet Lemon Bar, Bayam. Terrified of Gehrman's cold ruthlessness, Danitz faithfully serves as Gehrman's pirate informant and assists in the *Groselle's Travels* expedition.

:::

## Volume 5: Red Priest & The Oracle Appointment

Danitz travels to Backlund and the Southern Continent, delivering messages between the Golden Dream and the Tarot Club. He receives divine revelations from Mr. Fool above the gray fog.

## Volume 7 & Volume 8: The Holy Oracle of Bayam

Danitz presides over the Cathedral of the Fool in Bayam, greeting world leaders and guiding the newly relocated citizens of the City of Silver and Moon City into modern society.
