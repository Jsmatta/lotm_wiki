## Overview

**Suah (Abomination Suah)** is an ancient, terrifying King of Angels of the **Chained Pathway (Sequence 1: Abomination)** and the biological "son" and chief high priest of the Outer Deity **Mother Tree of Desire**. As the supreme commander of the **Indulgence Faction** of the **Rose School of Thought**, Suah embodies absolute depravity, horrific physical mutation, and catastrophic curses.

Manifesting as a colossal, grotesque humanoid entity covered in shifting dark liquid, weeping eyes, and writhing tentacles, Suah's presence inflicts instantaneous physical and mental degradation upon all living matter.

:::reveal at=3

## Volume 3: Legend of the Pirate Hunter & The Colonial Incursions

Suah projects divine curses into the Sonia Sea, attempting to eliminate Sharron and Klein Moretti before being repelled by high-sequence church reinforcements.

:::

Suah coordinates Rose School of Thought armies across the Southern Continent, constructing flesh-and-blood sacrificial pyramids to invite the Mother Tree of Desire into Earth's realm.

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Southern Wastelands

Klein Moretti and Miss Messenger (**Reinette Tinekerr**) ambush Suah's forces in the Southern Continent, severing several of Suah's demonic projections and liberating thousands of enslaved believers.

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Assault on Sefirah Castle & The Abomination's Retreat

During Klein Moretti's apotheosis to Sequence 0, Suah leads the Indulgence Faction's desperate assault on Sefirah Castle. 

Klein unleashes the supreme divine authority of **The Fool**, shattering Suah's physical projection and forcing the Abomination into humiliating retreat back into the cosmic void, liberating the Southern Continent from his tyranny.
