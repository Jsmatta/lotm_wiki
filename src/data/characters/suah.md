## Overview

**Suah (The Abomination / Son of the God of Desire)** is the supreme terrestrial military leader of the **Indulgence Faction** of the **Rose School of Thought** and a horrifying **Sequence 1: Abomination Angel** of the **Chained Pathway (Prisoner Pathway)**. As the direct spiritual incarnation and chosen child of the Outer Deity **Mother Tree of Desire**, Suah commands absolute **Corruption**, **Indulgence**, **Blood Curses**, and **Mutative Desires**.

Taking the terrifying mythical creature form of a mountain of contorted, pulsing tree roots dripping with foul blood, severed limbs, and thousands of weeping eyes, Suah is one of Klein Moretti's most persistent and terrifying high-tier adversaries.

:::reveal at=3

## Volume 3: Legend of the Pirate Hunter & The Bayam Descent

Suah extends his colossal demonic tree-arm into the material realm in Bayam to crush Gehrman Sparrow, forcing Reinette Tinekerr and Azik Eggers to cooperate to sever the cursed arm.

:::

:::spoiler volume=5

## Volume 5: Red Priest & The Royal Mausoleum Battle

Suah intervenes during King George III's apotheosis ritual, attempting to assassinate Klein before being repelled by the combined angelic forces of Evernight and the Tarot Club.
