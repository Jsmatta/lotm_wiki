## Overview

**Ince Zangwill** was a former Archbishop of the **Church of the Evernight Goddess**, the primary antagonist of Volumes 1 through 4, and the orchestrator of the **Tingen Tragedy**. Walking the **Death Pathway (Sequence 5: Gatekeeper)** before illegally advancing to **Sequence 4: Iron-Blooded Knight** of the Hunter Pathway, Ince was the bearer of the terrifying Grade 0 Sealed Artifact **0-08: Quill of Alzuhod (The Scriptwriter's Quill)**.

Distinguished by his blind eye covered by a dark patch, priest robes, and cold, calculating ambition, Ince orchestrated the theft of **Saint Selena's Ashes** and the **Antigonus Family's Notebook** to fulfill his demigod aspirations.

:::reveal at=1

## Volume 1: Clown & The Script of Death

Under the invisible guidance of 0-08, Ince Zangwill scripts every tragedy in Tingen—from Welch's death to Megose's assault. Ince murders Captain Dunn Smith and shoots Klein Moretti through the heart, fleeing with Saint Selena's Ashes and the Antigonus Notebook.

:::

:::spoiler volume=4

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past & Ince's Execution

Klein Moretti, Leonard Mitchell, and Daly Simone track Ince Zangwill across East Balam in the Southern Continent. In a climactic confrontation in Calderon City, Klein summons historical projections, Leonard unleashes Pallez Zoroast, and Miss Messenger suppresses 0-08. 

Klein delivers the fatal strike, executing Ince Zangwill and avenging Dunn Smith, Daly, and the Tingen Nighthawks.

:::

:::spoiler volume=5

## Volume 5: Adam's Quill

Following Ince's demise, **0-08** is retrieved by **Adam**, who reveals that Ince was merely an unwitting actor in Adam's grand historical novel, paving the way for the World War.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Concluded Vendetta

In Volume 8, Ince Zangwill is remembered as a prime cautionary tale of how high-level sealed artifacts exploit mortal greed, while Klein Moretti stands victorious as The Fool, having completely dismantled Ince's conspiratorial legacy.

:::
