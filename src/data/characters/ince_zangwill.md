## Overview

**Ince Zangwill** was a former Archbishop of the **Church of the Evernight Goddess**, the notorious traitor responsible for the **Tingen Tragedy**, and the primary antagonist of Volumes 1 through 4. Walking the **Death Pathway (Sequence 5: Gatekeeper)** and later advancing illegally to **Sequence 4: Iron-Blooded Knight** of the Hunter Pathway, Ince was the bearer of the terrifying Grade 0 Sealed Artifact **0-08: Quill of Alzuhod (The Scriptwriter's Quill)**.

Distinguished by his blind eye covered by a dark patch, priest robes, and cold, ruthless ambition, Ince orchestrated the deaths of Dunn Smith and Klein Moretti to obtain the Saint Selena Ashes and Antigonus Notebook.

## Sealed Artifact 0-08 & The Script of Fate

- **0-08 (Quill of Alzuhod)**: A quill pen made from the feather of a Spectator Angel (Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt) that writes events into reality by subtly manipulating coincidences.
- **The Tingen Conspiracy**: Writing the script that forced Megose, Lanevus, and the True Creator's fetus to converge on Tingen.

## Volume 1: Clown & The Betrayal at Chanis Gate

Ince steals Sealed Artifact 0-08, coordinates the suicide of the original Klein Moretti, and murders Captain Dunn Smith to steal Saint Selena's Ashes before fleeing to the Southern Continent.

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past & The Final Retribution

In the Southern Continent, Ince Zangwill's script begins to unravel under the unpredictable interference of Sefirah Castle. Leonard Mitchell, Daly Simone, and Dwayne Dantès (Klein Moretti) corner Ince Zangwill in a ruined cathedral.

Adam takes back Quill 0-08, leaving Ince stripped of his plot armor. Gehrman Sparrow executes Ince with Azik's bone claws and spirit bullets, successfully avenging the Tingen Nighthawks.
