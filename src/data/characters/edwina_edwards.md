## Overview

**Edwina Edwards (Vice Admiral Iceberg)** is one of the celebrated Seven Pirate Admirals of the Five Seas and the scholarly captain of *The Golden Dream*. Walking the **Reader Pathway (White Tower Pathway)** as a demigod **Sequence 4: Mysticism Magister**, Edwina is renowned for her cool intellect, encyclopedic mastery of Fourth Epoch history, and passion for archaeological research.

Covertly affiliated with the **Church of the God of Knowledge and Wisdom**, Edwina rejects reckless violence, operating her pirate crew as a traveling floating academy.

:::reveal at=3

## Volume 3: Legend of the Pirate Hunter & The Voyage of the Golden Dream

Edwina welcomes Gehrman Sparrow aboard *The Golden Dream*. Together, they decipher Fourth Epoch inscriptions and explore the living storybook world of **Groselle's Travels**, slaying the King of the North.

:::

:::spoiler volume=5

## Volume 5: Red Priest & Maritime Archeology

Edwina guides scholarly naval expeditions during the chaotic maritime skirmishes of the World War, protecting innocent merchant fleets and decoding ancient murals of the Cataclysm.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & Danitz's Ascension

Edwina watches in astonishment as her formerly unruly fourth mate **Danitz** transforms into the revered **Oracle of The Fool**, establishing a formal academic and religious partnership between her crew and the Church of the Fool.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Archangel's Colleague

In Volume 8, Vice Admiral Iceberg leads joint archaeological missions with the Tarot Club toward the newly opened **Western Continent**, chronicling the ancient history of Earth and securing knowledge for future generations.
