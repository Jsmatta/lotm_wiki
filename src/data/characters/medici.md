## Overview

**Medici (The Red Angel / War Angel)** was one of the legendary Eight Kings of Angels serving the **Ancient Sun God** in the Third Epoch. Wielding supreme authority over the **Hunter Pathway (Sequence 1: Conqueror)**, Medici was the supreme commander of the divine holy legions, personifying absolute **War**, **Provocation**, **Flames**, **Conquest**, and **Iron Blood**.

Fiercely sarcastic, foul-mouthed, and an unparalleled master of psychological provocation, Medici was betrayed and murdered in the Fourth Epoch during the War of the Four Emperors by Alista Tudor (The Blood Emperor).

## The Evil Spirit & The Tri-Soul Chimera

Following his physical death, Medici's vengeful spirit fused with the deceased souls of **Einhorn** and **Sauron** (two demigods of the Hunter pathway), creating the notorious **Red Angel Evil Spirit**:
- Possessing three distinct personalities sharing a single spiritual body, with Medici retaining dominant control while engaging in constant comedic arguments with the other two souls.

## Volume 2: The Underground Ruins of Backlund

Detective Sherlock Moriarty (Klein) discovers the Red Angel Evil Spirit sealed within Fourth Epoch subterranean ruins beneath Backlund. Klein manipulates the seal, causing the evil spirit to clash with royal investigators before escaping into the city.
