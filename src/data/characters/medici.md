## Overview

**Medici (The Red Angel / War Angel)** was one of the legendary Eight Kings of Angels serving the **Ancient Sun God** in the Third Epoch. Wielding the **Hunter Pathway (Red Priest Pathway)**, Medici was the supreme commander of the divine holy legions, representing absolute **War**, **Provocation**, **Flames**, **Conquest**, and **Iron Blood**.

Fiercely sarcastic, foul-mouthed, and an unparalleled master of psychological provocation, Medici was betrayed and murdered in the Fourth Epoch during the War of the Four Emperors by Alista Tudor (The Blood Emperor).

## The Evil Spirit & The Tri-Soul Chimera

Following his physical death, Medici's vengeful spirit fused with the deceased souls of **Einhorn** and **Sauron** (two demigods of the Hunter pathway), creating the notorious **Red Angel Evil Spirit**:
- Possessing three distinct personalities sharing a single spiritual body, with Medici retaining dominant control while relentlessly insulting the other two heads.
- Sealed for centuries beneath the underground ruins of **Backlund**, plotting his resurrection and ascension to Sequence 0: Red Priest.

## Volume 2: The Faceless & The Backlund Underground

Sherlock Moriarty (Klein Moretti) and detective Isengard Stanton discover the ancient subterranean ruins in Backlund. The Red Angel Evil Spirit manipulates low-sequence cultists and attempts to break free from his seal.

## Volume 5: Red Priest & The World War Instigation

The Red Angel Evil Spirit escapes Backlund, coordinating with the Demoness Sect, royal family, and Amon. He actively fuels continental geopolitical hatred, igniting the flames of the World War to digest the Sequence 1 Conqueror characteristic.

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7 & Volume 8: The Slaying of Mr. Door & Adam's Alliance

Medici allies with Amon to accelerate Bethel Abraham's return. Later, Medici forms an operational pact with Adam, receiving divine backing to pursue the Sequence 0: Red Priest throne to lead Earth's planetary legions during the cosmic apocalypse.
