## Overview

**Bethel Abraham (Mr. Door)** was the legendary Fourth Epoch patriarch of the **Abraham Family**, King of Angels of the **Door Pathway (Sequence 1: Key of Stars)**, and one of the most powerful entities in human history. Wielding supreme mastery over spatial transitions, pocket dimensions, and sealing arts, Bethel was regarded during the Tudor Dynasty as the strongest archangel beneath True Gods.

Seeking deeper cosmic truths, Bethel ventured beyond Earth's astral barrier into the deep cosmos during the Fourth Epoch, only to be ambushed, sealed, and relentlessly corrupted by the Outer Deity **Mother Goddess of Depravity**.

:::reveal at=2

## Volume 2: The Crimson Moon Whispers

Every Full Moon, all descendants of the Abraham Family hear agonizing, maddening murmurs echoing from the void of the cosmos:
> *"Don't save me... Don't save me..."*

These psychic ravings, originating from Bethel's corrupted prison, cause countless Abraham Beyonders to lose control and mutate.

:::

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past & The Family Heirloom

Fors Wall learns of Bethel's tragic plight through Dorian Gray Abraham, while Klein Moretti observes Bethel's astral coordinates from above the gray fog in Sefirah Castle.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: The Cosmic Descent Trap

Corrupted by the Mother Goddess of Depravity, Bethel's return to Earth is engineered by cosmic cultists to shatter Earth's astral barrier. Recognizing his impending loss of self, Bethel struggles with his remaining shred of consciousness to warn humanity.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Archangel's Heroic Sacrifice

In Volume 8, Bethel returns to Earth. Knowing that his physical survival will invite cosmic destruction, Bethel heroically shouts for the Orthodox Gods to slay him:
> *"Kill me! Do not let the cosmos descend!"*

Amon parasitizes Bethel and steals his Door Pathway characteristics and Uniqueness to become a dual-pathway True God. Bethel's sacrifice saves Earth's protective barrier, and his remaining uncorrupted legacy is entrusted to Fors Wall and the Abraham Family by The Fool.

:::
