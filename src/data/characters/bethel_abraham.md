## Overview

**Bethel Abraham (Mr. Door)** was the legendary patriarch and founding ancestor of the **Abraham Family**, widely revered as the strongest and most brilliant **King of Angels** of the Fourth Epoch. Accommodating the **Sequence 0: Door Uniqueness** and all three Sequence 1: Key of Stars characteristics, Bethel was on the verge of ascending to Sequence 0: Door when he was exiled into deep cosmic space during the War of the Four Emperors.

Possessing peerless mastery over spatial exile, dimensional seals, astral wandering, and universal conceptual copying, Bethel is one of the most tragic and heroic figures in the history of the mystical world.

## The Cosmic Exile & The Blood Moon Curse

Trapped beyond the planetary barrier for over a thousand years:
- Bethel wandered through the dark starry sky, desperately searching for a way home to his family.
- During his celestial wanderings, he was contaminated by the Outer Deity **Mother Goddess of Depravity**, becoming a high-risk psychic conduit.
- Whenever Bethel approached Earth during the **Blood Moon**, his anguished psychic screams resonated through the bloodlines of all Abraham Family descendants, causing generations of Beyonders to suffer madness and loss of control.

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & The Supreme Sacrifice

The Aurora Order and corrupted descendant Verdu Garcia perform the summoning ritual to drag Mr. Door back to Earth. Recognizing that his descent will bring the Mother Goddess of Depravity straight into Earth's atmosphere and extinguish humanity, Bethel makes the ultimate heroic sacrifice:
- He screams to Amon: *"Kill me!"*
- He willingly opens his mind to let Amon steal his Door Uniqueness and Sequence 1 characteristics, ensuring that a sane Earth demigod receives his power while severing the Outer Deity's grip.
- Before his soul dissipates, Bethel looks fondly upon Earth, bestowing his final blessing upon the Abraham family and Fors Wall.
