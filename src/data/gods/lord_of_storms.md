## Overview

The **Lord of Storms (Leodero)** is one of the seven Orthodox Deities of the Fifth Epoch and the primary naval deity of the **Loen Kingdom**, the **Rorsted Archipelago**, and the **Five Seas**. As the true god of **Sequence 0: Tyrant (Sailor Pathway)**, Leodero wields absolute dominion over **Thunder**, **Lightning**, **Tsunamis**, **Hurricanes**, and **Militaristic Conquest**.

Fierce, wrathful, and prideful, Leodero is famously responsive to anyone who utters his divine true name without reverence:
> Uttering *"Leodero"* aloud anywhere in the world instantly attracts a blinding bolt of divine lightning from the heavens.

## Divine Lineage & The Wind Angel

In the Third Epoch, Leodero was revered as **The Wind Angel**, one of the Eight Kings of Angels serving the **Ancient Sun God**. During the mutiny of the Rose Redemption ritual, Leodero struck down the Ancient Sun God alongside Aucuses and Herabergen, consuming the Sequence 0 Tyrant characteristic.
