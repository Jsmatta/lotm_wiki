## Overview

The **God of Knowledge and Wisdom (Herabergen)** is one of the seven Orthodox Deities of the Fifth Epoch and the state religion of the **Lenburg Kingdom**, **Masin**, and **Segar**. Ruling over **Sequence 0: White Tower (Reader Pathway)**, Herabergen is the supreme master of **Omniscience**, **Mystical Analysis**, **Spell Imitation**, **Prophecy**, and **Universal Truth**.

Depicted as a scholar holding a massive open grimoire or a majestic white feathered dragon enveloped in ancient runes, he is revered by academics, mages, and detectives across the world.

## Divine Lineage & The Dragon of Wisdom

Originating in the Second Epoch as the ancient mythical beast **Herabergen (The Dragon of Wisdom)**, he submitted to the **Ancient Sun God** in the Third Epoch and was appointed as **The Wisdom Angel**, one of the Eight Kings of Angels. 

During the Rose Redemption betrayal, Herabergen calculated the precise metaphysical variables required to consume the White Tower characteristic and ascend as Sequence 0.
