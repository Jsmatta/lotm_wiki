## Overview

The **Ancient Sun God (The City of Calamity / God Almighty / Lord of the Astral World)** is the foundational supreme deity of the **Third Epoch (The Cataclysm / Epoch of Glory)**. Awakening from the subterranean human sanctuary of **Chernobyl** on ancient Earth holding the First Blasphemy Slate, he reclaimed humanity from the tyranny of ancient monstrous gods (Flegrea, Salinger, Gregrace, and the Giant King Aurmir), forging the Holy Kingdom and establishing the modern 22 Beyonder Pathways system.

Revered as the **Father of all Angels**, he accommodated the Sefirah **Chaos Sea** and five interrelated pathways (**Sun**, **Sailor**, **Reader**, **Spectator**, and **Secrets Supplicant**), nearly ascending as the supreme Great Old One **God Almighty**.

## The Eight Kings of Angels

To govern his celestial dominion, the Ancient Sun God appointed his eight supreme angel attendants:
- **Adam** (The Angel of Imagination) — His eldest son.
- **Amon** (The Angel of Time) — His second son.
- **Medici** (The Red Angel / War Angel).
- **Ouroboros** (The Tail-Devourer / Angel of Fate).
- **Sasrir** (The Dark Angel / Deputy of the Kingdom).
- **Leodero** (The Wind Angel / future Lord of Storms).
- **Aucuses** (The White Angel / future Eternal Blazing Sun).
- **Herabergen** (The Wisdom Angel / future God of Knowledge and Wisdom).

## The Fall & Tragedy of Betrayal

As the Ancient Sun God neared the apex of God Almighty, the primordial cosmic will of the original God Almighty awakened inside him, threatening him with total loss of identity. To purge the corruption, he arranged a controlled sacrificial resurrection with Sasrir and Rose Redemption.

However, during the critical ritual, his trusted attendants **Leodero**, **Aucuses**, and **Herabergen** betrayed him, dismembering his divine body and consuming his sequence characteristics to ascend as true gods. His lingering blood and hatred manifested as the **True Creator**, while his pure divine consciousness survived in his son **Adam**.
