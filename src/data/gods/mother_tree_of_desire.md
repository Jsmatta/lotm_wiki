## Overview

The **Mother Tree of Desire (Father of Devils / The Heartless One)** is a malevolent, voracious **Outer Deity (Great Old One)** currently lurking beyond Earth's astral seal. Worshipped by the corrupted Indulgence Faction of the **Rose School of Thought**, this cosmic terror embodies **Uncontrollable Desire**, **Primal Hedonism**, **Abyssal Malice**, **Curses**, and **Spiritual Enslavement**.

Visualized in occult visions as a colossal grotesque bleeding tree whose root system is composed of writhing human limbs and whose fruits are weeping demon faces, the Mother Tree of Desire corrupts any entity that yields to unrestrained emotion.

## Stolen Authorities & Sefirah Plunder

During the convergence of the Original Creator, the Mother Tree of Desire had substantial portions of its cosmic power ripped away and absorbed into Earth:
- It lost parts of the **Chained Pathway (Prisoner / Abomination)** and the **Abyss Pathway (Criminal / Devil)**.
- It lost connection to its corresponding Sefirah, the **Tenebrous World**.
- Consequently, it continually seeks to breach Earth's barrier to absorb the Chained Angel **Reinette Tinekerr**, the Devil monarchs, and the Tenebrous World.
