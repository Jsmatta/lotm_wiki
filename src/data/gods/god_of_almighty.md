## Overview

**God Almighty** (formally: *God Almighty, Creator, Maker, The Omnipotent and Omniscient, Lord of the Astral World*) is one of the primordial **Pillars of the Universe (Great Old Ones)**. Ruling over the Sefirah **Chaos Sea**, this supreme entity commands the five interconnected pathways of divinity: **Visionary (Spectator)**, **Sun**, **Tyrant (Sailor)**, **White Tower (Reader)**, and **The Hanged Man (Secrets Supplicant)**.

Along with the *Lord of the Mysteries* and the *Mother Goddess of Depravity*, God Almighty represents one of the three primary pillars that govern the fundamental physics and metaphysics of the cosmos.

## Honorific Title & Sefirah

> *"God Almighty;*  
> *The Omnipotent and Omniscient;*  
> *Maker, Creator;*  
> *Lord of the Astral World."*

- **Authorities**: Omnipotence, omniscience, mind alteration, light creation, elemental storm destruction, infinite knowledge, and sin redemption.
- **Sefirah Source**: **The Chaos Sea**, the primordial sub-dimensional ocean of spiritual magma that lies beneath the Northern Continent.

## Ancient Lineage

- **Pre-Epoch**: The original God Almighty engaged in a cataclysmic battle against the Celestial Worthy, leading to both of their physical deaths on ancient Earth.
- **Third Epoch**: The **Ancient Sun God** awakened in Chernobyl, accommodating the Chaos Sea and walking the path of God Almighty before being betrayed by Storm, Sun, and Wisdom.
