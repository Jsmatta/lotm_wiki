## Overview

The **True Creator** (also known as the **Fallen Creator** or **The Hanged Man**) is the sinister, wrathful deity worshipped by the **Aurora Order**. Embodying the **Hanged Man Pathway (Secrets Supplicant / Shepherd)**, the True Creator is portrayed in occult iconography as a giant inverted bleeding deity chained to a crimson crucifix, listening perpetually to the depraved sins, weeping, and madness of the mortal realm.

While universally reviled by the Orthodox Churches as the most vicious and unstable evil god of the Fifth Epoch, his true origin is inextricably tied to the tragic downfall of the **Ancient Sun God**.

## Mythical Origin & The Fallen Divinity

When the Ancient Sun God was assassinated and devoured by the Three Traitor Gods (Storm, Sun, and Wisdom) at the end of the Third Epoch, the god's lingering hatred, resentment, and sacrificial blood fell into the subterranean **Chaos Sea**. 

Merging with the corpse of Dark Angel Sasrir, this concentrated divine malice coalesced into the **True Creator**, obsessed with purifying humanity through apocalyptic rebirth and reclaiming his stolen pathways.

## Divine Authorities & Modus Operandi

- **Pathway**: Sequence 0: The Hanged Man.
- **Divine Domain**: Flesh manipulation, shadow shadows, grazing souls, listening to secrets, sacrificial redemption through agonizing suffering.
- **Iconography**: An inverted human figure bound to a wooden or thorny cross, bleeding from crown to feet.
