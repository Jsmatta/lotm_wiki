## Overview

The **Mother Goddess of Depravity (Origin of Evil / The Indestructible)** is the oldest, most horrific, and most powerful of the **Outer Deities (Great Old Ones)** encircling Earth's astral barrier. In primordial cosmic history, she was one of the three supreme **Pillars of the Universe**, governing **Femininity**, **Creation**, **Reproduction**, **Biological Proliferation**, and **Cosmic Motherhood**.

Her presence is directly manifested through the **Crimson Moon (Blood Moon)**, which bathes Earth in corrupting psychic radiation that causes biological mutations, madness, and involuntary pregnancies among Beyonders.

## The Shattered Authority & Sefirah Plunder

When the **Original Creator** awakened and converged on pre-epoch Earth:
- The Creator tore away half of the Mother Goddess of Depravity's cosmic essence, pulling the Sefirah **Brood Hive**, the **Sequence 0: Moon**, and the **Sequence 0: Mother** authorities into Earth's planetary crust.
- Reduced in status from a supreme Pillar to an incomplete Great Old One, the Mother Goddess of Depravity has relentlessly circled Earth for millions of years, desperate to breach the planetary barrier and reclaim her stolen Brood Hive.
