## Overview

The **Lord of the Mysteries** (formally: *The King of Space-Time, Beacon of Destiny, Dominator of the Spirit World, Lord of the Mysteries*) is one of the three supreme, primordial **Pillars of the Universe (Great Old Ones)**. Holding dominion over the Sefirah **Sefirah Castle (The Door of Light)**, this cosmic entity governs the trinity of the **Fool Pathway**, **Door Pathway**, and **Error Pathway**.

A true Pillar of the Universe represents the highest stable energetic and conceptual pinnacle achievable in the cosmos. Beyond this level, accommodating more Sefirot causes irreversible mental collapse and cosmic divergence into the Original Creator.

## Honorific Title & Authorities

> *"The King of Space-Time;*  
> *The Beacon of Destiny;*  
> *The Dominator of the Spirit World;*  
> *Lord of the Mysteries."*

- **Authorities**: Total omnipotent manipulation of space, time, dimensions, historical projection, concealment, grafting concepts, fooling laws of reality, fate reweaving, and spiritual omniscience.
- **Sefirah Source**: **Sefirah Castle** (The Door of Light within the Spirit World's gray fog).

## The Successor & The Cosmic Slumber

Following the pre-epoch death of the original holder (**The Celestial Worthy**), the title and cosmic mantle remained vacant for five epochs until claimed by **Klein Moretti (The Fool)**. To preserve Earth against the approaching Outer Deities while defending his own soul from the Celestial Worthy's awakening, Klein fell into a cosmic slumber above the gray fog.
