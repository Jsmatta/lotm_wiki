## Overview

**Lilith** (originally known as **The Beauty Goddess / Mother Goddess**) is the ancient progenitor and primordial ancestor of the entire **Sanguine (Vampire)** race. In the modern Fifth Epoch, she is publicly worshipped across the Southern Continent and the **Feynapotter Kingdom** under the holy disguise of the **Earth Mother**.

Commanding the dual divine authorities of the **Moon Pathway** and the **Mother Pathway (Planter / Apothecary)**, Lilith is the supreme sovereign of **Fertility**, **Life**, **Nature**, **Biological Proliferation**, and **Blood Magic**.

## The Millennium Deception

During the Cataclysm of the Second Epoch, ancient historical texts recorded that Lilith was slain by the Giant King Aurmir. In reality:
- With the secret intervention of the **Evernight Goddess (Amanises)**, Lilith survived in concealment.
- Following the death of Giant Queen Omebella, Lilith took on Omebella's appearance and ascended to Sequence 0: Mother, establishing the **Church of the Earth Mother**.
- For three entire epochs, the world—and even the orthodox gods—believed the Earth Mother was Omebella, allowing Lilith to protect her scattered Sanguine descendants while biding her time.
