## Overview

**The Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings** (often abbreviated as **The Celestial Worthy**) is one of the original, primordial **Pillars of the Universe (Great Old Ones)** who existed on Earth prior to the First Epoch. Embodying the supreme conceptual authorities of **Space-Time**, **Destiny**, **History**, **Miracles**, and **Concealment**, the Celestial Worthy originally held complete dominion over **Sefirah Castle** and the trinity of the **Fool**, **Door**, and **Error Pathways**.

In ancient Chinese mythology on pre-epoch Earth, he was revered by secret cultivators and esoteric Taoist traditions as the supreme sovereign of mysteries.

## Pre-Epoch Schemes & The Transmigrator Cocoon

Recognizing the inevitable cosmic law of convergence and divergence that leads even Great Old Ones to perish, the Celestial Worthy formulated an epoch-spanning resurrection arrangement prior to his physical demise:
- He sealed the primordial **Sefirah Castle** within the gray fog of the Spirit World.
- He abducted thousands of modern human souls from pre-epoch Earth (including **Zhou Mingrui / Klein Moretti**, **Roselle Gustav**, and **Amanises**), enclosing them inside light-cocoon chrysalises suspended above the gray fog.
- He programmed Sefirah Castle to release these souls across subsequent epochs, hoping one would advance to Sequence 0 and serve as his vessel for total mental resurrection.

## The Mental Awakening & Battle of Wills

Whenever a Beyonder of the Fool, Door, or Error Pathway advances to demigod and angel sequences, the dormant primordial consciousness of the Celestial Worthy begins reviving within their mind, attempting to eradicate their human ego and take over their body.

## Volume 1: The Luck Enhancement Ritual

Zhou Mingrui performs the four-corner fortune-telling ritual using the ancient Hermes/Chinese incantation: *"The Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings..."*, triggering his soul's release from the light cocoon and transmigration into Klein Moretti's deceased body in Tingen.

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & The Door of Light

Klein uncovers the true nature of Sefirah Castle and sees the countless translucent soul cocoons dangling above the fog, realizing that he, Roselle, and the Evernight Goddess were all pawns in the Celestial Worthy's resurrection gamble.

## Volume 8: The Cosmic Slumber & Struggle for Self

After defeating Amon and accommodating the Fool, Door, and Error Uniquenesses, Klein Moretti initiates his ascension to **Lord of the Mysteries**. To prevent the Celestial Worthy's ancient will from erasing his humanity and consuming Earth, Klein chooses to enter a deep cosmic slumber, engaging in an eternal battle of wills above the fog.
