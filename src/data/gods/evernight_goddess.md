## Overview

The **Evernight Goddess**, formally known in ancient history as **Amanises**, is the supreme orthodox deity of the **Church of the Evernight Goddess**, widely revered as the primary state religion of the Loen Kingdom. She commands divine authority over **Darkness**, **Concealment**, **Silence**, **Repose**, **Misfortune**, and **Horror**.

Her holy emblem is a crimson crescent moon surrounded by radiant starlight against a deep obsidian night sky, symbolizing tranquil rest, protection for the weak, and the terror that lurks within the shadows.

## True Origins: The Pre-Epoch Transmigrator

Amanises is one of the original human transmigrators who fell into suspended animation inside Sefirah Castle on pre-epoch Earth. Awakening in the brutal Second Epoch (The Dark Epoch), she took the form of the subsidiary Demonic Wolf of Misfortune under the ancient deity Flegrea. Using her extraordinary intellect and ruthless calculation, she orchestrated Flegrea's downfall, digested the Sequence 0 characteristics, and ascended to become a True God.

## Alignment with Klein Moretti

Throughout the series, the Evernight Goddess acts as Klein Moretti's most formidable divine patron. Recognizing Klein as a fellow transmigrator ("fellow countryman from Earth"), she guides his growth with exquisite subtlety:
- Placing the **Antigonus Family's Notebook** within Tingen to guide his initiation into the Seer pathway.
- Providing divine concealment to shroud his actions above the gray fog from the prying eyes of the True Creator and Amon.
- Bestowing the **Trunsoest Brass Book** and high-sequence holy blessings during his most perilous battles.

## Volume 2: The Faceless & Backlund Concealment

The Evernight Goddess's clergy, led by Archbishop Anthony Stevenson and the Nighthawks, maintain the containment of ancient sealed artifacts beneath Saint Samuel Cathedral. Her divine concealment authority prevents the True Creator from fully synchronizing with his descent vessel during the Great Smog of Backlund.

:::

:::spoiler volume=5

## Volume 5: The War of Gods

During the world war engineered by the Twilight Hermit Order, the Evernight Goddess maneuvers behind the scenes of the orthodox pantheon, preparing a decisive strike against the aggressive **God of Combat (Badheil)**.

:::

:::spoiler volume=6

## Volume 6: Light of the City of Silver

When Amon traps Klein in the Forsaken Land and drags him to Chernobyl, the Evernight Goddess intervenes directly through concealment. She enables Klein to commit ritual suicide and resurrect safely back in Backlund, preventing Amon from claiming Sefirah Castle.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & Divine Apotheosis

The Evernight Goddess initiates the **Divine Battle of Gods**. Revealing her ancient alliance with the Earth Mother (who was secretly Lilith, the ancient Sanguine Ancestor), she catches the God of Combat completely off guard. 

Badheil is slain, and Amanises accommodates the **Twilight Giant Pathway's** Sequence 0 characteristic and Uniqueness, along with the **Death Pathway's** Uniqueness.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: Eternal Darkness

Amanises assists Klein Moretti during his Fool Apotheosis Ritual, shielding Earth against cosmic interference from the Outer Deities. Holding the authorities of **Darkness**, **Death**, and **Twilight Giant**, she accommodates the Sefirah **River of Eternal Darkness**, preparing to ascend as the Great Old One **Eternal Darkness (Calamity of Destruction)** to defend Earth's barrier.

:::
