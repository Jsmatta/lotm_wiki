## Overview

The **Outer Deities (Cosmic Great Old Ones / Cosmos Horrors)** are supreme, ancient eldritch entities originating from the deep cosmos outside Earth. Drawn across lightyears by the irresistible cosmic convergence force of the **Original Creator**, these entities currently encircle Earth's astral seal in a perpetual cosmic blockade.

Their mere gaze, whispers, or stray thoughts can instantly mutate and drive normal human minds, demigods, and angels into irreversible insanity.

## The Planetary Siege & The Astral Barrier

Prior to his temporary physical divergence, the Original Creator's death created an invisible, semi-permeable **Astral Barrier** enclosing Earth:
- The barrier prevents Outer Deities from directly descending into the material plane.
- However, the barrier is continuously decaying and will completely shatter by the year 1368 (the Fifth Epoch's predicted **Apocalypse**).
- Consequently, orthodox deities and Great Old One candidates (**Lord of the Mysteries**, **God Almighty**, **Eternal Darkness**) race against time to ascend and defend human civilization.

## Key Outer Deities Circling Earth

1. **Mother Goddess of Depravity (Origin of Evil)**: Former universal pillar seeking the *Brood Hive* and *Moon/Mother* authorities.
2. **Mother Tree of Desire (Father of Devils)**: Corrupting the Rose School of Thought to reclaim the *Tenebrous World* and *Chained/Abyss* pathways.
3. **Son of Chaos (The Indefinable)**: Seeking the *Nation of Disorder* and freedom from the *Magic Wishing Lamp*.
4. **Primordial Hunger**, **Supernova Dominator**, **Goddess of Fate**, **Inextinguishable Ravings**, and **Monarch of Decay**.
