## Overview

The **Eternal Blazing Sun (Aucuses)** is one of the seven Orthodox Deities of the Fifth Epoch and the supreme state religion of the **Intis Republic**. Ruling over **Sequence 0: Sun**, he commands absolute divine authority over **Light**, **Purification**, **Solar Radiance**, **Holy Fire**, and **Oath Justice**.

In mortal iconography, he is depicted as a radiant golden solar orb or a crowned angel surrounded by twelve solar flares, revered as the banisher of darkness and unclean abominations.

## Divine Origins & The White Angel

During the Third Epoch, Aucuses was known as **The White Angel**, one of the Eight Kings of Angels serving the **Ancient Sun God**. Charged with guarding solar purity and holy light, he conspired with Leodero (Wind Angel) and Herabergen (Wisdom Angel) during the Rose Redemption ritual. 

Following their betrayal and consumption of the Ancient Sun God's flesh and characteristics, Aucuses claimed the Sequence 0: Sun Uniqueness, establishing the **Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun**.
