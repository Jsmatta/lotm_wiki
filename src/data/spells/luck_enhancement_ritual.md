## Overview

The **Luck Enhancement Ritual (The Four-Corner Transmigration Ritual)** is an ancient, esoteric ceremony originally performed in the pre-epoch modern world by **Zhou Mingrui** after obtaining a mysterious occult manual (*The Quintessence of Ancient Divination and Divinatory Arts*). Believing it to be a harmless novelty charm to alter his fortune, Zhou Mingrui performed the ritual in his apartment—unknowingly triggering the cosmic mechanisms of **Sefirah Castle** and causing his soul to transmigrate into the deceased body of **Klein Moretti** in the Fifth Epoch.

Throughout the series, the Luck Enhancement Ritual serves as Klein's primary, reliable spiritual bridge to ascend to the mysterious realm above the gray fog, host the **Tarot Club**, and access the divine authority of the **Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth**.

## Ritual Procedure & Mechanics

The ceremony requires strict spatial and mystical alignment:
1. **Sacrificial Offerings**: Four portions of staple food placed in the four corners of a private room (originally sliced white bread, meat patties, noodles, and rice; later substituted with bread, pastries, and distilled spirits).
2. **Sacred Square Walk**: Starting from the center of the room, the practitioner takes four steps counterclockwise in a square pattern, pausing at each corner.
3. **Four-Line Incantation**: At each step, the practitioner recites one line of the ancient Chinese incantation:
   - Step 1: *The Immortal Lord of Heaven and Earth for Blessings* (福生玄黄天尊 / Fú shēng xuánhuáng tiānzūn)
   - Step 2: *The Sky Lord of Heaven and Earth for Blessings* (福生玄黄领军 / Fú shēng xuánhuáng lǐngjūn)
   - Step 3: *The Exalted Thearch of Heaven and Earth for Blessings* (福生玄黄上帝 / Fú shēng xuánhuáng shàngdì)
   - Step 4: *The Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings* (福生玄黄天君 / Fú shēng xuánhuáng tiānjūn)
4. **Ascension**: Closing one's eyes and regulating breath until a continuous, deafening roar of cosmic whispers erupts in the mind, lifting the spirit past the Astral Realm into Sefirah Castle.

## Volume 1: Clown & The Fog of Sefirah Castle

Klein repeats the ritual in his rented room on Daffodil Street, hoping it will send him back to modern Earth. Instead, his spirit ascends above the gray fog, discovering the ancient bronze palace and accidentally pulling **Audrey Hall (Justice)** and **Alger Wilson (The Hanged Man)** into the first session of the **Tarot Club**.

:::

## Volume 3: The Sanctuary of Divination & Purification

Klein utilizes the ritual as an instant emergency escape button for his spiritual body, purging lethal spiritual curses, psychic contaminations, and high-sequence divinations inside Sefirah Castle.

## Volume 7 & Volume 8: The Celestial Worthy's Awakening

Klein discovers the dark truth behind the ritual: it was created by the **Celestial Worthy** as a soul-harvesting and resurrection protocol to implant transmigrators with the Sefirah Castle seal. As Klein ascends to Sequence 0: The Fool, the incantation becomes Klein's own divine anchor.
