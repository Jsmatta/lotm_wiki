## Overview

**Dream Divination** is an essential, high-precision fortune-telling technique belonging to the **Seer Pathway (Fool Pathway)** and the **Mystery Pryer Pathway**. By formulating a strictly defined, non-ambiguous divination statement, writing it on paper, and entering a light spiritual trance through cogitation, the practitioner bypasses physical cognitive limitations to query the **Spirit World**, receiving revelations in the form of rich, symbolic dreams.

Throughout his journey, Klein Moretti relies on Dream Divination as his most dependable investigative and deductive weapon to uncover murder conspiracies, track hidden enemies, and verify mystical formulae.

## Divination Methodology & Cogitation

1. **The Divination Statement**: The caster writes a precise declaration (e.g., *"The location of the Antigonus family's notebook"* or *"There is danger in entering this building"*). Ambiguity or compound questions degrade accuracy.
2. **Cogitation & Recitation**: Holding the written statement or relevant medium (a cane, a pendulum, a hair sample), the caster recites the statement seven times while visualizing celestial light to silence conscious thoughts.
3. **Dream Ingress**: Sinking into a shallow sleep state where the Spirit World projects allegorical scenes, audio fragments, chromatic halos, and symbolic metaphors directly into the subconscious mind.
4. **Symbolic Interpretation**: Translating the dream imagery back into factual reality (e.g., a broken carriage symbolizing travel peril, a crimson moon indicating blood sacrifice, a weeping raven indicating death).

## Sefirah Castle Amplification & Anti-Interference

In ordinary reality, powerful demigods, angels, and concealment spells can distort or falsify dream divination results. However, when Klein performs Dream Divination atop the bronze table of **Sefirah Castle** above the gray fog:
- The gray fog cuts through all mortal anti-divination wards and false illusions.
- It completely eliminates mental corruption risks when divining high-sequence artifacts and deity-level secrets.
