## Overview

**Dream Divination** is one of the most reliable and profound divination disciplines practiced by the **Seer Pathway** and **Darkness Pathway**. By entering a meditative self-hypnotic trance or natural sleep while holding a specific divination statement, the practitioner allows their spiritual body to wander into the collective subconscious and the **Spirit World**, where past, present, and future converge.

Unlike mundane dreams which stem from chaotic psychological discharges, Dream Divination receives direct symbolic impressions, prophetic omens, and hidden truths projected by the cosmos.

## The Divination Protocol

To avoid self-deception and psychological interference, a Seer follows precise mystical guidelines:
1. **The Divination Statement**: The inquiry must be formulated as a clear, concise, and unambiguous declarative sentence (e.g., *"The origin of this diary page"* or *"The danger present in 36 Zouteland Street"*).
2. **Mental Cleansing (Cogitation)**: The diviner regulates their breath, enters a state of deep cogitation, and repeats the divination statement seven times in their mind.
3. **Symbolic Decoding**: The information received in dreams is rarely literal; it arrives as dense allegorical imagery, archetypal figures, colors, weather patterns, and emotional impressions requiring rigorous interpretation.
