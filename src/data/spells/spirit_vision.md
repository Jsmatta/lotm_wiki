## Overview

**Spirit Vision (The Mystical Eye / Ethereal Perception)** is a foundational occult sensory technique utilized by Beyonders across all 22 pathways (and native to the **Seer**, **Mystery Pryer**, **Sleepless**, and **Death** pathways). By synchronizing one's spiritual awareness with the optical nerves through cogitation, Spirit Vision allows the user to see beyond physical matter into the **Ether Body**, emotional auras, spiritual residues, and the unseen inhabitants of the **Spirit World**.

## Activation & Practical Mechanics

- **Activation Triggers**: Taught to Klein Moretti by **Old Neil**; standard activation involves entering a light meditative trance and tapping the glabella (forehead) twice, blinking rapidly, or squeezing the bridge of the nose.
- **Ether Body Analysis**: Observing the translucent spiritual shell wrapping physical bodies to diagnose physical health, fatigue, Beyonder potion integration, and spiritual wounds.

## Aura Color Interpretation

Spirit Vision translates psychological, spiritual, and emotional states into vibrant chromatic layers:
- **Red / Crimson**: Excitement, anger, passionate intent, or active combat aggression.
- **Deep Blue / Indigo**: Calm rationality, deep thought, analytical concentration.
- **White / Silver**: Pure spirituality, holy devotion, or peaceful tranquility.
- **Yellow / Orange**: Warmth, optimism, or cautious deliberation.
- **Murky Gray / Black / Brown**: Sickness, deep emotional sorrow, impending physical death, or supernatural corruption.
- **Purple**: Mystical enlightenment, spiritual ascension, or contact with the divine.

## Mystical Dangers & Blindness Hazards

Looking at high-sequence entities, evil god statues, or corrupted relics with Spirit Vision can instantly overwhelm mortal sensory channels, causing spontaneous eye combustion, psychic hemorrhage, severe mental collapse, or permanent blindness. Klein frequently uses Sefirah Castle as a protective filter when conducting high-tier spiritual observations.
