## Overview

**Spirit Vision** is a foundational mystical perception technique accessible to practitioners of the **Seer Pathway**, **Mystery Pryer Pathway**, **Sleepless Pathway**, and several other occult traditions. By shifting one's spiritual perception toward the etheric and astral planes, a Beyonder transcends ordinary ocular limitations to perceive the invisible spiritual currents that envelop reality.

In its initial form, activating Spirit Vision requires minor ritual catalysts or physical triggers—such as Klein Moretti tapping his left glabella (the spot between the eyebrows) twice, or using a drop of pure dew or herbal extract.

## Perceptual Layers & Aura Colors

Spirit Vision reveals the multi-layered spiritual anatomy of living organisms and mystical entities:
1. **Ether Body (Vitality Layer)**: Outlines the physical vitality, health status, organ integrity, and lifespan of a biological being.
2. **Astral Projection (Emotional & Mental Layer)**: Displays fluid, shifting colors reflecting inner psychology, mood, and mental stability:
   - **Red**: Passion, rage, aggression, or combat excitement.
   - **Blue**: Calmness, contemplation, logical calculation, or sorrow.
   - **Yellow / Orange**: Warmth, optimism, or deceit.
   - **Green**: Relief, peace, or physical healing.
   - **Purple / Violet**: Spirituality, mystical focus, intuition, or occult resonance.
   - **Black / Dark Gray**: Illness, terminal physical degeneration, or deathly curses.
3. **Spiritual Body**: The true metaphysical core of a conscious being, visible in the spirit world or as lingering spectral remnants after physical death.

## Volume 1: The Glabella Tap in Tingen

Klein Moretti learns Spirit Vision under the instruction of Dunn Smith and Old Neil at the Blackthorn Security Company. It becomes Klein's primary investigative tool to identify Beyonders, detect hostile malicious spirits, inspect magical ingredients, and examine tarot reading clients at the Divination Club.
