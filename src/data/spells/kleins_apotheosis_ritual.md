## Overview

**Klein's Apotheosis Ritual (The Fool's Ascension Ritual)** is the transcendent divine ceremony through which **Klein Moretti (The World / Gehrman Sparrow)** accommodated the **Fool Pathway Uniqueness**, consumed the remaining **Sequence 1: Attendant of Mysteries** characteristics, and ascended to the true divine throne of **Sequence 0: The Fool**.

Conducted amidst the apocalyptic crisis of the Fifth Epoch and fiercely contested by the Blasphemer **Amon**, the ritual required Klein to master the cosmic conceptual authority of **"Fooling"** across time, space, history, and destiny.

## The Fool Sequence 0 Ritual Requirements

To ascend to Sequence 0 of the Seer Pathway, the candidate must accomplish an unfathomable conceptual feat:
1. **Fooling History**: Creating a profound historical deception or temporal paradox where history itself is deceived into recording an event contrary to normal cause-and-effect.
2. **Accommodating the Uniqueness**: Subduing and assimilating the living conceptual Uniqueness of the Fool pathway (held within the slumbering monster Antigonus atop the Hornacis mountain peak).
3. **Consuming the Potion**: Ingesting the Sequence 0 potion containing the Uniqueness and all three Sequence 1 Attendant of Mysteries characteristics while resisting the mental awakening of the **Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth**.

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & The Hornacis Ascent

Klein journeys to the Hornacis mountain peak. With the assistance of the **Evernight Goddess**, Klein uses water from the River of Eternal Darkness to pacify the slumbering Demonic Wolf Antigonus, extracting the Fool Uniqueness and performing the miracle of *Fooling History* to complete his apotheosis.

:::

## Volume 8: The Battle Above the Gray Fog

Amon invades Sefirah Castle as a dual-pathway True God (Door & Error) to assassinate Klein during his vulnerable post-apotheosis integration. 

Klein executes his ultimate gamble: willingly allowing the **Celestial Worthy's** consciousness to awaken within his body. Driven by the fear of mutual destruction, Amon flees into the cosmos, leaving Klein Moretti as the undisputed **Lord of the Mysteries**.
