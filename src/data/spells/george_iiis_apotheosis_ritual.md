## Overview

**George III's Apotheosis Ritual** was the colossal, forbidden divine ceremony secretly prepared over decades by **King George Augustus III** of the Loen Kingdom to accommodate the **Black Emperor Uniqueness** and ascend to **Sequence 0: Black Emperor**. 

Involving clandestine cooperation between the **Loen Royal Family**, the **Demoness Sect**, the **Aurora Order**, and the **Twilight Hermit Order (Adam)**, the ritual stands as the primary driving conspiracy behind the **Great Smog of Backlund** and the outbreak of the **World War**.

## Black Emperor Apotheosis Requirements

Ascending to Sequence 0 of the Lawyer Pathway demands absolute mastery over legal distortion, social order, and monumental architecture:
1. **The Nation's Legal Order**: The candidate must bind their personal authority into the constitutional and legal bedrock of an empire, altering and distorting national laws to reflect their absolute will.
2. **Nine Secret Mausoleums**: Construction of **nine pyramid-shaped mausoleums** across the empire's territory, designed with strict historical and mystical geometries to anchor the candidate's divine conceptual existence.
3. **National Reverence & Proclamation**: Holding a grand ceremony where the entire population looks upon the ruler with awe, submission, and imperial fervor.
4. **The Potion Ingestion**: Drinking the Sequence 0 potion inside the central mausoleum amidst the culmination of national order and chaos.

## Volume 5: Red Priest & The Collapse of the Nine Mausoleums

King George III attempts to complete the ritual during the patriotic military parade in Backlund while world war engulfs the Northern Continent.

Klein Moretti, Miss Judgment (Xio), Miss Justice (Audrey), Trissy, and Queen Mystic Bernadette launch a coordinated strike on the secret mausoleums. Trissy detonates the hidden Demoness mirror wards, destroying the mausoleum anchors.

With the nine-pyramid matrix severed, George III's ritual collapses into massive divine backlash, stripping his Black Emperor aura and allowing Klein Moretti to deliver the fatal blow.
