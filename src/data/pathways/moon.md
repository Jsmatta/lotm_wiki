## Overview

The **Moon Pathway** (historically called the **Apothecary Pathway**) governs the primordial cosmic concepts of **Moon**, **Night**, **Life Essence**, **Medicine**, **Beasts**, and **Feminine Mystery**. Deeply connected to the ancient **Sanguine (Vampire)** race and the divine sphere of the **Earth Mother (Lilith)**, this pathway balances compassionate medical alchemy with deadly lunar curses.

Along with the **Mother Pathway (Planter)**, it forms the interchangeable branches of the Great Old One **Goddess of Origin (Mother of All Living)**, connected to the Sefirah **Brood Hive**.
