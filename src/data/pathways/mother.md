## Overview

The **Mother Pathway** (originally termed the **Planter Pathway**) commands divine authority over **Life**, **Creation**, **Harvest**, **Nature**, **Biological Hybridization**, and **Fertility**. Originating in the Southern Continent and preserved within the **Church of the Earth Mother**, this pathway nurtures living organisms while possessing terrifying biological manipulation capabilities.

Along with the **Moon Pathway (Apothecary)**, it forms the interchangeable branches of the Great Old One **Goddess of Origin (Mother of All Living)**, connected to the Sefirah **Brood Hive**.
