## Overview

The **Chained Pathway** (also known as the **Prisoner Pathway** or **Mutant Pathway**) commands divine authority over **Restraint**, **Desire**, **Spirits**, **Zombies**, **Curses**, and **Malice**. Originating from the ancient Highlen Kingdom of the Southern Continent and the worship of the **Chained God**, this pathway represents the existential tension between absolute ascetic temperance and uncontrollable primal depravity.

Along with the **Abyss Pathway (Devil Pathway)**, it belongs to the Great Old One group under the **Mother Tree of Desire (Father of Devils)**.
