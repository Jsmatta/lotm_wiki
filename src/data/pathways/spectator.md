## Overview

The **Spectator Pathway** (also known as the **Visionary Pathway**) commands absolute divine authority over the **Mind**, **Subconscious**, **Imagination**, **Envisioning Reality**, and the **Collective Unconscious**. Originating from the ancient Dragon race and Hermes's ancient teachings, this pathway treats human thoughts, desires, and civilizations as a grand theater production.

Along with the **Sun**, **Sailor**, **Reader (White Tower)**, and **Secrets Supplicant (Hanged Man)** pathways, it forms the group that converges into the supreme pillar **God Almighty (Creator, Lord of the Astral World)**.
