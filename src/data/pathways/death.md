## Overview

The **Death Pathway** (also known as the **Corpse Collector Pathway**) commands absolute divine authority over **Death**, **The Underworld**, **Spirits**, **Necromancy**, and **Silent Eternal Rest**. Originating in the ancient Balam Empire of the Southern Continent under the Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace and later the Fourth Epoch's Death (Salinger), this pathway bridges the boundary between the living and the dead.

Along with the **Darkness Pathway (Sleepless)** and the **Twilight Giant Pathway (Warrior)**, it forms the interchangeable branches of the Great Old One **Eternal Darkness (Lord of the River of Eternal Darkness)**.

## Sequence Progression

### Sequence 9: Corpse Collector
- **Abilities**: Natural resistance to cold, decay, and corpse toxins; intuitive spiritual awareness of deceased remains; the ability to identify causes of death through post-mortem spiritual inspection.

### Sequence 8: Gravedigger
- **Abilities**: Superhuman strength, agility, stamina, and heightened resistance to necrotic energies.

### Sequence 7: Spirit Guide
- **Abilities**: Communicating directly with newly deceased souls, pacifying lingering evil spirits, and guiding wandering ghosts into the underworld.

### Sequence 6: Spirit Medium
- **Abilities**: Channeling deceased entities through one's own spiritual body, conducting spirit medium rituals, and manipulating spiritual threads.

### Sequence 5: Gatekeeper
- **Abilities**: Guarding the threshold to the Underworld, sealing and summoning evil spirits, and dragging hostile souls directly into nether domains.
- **Historical Bearer**: **Ince Zangwill** walked this sequence before illegally transitioning to Sequence 4 Iron-Blooded Knight.
