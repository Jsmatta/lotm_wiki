## Overview

The **Sun Pathway** (originally called the **Bard Pathway**) governs absolute divine authority over **Solar Radiance**, **Light**, **Purification**, **Truth**, **Courage**, and **Sanctification**. It is the ultimate natural counter to the undead, abyssal devils, evil spirits, and corruption from the dark cosmos.

Along with the **Sailor Pathway**, **Reader Pathway**, **Spectator Pathway**, and **Secrets Supplicant Pathway (Hanged Man)**, it forms one of the five core branches under the supreme pillar **God Almighty (Creator of the Astral World)**.
