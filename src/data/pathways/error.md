## Overview

The **Error Pathway** (also known as the **Marauder Pathway**) governs **Theft**, **Loopholes**, **Deceit**, **Parasitism**, and **Time Manipulation**. Along with the **Seer** and **Door** pathways, it is one of the three core pathways that converge into the supreme Great Old One **Lord of the Mysteries (Beacon of Destiny, King of Space-Time)**.

Beyonders of this pathway are master deceivers who treat the laws of physics, mysticism, and fate as software filled with exploitable glitches and vulnerabilities.
