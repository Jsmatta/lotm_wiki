## Overview

The **Hunter Pathway** (also known as the **Red Priest Pathway**) commands divine authority over **War**, **Provocation**, **Flames**, **Conquest**, and **Iron Blood**. Historically embodied by the ancient Einhorn family of Feysac, the Sauron family of Intis, and the legendary King of Angels **Medici**, this pathway thrives on battlefield slaughter, tactical cunning, and inciting furious conflicts.

Along with the **Demoness Pathway (Assassin)**, it forms the interchangeable branches of the Great Old One **Calamity of Destruction (City of Calamity)**.

## Sequence Progression
