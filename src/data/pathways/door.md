## Overview

The **Door Pathway** (also known as the **Apprentice Pathway**) commands absolute divine authority over **Space**, **Dimensions**, **Seals**, **Concealment**, and **Cosmic Travel**. Originating from the ancient Abraham family—one of the foundational noble bloodlines of the Tudor Dynasty—this pathway allows Beyonders to record abilities from all 22 pathways and freely traverse physical and astral barriers.

Along with the **Seer Pathway** and **Error Pathway**, it is one of the three interchangeable pathways under the supreme Great Old One group **Lord of the Mysteries (King of Space-Time, Beacon of Destiny)**.
