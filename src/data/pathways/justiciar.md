## Overview

The **Justiciar Pathway** (traditionally known as the **Arbiter Pathway**) commands absolute divine authority over **Order**, **Law**, **Rules**, **Punishment**, and **Prohibition**. Originating in the ancient Solomon Empire and later preserved within the royal families of the Loen Kingdom (Castiya family) and the Feysac Empire (Einhorn family), this pathway ensures the enforcement of structured authority and absolute civil discipline.

Along with the **Black Emperor Pathway (Lawyer)**, it forms the interchangeable branches under the Great Old One **The Anarchy (Nation of Disorder)**.
