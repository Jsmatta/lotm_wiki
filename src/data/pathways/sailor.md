## Overview

The **Sailor Pathway** (also known as the **Tyrant Pathway**) commands absolute divine authority over **The Sea**, **Ocean Storms**, **Thunder**, **Lightning**, **Wind**, and **Rage**. Rooted in the **Church of the Lord of Storms**, this pathway cultivates ferocious physical warriors who dominate maritime battlefields with terrifying elemental fury.

Along with the **Sun**, **Spectator**, **Reader**, and **Secrets Supplicant (Hanged Man)** pathways, it forms the interchangeable branches of the supreme Great Old One **God Almighty (Creator of the Astral World)**.

## Sequence Progression

### Sequence 9: Sailor
- **Abilities**: Exceptional underwater mobility, acute sense of nautical orientation, enhanced strength and endurance, and comfort in rough seas.

### Sequence 8: Folk of Rage
- **Abilities**: Channelling anger to drastically enhance physical strike power, intimidating adversaries, and resisting mental suppression.
- **Iconic User**: **Alger Wilson (The Hanged Man)**, who begins as a Sequence 8 captain of the Church of the Lord of Storms before ascending through the Tarot Club.
