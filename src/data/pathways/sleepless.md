## Overview

The **Sleepless Pathway** (also known as the **Darkness Pathway**) commands supreme authority over **Darkness**, **Night**, **Dreams**, **Concealment**, **Silence**, **Misfortune**, and **Eternal Repose**. Maintained as the holy pathway of the **Church of the Evernight Goddess**, it produces the formidable **Nighthawks** and elite **Red Gloves** strike squads who safeguard human society from occult horrors.

Along with the **Death Pathway** and **Twilight Giant Pathway**, it forms the group that converges into the Great Old One **Eternal Darkness (Calamity of Destruction, Origin of Evil)**.
