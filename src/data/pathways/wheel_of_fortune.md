## Overview

The **Wheel of Fortune Pathway** (historically called the **Monster Pathway**) governs absolute divine authority over **Fate**, **Destiny**, **Probability**, **Luck**, **Calamity**, and **Cycle**. Isolated from all other pathways, it has **no neighboring pathways**, forming a self-contained single-pathway Great Old One: the **Key of Light (Endless Wheel)**.

Practitioners perceive the invisible river of fate, shifting probabilities in their favor while navigating unpredictable cosmic misfortunes.
