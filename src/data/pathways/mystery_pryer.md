## Overview

The **Mystery Pryer Pathway** (often called the **Hermit Pathway**) commands divine authority over **Mystical Knowledge**, **Occult Philosophy**, **Information Manipulation**, **Fairy Tales**, and **Astrology**. Highly secretive, this pathway believes that "Knowledge is Power," yet warns that uncontrolled insight leads to instantaneous madness from the cosmic void.

Along with the **Savant Pathway (Paragon)**, it forms the interchangeable group under the Great Old One **Demon of Knowledge (Knowledge Moor)**.

## Sequence Progression

### Sequence 9: Mystery Pryer
- **Abilities**: Natural affinity for occult rituals, spiritual intuition for hidden patterns, deciphering ancient texts (Hermetic, Jotun, Dragonese), and enhanced magical perception.

### Sequence 8: Melee Scholar
- **Abilities**: Balancing mystical intellect with physical martial prowess, calculating combat angles, and utilizing enchanted close-quarters weapons.
