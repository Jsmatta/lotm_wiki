## Overview

The **Church of the Lord of Storms** is one of the oldest orthodox churches on the Northern Continent, holding supreme religious dominion over the navies, maritime trade routes, harbors, and island colonies of the **Loen Kingdom**, **Rorsted Archipelago**, and the **Five Seas**. 

Worshipping the **Lord of Storms (Leodero)**, the church embodies the raw, wrathful elemental fury of **Thunder**, **Lightning**, **Wind**, **Tsunamis**, and **Naval Conquest**.

## Armed Forces: The Mandated Punishers

The official Beyonder enforcement apparatus of the Church of Storms is known as the **Mandated Punishers** (also called the *Storm Inquisitors*):
- **Specialization**: Composed almost exclusively of Beyonders of the **Sailor Pathway (Tyrant Pathway)**.
- **Combat Doctrine**: Aggressive, direct, and overwhelmingly lethal. Mandated Punishers favor overwhelming thunder and lightning strikes, aquatic ambushes, and close-quarters explosive brutality over subtle investigation.
- **Key Figures**:
  - **Archbishop Jahn Kottman**: High-sequence demigod overseeing maritime defenses in Bayam and the Sonia Sea.
  - **Alger Wilson**: Captain of the *Blue Avenger* and covert Tarot Club member **The Hanged Man**.

## Holy Relics & Emblems

- **Sacred Emblem**: An intricate sigil composed of roaring lightning arcs striking across foaming storm waves.
- **Holy Scripture**: *The Book of Storms*.
- **Sacred Artifacts**: The ancient *Cataclysmic Trident* and the *Scepter of the Sea God* (reclaimed from Kalvetua).
