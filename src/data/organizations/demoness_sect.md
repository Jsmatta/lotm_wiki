## Overview

The **Demoness Sect** is an ancient, sinister matriarchal occult organization dedicated to the worship of the **Primordial Demoness (Cheek)**. Rooted in the **Demoness Pathway (Assassin Pathway)**, the sect specializes in assassination, biological curses, pestilence, mirror divination, black magic, and sexual manipulation.

One of the sect's most notorious aspects is that male Beyonders who consume the **Sequence 7: Witch** potion undergo an involuntary, total biological sex change into extraordinarily beautiful women.

## Leadership & Hierarchy

- **Supreme Deity**: The Primordial Demoness (Sequence 0: Demoness / Cheek).
- **High Leadership**: The Council of High-Sequence Demonesses, led by Saintesses such as **Katarina Pelle (Saintess of White)** and **Tracy (Vice Admiral Ailment)**.
- **Notable Members**: **Trissy Cheek** (former assassin turned witch involved in the Backlund royal conspiracies).

The Demoness Sect collaborates with Prince Edessak and the royal faction to unleash biological plague cauldrons across East Borough. The resulting plague merges with the True Creator's sacrificial smog, causing the horrific **Great Smog of Backlund** that kills tens of thousands of civilians.
