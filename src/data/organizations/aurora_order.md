## Overview

The **Aurora Order** is an infamous, fanatical occult secret organization dedicated to the worship of the **True Creator (The Fallen Creator / The Hanged Man)**. Believing that the True Creator is the original omnipotent Creator who was betrayed and dismembered by the orthodox deities, the order seeks to bring about his physical descent into the material world to cleanse humanity through holy suffering and apocalyptic rebirth.

Operating across the Northern and Southern Continents, the Aurora Order is universally designated by all orthodox churches and police agencies as an extremely dangerous, radical terrorist organization.

## Hierarchy & Divine Structure

- **Supreme Deity**: The True Creator (commanding the **Secrets Supplicant / Hanged Man Pathway** and the **Shadow / Giant** authorities).
- **Leadership**: Led by the **Five Angels of the Cross** and the **Twenty-Two Oracles**, who oversee regional gathering points, secret sacrificial dens, and assassination cells.
- **Key Oracles & Saints**:
  - **Mr. A**: The powerful Oracle of Backlund (Sequence 5 Shepherd), notorious for gathering nobles and funding occult conspiracies.
  - **Mr. X (Lewis Wien)**: Backlund organizer who coordinated with the royal family and Ince Zangwill.
  - **Saint of Secrets (Botis)**: High-ranking demigod who later stole the Abraham Family's relics.

## Modus Operandi & Blood Sacrifices

Aurora Order cultists believe that the road to divine salvation lies through suffering, pain, and listening to the holy whispers of the True Creator. Their rituals frequently involve large-scale blood sacrifices, planting flesh-bombs, kidnapping children, and engineering biological mutations.

## Volume 1: Tragedy in Tingen

The Aurora Order's rogue investigator **Lanevus** partners with the traitor **Ince Zangwill**, orchestrating the descent of the fetus of the True Creator in Tingen City using the power of Megose and the Antigonus Notebook, leading to the destruction of the Tingen Nighthawks.

:::

:::spoiler volume=2

## Volume 2: The Backlund Descent & The Great Smog

The Aurora Order coordinates with the Demoness Sect and royal conspirators in Backlund. Mr. A prepares the grand sacrificial descent of the True Creator during the **Great Smog of Backlund**, nearly destroying the entire capital before being thwarted by Klein Moretti and the orthodox demigods.

:::

:::spoiler volume=4

## Volume 4: The Fall of the Oracles

Klein Moretti (as Dwayne Dantès and Gehrman Sparrow) systematically hunts down Aurora Order cells in Backlund, assassinating **Mr. X** and crippling the Order's regional influence.

:::

:::spoiler volume=6

## Volume 6: The Botis Purge

The Tarot Club conducts a unified global strike against **Saint of Secrets Botis**, executing him and recovering critical Door Pathway characteristics.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: Holy Fusion

Following the merge of the **True Creator** and **Adam (The Visionary)** to restore the complete divinity of the **Ancient Sun God**, the remaining factions of the Aurora Order are integrated into Adam's divine order to prepare for the cosmic apocalypse.
