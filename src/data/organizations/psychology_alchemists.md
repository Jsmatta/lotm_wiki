## Overview

The **Psychology Alchemists** is an esoteric, academic secret organization specializing in the study of human consciousness, psychiatry, the collective unconscious, and the **Spectator Pathway (Visionary Pathway)**. Founded in the late Fourth Epoch upon the recovered mystical manuscripts of the ancient sorcerer **Hermes**, the group recruits intellectuals, noble psychologists, doctors, and philosophers across the Northern Continent.

Beneath its polite, refined scholarly veneer, the organization's upper echelon is secretly controlled by ancient demigod **Mind Dragons** and the **Twilight Hermit Order** led by **Adam**.

## Structure & Council of Seven

- **Supreme Originator**: The ancient human angel **Hermes**.
- **Governance**: Governed by the secretive **Council of Seven (Board of Directors)**.
- **Notable Figures**:
  - **Hvin Rambis**: High-ranking council director (Sequence 3: Dream Weaver / ancient dragon lineage) embedded in the Loen royal court.
  - **Audrey Hall (Miss Justice)**: Recruited as an apprentice in Backlund who systematically uncovers the organization's royal conspiracies.
  - **Escalante**: Audrey's early instructor and liaison.

## Volume 2: The Faceless & Audrey's Infiltration

Audrey Hall joins the Backlund branch of the Psychology Alchemists. She receives Spectator, Telepathist, and Psychiatrist formulas, using her noble connections to analyze the organization's subtle psychological manipulation of Loen politicians.

:::spoiler volume=5

## Volume 5: Red Priest & The Fall of Hvin Rambis

Hvin Rambis attempts to place Audrey under high-sequence psychological cues to spy on the Hall family and Dwayne Dantès. Audrey coordinates with Klein Moretti (Gehrman Sparrow), who ambushes and executes Hvin Rambis in Backlund, liberating Audrey from council surveillance.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: Reorganization

Following Adam's divine fusion and the fall of the dragon elders, the Psychology Alchemists is restructured under the benign guidance of **Audrey Hall (The Dream Weaver)**, focusing on psychiatric healing and trauma relief for war survivors.
