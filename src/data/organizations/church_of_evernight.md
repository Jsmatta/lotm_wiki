## Overview

The **Church of the Evernight Goddess** is the largest and most influential orthodox religious institution in the **Loen Kingdom**. Worshipping the **Evernight Goddess (Amanises)**, the church governs the concepts of **Darkness**, **Concealment**, **Silence**, **Repose**, **Misfortune**, and **Horror**. 

Headquartered at the grand **Saint Hieropolis Cathedral**, the church operates thousands of cathedrals, orphanages, charity kitchens, and hospitals across the continent, providing solace to the working classes while operating a ruthless clandestine apparatus to contain occult corruption.

## Clerical & Mystical Hierarchy

- **Supreme Deity**: The Evernight Goddess (Sequence 0: Darkness).
- **Executive Leadership**: The **Holy See** and the Council of Archbishops, led by the Pope in Winter County.
- **Official Armed Divisions**:
  - **The Nighthawks**: Regional undercover Beyonder squads operating under commercial fronts (e.g. *Blackthorn Security Company* in Tingen) tasked with supernatural investigations and Chanis Gate containment.
  - **The Red Gloves**: High-mobility, elite combat squads composed of mid-to-high sequence Sleepless Beyonders dispatched across the kingdom for high-priority containment breaches and heretic extermination.
  - **Goddess's Handmaidens / Silent Maidens**: Secret cloistered demigods dedicated to extreme concealment rituals.

## Sacred Symbols & Holy Relics

- **Holy Emblem**: A crimson crescent moon enclosed within radiant starlight against a pitch-black circular field.
- **Holy Scripture**: *The Revelation of Evernight*.
- **Grade 0 Sealed Relics**: *0-17: The Curtain of Concealment*, *0-08: Quill of Alzuhod* (stolen by Ince Zangwill), and *Saint Selena's Ashes*.

## Volume 1: Tragedy in Tingen

The Tingen diocese, headquartered at **Saint Selena Cathedral**, manages local occult containment. Following Ince Zangwill's treason and the theft of *Saint Selena's Ashes*, the church initiates a continent-wide red alert.

:::

:::spoiler volume=2

## Volume 2: The Great Smog of Backlund

Archbishop Anthony Stevenson and the Backlund Nighthawks deploy Grade 1 and Grade 0 artifacts beneath **Saint Samuel Cathedral** to suppress the True Creator's descent during the Great Smog.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & The Divine Coalition

The Church of Evernight allies with the Church of Earth Mother during the Battle of Gods. Following the slaying of the God of Combat, the church annexes the Feysac Empire's religious infrastructure, securing supreme continental authority.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Great Old One Alliance

The Church enters a sacred pact with the emerging **Church of the Fool**, establishing joint protective wards around major cities to defend Earth against the Outer Deities.
