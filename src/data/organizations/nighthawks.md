## Overview

The **Nighthawks** are the official undercover Beyonder enforcement division of the **Church of the Evernight Goddess**. Formed to protect ordinary human society from occult horrors, heretical cults, evil spirits, and Beyonders who have lost control, the Nighthawks operate in every major city across the **Loen Kingdom**.

Their solemn motto embodies their eternal sacrifice:
> *"We are guardians, but also a bunch of miserable wretches that are constantly fighting against threats and madness."*

## Squad Structure & Daily Operations

- **City Squads**: Stationed under commercial fronts (e.g. *Blackthorn Security Company* in Tingen) to maintain civilian cover and collaborate with the municipal police.
- **Chanis Gate Keeper**: Each headquarters guards an underground **Chanis Gate**, a holy subterranean containment vault lined with Saint Selena's holy relics to seal Grade 1, 2, and 3 mystical artifacts.
- **Pathway Composition**: Primarily composed of the **Sleepless Pathway (Darkness)**, supplemented by Corpse Collectors (Death Pathway), Mystery Pryers, and Diviners.

## The Red Gloves

The **Red Gloves** are the elite, high-mobility rapid reaction strike force of the Nighthawks:
- Dressed in formal black coats with distinctive red gloves on their left hands (symbolizing the blood of fallen comrades).
- Dispatched across provinces to hunt high-tier heretics, investigate catastrophic containment breaches, and neutralize rogue demigods.
- Notable Commander: **Leonard Mitchell (The Star)**.

## Volume 1: The Tragedy of Tingen

The Tingen Nighthawk squad under Captain **Dunn Smith** initiates Klein Moretti into the mystical world. The squad uncovers Ince Zangwill's conspiracy involving the Antigonus Notebook. In the final battle against Megose, Captain Dunn Smith and Klein Moretti sacrifice their lives to save Tingen.

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past & The Revenge in the South

Leonard Mitchell leads his Red Gloves squad to the Southern Continent, cooperating with Dwayne Dantès (Klein Moretti) and Daly Simone to corner and execute the traitor **Ince Zangwill**, avenging Dunn Smith and the fallen Tingen Nighthawks.

Following the World War and the ascension of The Fool, the Nighthawks coordinate with the Church of the Fool's enforcers to maintain the global cordon against cosmic madness.
