## Overview

The **Church of the Earth Mother** is an orthodox religious body predominantly rooted in the **Feynapotter Kingdom** and southern agricultural territories. Worshipping the **Earth Mother**, the church governs **Life**, **Nature**, **Harvest**, **Fertility**, and the **Mother & Moon Pathways**.

While presenting itself to mortal society as a gentle, pastoral church dedicated to peace and agricultural prosperity, its upper echelon conceals one of the most profound divine secrets of the Third, Fourth, and Fifth Epochs.

## The Divine Truth: The Sanguine Ancestor Lilith

During the Cataclysm of the Third Epoch, the ancient Sanguine Ancestor **Lilith (The Beauty Goddess)** was believed to have perished. In reality, with the covert assistance of the **Evernight Goddess (Amanises)**, Lilith assumed the vacant identity of the **Earth Mother**, covertly guiding both human agriculturalists and the scattered **Sanguine (Vampire)** clans throughout history.

## Holy Pathways & Official Beyonders

- **The Mother Pathway (Planter / Apothecary)**: Wielding absolute biological proliferation, herbal healing, and mutated agricultural creation.
- **The Moon Pathway (Vampire / High Summoner)**: Commanding darkness, blood magic, spiritual summonings, and nocturnal regeneration.
- **Key Clerics & Allies**:
  - **Father Utravsky**: Former gladiator turned devout priest at the Harvest Church in Backlund, guiding Emlyn White toward spiritual redemption.
  - **Emlyn White (The Moon)**: Sanguine noble recruited into the Tarot Club who becomes the key liaison between the Sanguine Council and the Earth Mother.
