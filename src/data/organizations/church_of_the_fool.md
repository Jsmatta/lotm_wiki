## Overview

The **Church of the Fool** is the religious organization established in the mortal realm to worship **The Fool (Klein Moretti)**. Emerging initially from the maritime coastal prayers of the Rorsted Archipelago and the Sea God believers, it coalesced into a formal divine institution following the liberation of the **City of Silver** and **Moon City** from the Forsaken Land of the Gods.

Headquartered at the grand **Cathedral of the Fool** in **Bayam**, the church preaches salvation, miracles, protection against cosmic madness, and the reclamation of lost light.

## Holy Hierarchy & Clergy

- **Supreme Deity**: **The Fool** (Sequence 0: The Fool / Lord of the Mysteries).
- **The Blessed One / The World**: **Gehrman Sparrow**, revered as The Fool's chief holy executioner and sword of judgment.
- **Council of Elders & High Priests**:
  - **Derrick Berg (The Sun)**: Chief Elder representing the City of Silver, serving as the high priest of solar purification.
  - **Nim (Moon City Elder)**: Guiding the purified residents of Moon City.
  - **Alger Wilson (The Hanged Man)**: Holy Commander overseeing the church's maritime missionary fleets.
  - **Danitz (The Blazing)**: Appointed as the public **Oracle of The Fool**, managing daily administrative diplomacy and relations with foreign powers.

## Sacred Scripture & Miracles

- **Holy Emblem**: An intricate occult symbol combining the **Pupil-less Eye** (symbolizing omniscient insight) and the **Contorted Lines of Fog** (symbolizing history and destiny).
- **Holy Canon**: *The Book of the Fool* (documenting the 300 Wishes granted by Merlin Hermes and the miraculous exodus of the City of Silver).

## Volume 6: The Great Exodus of the City of Silver

Klein Moretti summons the holy door at the Giant King's Court, leading the thousands of survivors of the City of Silver out of the eternal darkness. The citizens consecrate their eternal devotion to Mr. Fool.

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & Public Consecration

The Church of the Fool is officially recognized as an orthodox religion across the Rorsted Archipelago and the Southern Continent. Miracle Invoker Merlin Hermes travels the Northern Continent granting wishes to commoners, cementing the deity's compassionate reputation.
