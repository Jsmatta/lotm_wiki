## Overview

The **Loen Royal Family** (the **House of Augustus**) is the imperial ruling dynasty of the **Loen Kingdom**, one of the foremost industrial and colonial empires of the Fifth Epoch. Tracing their lineage back to the Fourth Epoch's **Trunsoest United Empire**, the Augustus bloodline controls the **Justiciar Pathway (Arbiter)** and elements of the **Black Emperor Pathway (Lawyer)**.

While publicly presenting themselves as constitutional monarchs governing alongside the House of Lords and House of Commons, the royal family operates an immense covert intelligence network (MI9), aristocratic cartels, and secret mausoleum complexes.

## Key Royal Figures

- **King George Augustus III**: The reigning monarch who covertly plots to become Sequence 0: The Black Emperor.
- **Prince Edessak**: Tragic prince manipulated by the Demoness Sect during the Great Smog.
- **William Augustus I**: Ancient family patriarch and Fourth Epoch angel who protects the crown.
- **Prince Grove** and **Viscount Stratford**.

## Volume 1 & Volume 2: Imperial Expansion & The Great Smog

The Crown directs colonial conquests across the Rorsted Archipelago and East Balam. In Backlund, the royal family secretly permits human trafficking rings (Capim) and biological cauldron testing to harvest thousands of souls from East Borough, leading to the **Great Smog of Backlund**.

:::

:::spoiler volume=5

## Volume 5: Red Priest & The Black Emperor Conspiracy

Klein Moretti, Miss Justice, and Miss Judgment uncover King George III's monstrous treason: to fulfill the ritual of **Sequence 0: Black Emperor**, George III secretly partnered with the Demoness Sect and the Aurora Order, enslaving tens of thousands of workers in subterranean secret mausoleums across Backlund.

Klein, Miss Justice, and high-ranking demigods execute a daring raid during George III's public national address, detonating his central mausoleum and assassinating George III, preventing his apotheosis.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Aftermath & The New Era

Following the World War and George III's death, Prince Grove ascends the throne, and the Augustus family signs strict peace and anti-corruption treaties with the Church of Evernight and the Church of the Fool.
