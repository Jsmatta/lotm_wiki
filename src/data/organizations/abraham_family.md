## Overview

The **Abraham Family** is one of the most storied and tragic ancient noble bloodlines in the mystical world. Originating in the Fourth Epoch as one of the founding pillars of the **Tudor Dynasty (The Blood Emperor)**, the family holds hereditary mastery over the **Door Pathway (Apprentice Pathway)**. 

During their zenith, the family commanded multiple high-sequence demigods and angels, led by their legendary patriarch **Bethel Abraham (Mr. Door)**. However, following the War of the Four Emperors and the sealing of Bethel Abraham in the outer cosmos, the family suffered a catastrophic millennium-long decline.

## The Blood Moon Curse

For centuries, every direct descendant of the Abraham Family who attempts to advance in the Apprentice Pathway is afflicted by horrifying psychic ravings during the **Blood Moon**. The ravings originate from Bethel Abraham's desperate attempts to return to Earth from the cosmos, contaminated by the Outer Deity **Mother Goddess of Depravity**. 

Unaware of the source, many family members lost control, perished, or transformed into aberrant monsters, forcing the surviving elders to strictly ration potion formulas and scatter the family into isolated branches.

## Key Figures

- **Bethel Abraham (Mr. Door)**: The supreme King of Angels of the Door Pathway, wandering the outer starry sky.
- **Dorian Gray Abraham**: A gentle music teacher residing in Pritz Harbor who serves as the keeper of the family's remaining artifacts and records.
- **Fors Wall**: Adopted apprentice of the Abraham lineage who receives their formulas and mystical relics in exchange for providing them with Sefirah Castle's purification amulets.

## Volume 2: The Faceless & Backlund Connections

Fors Wall establishes contact with the Abraham family branch in Backlund. Suffering from the full moon curses herself, she discovers that Mr. Fool's divine aura above the gray fog completely isolates and purifies the cosmic ravings, providing the first safe method of survival for the pathway in centuries.

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past

Fors introduces Dorian Gray Abraham to the blessing of Mr. Fool. In gratitude, Dorian provides formulas for **Sequence 6: Scribe** and **Sequence 5: Traveler**, along with the legendary magical tome **Leymano's Travels**.

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & The Fall of Mr. Door

Bethel Abraham is manipulated by the Mother Goddess of Depravity and returns to Earth's astral barrier. Recognizing his profound cosmic corruption, Bethel sacrifices himself to protect humanity, allowing Amon to steal his characteristics and Uniqueness. Before his death, Bethel bestows his final blessing and legacy upon the Abraham family and Fors Wall.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: Renaissance of the Lineage

Following the Apotheosis of The Fool, the Abraham Family officially aligns with the **Church of the Fool**. With the Blood Moon curse permanently lifted, the family begins its grand revival under the leadership of **Fors Wall (The Magician)** and Dorian Gray Abraham.
