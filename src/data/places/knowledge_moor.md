## Overview

The **Knowledge Moor (The Infinite Archive / The Sea of All Knowing)** is the supreme **Sefirah** governing the **Hermit (Mystery Pryer)** and **Paragon (Savant/Mystery Craftsman)** pathways. Sealed deep within the celestial barrier of the **Western Continent**, the Knowledge Moor manifests as an endless, misty marshland of floating parchment scrolls, glowing mathematical equations, gears of infinite logic, and crystallization of all physical and occult laws.

Embodying the Great Old One authority of **Demon of Knowledge (The Omniscient Archive / The Eye of Wisdom)**, the Knowledge Moor is the ultimate objective of the **Hidden Sage**, the **God of Steam and Machinery**, and high-sequence mystics across the world.
