## Overview

**Utopia (The Marionette City)** was an extraordinary, hyper-realistic constructed town created in the **Loen Kingdom's** coastal countryside by **Klein Moretti** to complete his **Sequence 1: Attendant of Mysteries Advancement Ritual**. 

Constructed using high-sequence Seer abilities, historical summoning, and spirit-body thread manipulation, every single "citizen" in Utopia—from the mayor, hotel clerks, police constables, bakers, and telegraph operators to stray dogs and street cats—was an individual **Marionette** simultaneously controlled by Klein's consciousness.

## The Attendant of Mysteries Ritual Requirements

To digest the Sequence 1 potion and advance to Attendant of Mysteries:
1. **Constructing a Secret Marionette Realm**: Establishing an entire city with distinct professions, daily routines, emotional conflicts, and municipal logistics where thousands of marionettes live lifelike lives without ordinary mortals realizing they are puppets.
2. **Connecting to the Fog of History**: Grafting the town into historical records and railway maps so that real travelers visit, interact, lodge, and depart without discovering the illusion.

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & The Grand Battle of Utopia

Real railway travelers, detectives, and merchants arrive in Utopia, experiencing the bizarre, perfectly timed hospitality of Klein's puppets. 

The Secret Order's leader **Zaratul**, **Amon**, and **Adam** notice the anomaly, turning Utopia into a multi-angel battlefield. Klein coordinates all his marionettes in a grand symphony of spirit threads and historical projections, outmaneuvering Zaratul and consuming the Sequence 1 characteristic to ascend to **Attendant of Mysteries**.
