## Overview

**Utopia** was a miraculous, living phantom city created along the coast of the Loen Kingdom by **Klein Moretti (acting as the wandering magician Merlin Hermes)**. Designed with meticulous urban detail—featuring bustling harbors, telegraph stations, bakeries, police departments, and cozy taverns—every single one of its thousands of residents was a **marionette** controlled by Klein's spiritual threads.

Utopia was engineered to fulfill the profound Sequence 1 ritual requirement: creating a cohesive, living city and fooling history before advancing to **Sequence 1: Attendant of Mysteries**.

:::reveal at=7

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & The Utopia Ritual

Klein breathes lifelike personality into Utopia, with marionettes playing out interconnected daily lives. However, the city is targeted by **Zaratul**, **Adam**, and the **Church of the Lord of Storms**, turning Utopia into the stage for an explosive demigod confrontation. Klein detonates the city's historical anchor, completing his ritual amidst the ruins.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Legend in History

In Volume 8, although physically eradicated, Utopia remains forever immortalized in the **Historical Void**. Klein can summon the entire city and its warm-hearted marionette citizens as historical projections above Sefirah Castle.

:::
