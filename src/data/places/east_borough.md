## Overview

**East Borough** is the notorious, overcrowded industrial slum district of **Backlund**, situated south of the Taspin River. Home to hundreds of thousands of impoverished factory laborers, dockworkers, washerwomen, and homeless families, East Borough is characterized by toxic coal smoke, ramshackle tenements, rampant disease, and extreme destitution.

Despite its crushing poverty, East Borough forms the moral heartbeat of Klein Moretti's journey, reinforcing his unyielding compassion for ordinary human suffering.

:::reveal at=2

## Volume 2: The Great Smog & Old Kohler

Detective Sherlock Moriarty (Klein) frequents East Borough, befriending the impoverished laborer **Old Kohler**. When the **Great Smog of Backlund** is unleashed, thousands of East Borough laborers choke to death on toxic fumes, including Old Kohler, cementing Klein's resolve to bring justice to the perpetrators.

:::

## Volume 4: Dwayne Dantès's Charitable Foundation

Returning as Dwayne Dantès, Klein establishes a massive charitable scholarship and relief foundation, funding clean water, soup kitchens, and educational opportunities for East Borough children.

:::

:::spoiler volume=5

## Volume 5: The War Relief

During the airship bombings of Backlund, Klein uses Life's Cane and street-level clown performances to heal wounded laborers and protect East Borough tenements from firestorms.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Clearing of the Skies

In Volume 8, with the implementation of post-war labor reforms and the guidance of the Church of the Fool, East Borough experiences genuine revitalization, with clean air and decent housing replacing the misery of the past.

:::
