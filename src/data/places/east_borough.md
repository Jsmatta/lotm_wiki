## Overview

**East Borough** is the most densely populated, destitute, and disease-ridden industrial slum district in **Backlund**, situated along the polluted eastern curve of the Taspin River. Home to hundreds of thousands of impoverished factory laborers, dock workers, seamstresses, homeless vagrants, and street orphans, East Borough represents the grim, unforgiving underbelly of the Loen Kingdom's industrial expansion.

Characterized by crumbling brick tenements, unpaved muddy alleys, overflowing gutters, sulfurous coal soot, and cheap black bread shops, East Borough provides the profound moral and emotional foundation for **Klein Moretti's** actions as both **Sherlock Moriarty** and **The Fool**.

## Life in the Slums & Key Locations

- **Working-Class Hardships**: Laborers working 16-hour shifts in textile mills and chemical factories for a few pennies a day, frequently unable to afford meat, clean coal, or medical treatment.
- **Bravehearts Bar (Iron Gate Street)**: An underground drinking den featuring illegal fist-fighting rings where Beyonders and mercenaries (including **Maric** and **Sharron**) discreetly gather.
- **Black Market Dens & Capim's Syndicate**: Human trafficking syndicates operating with impunity by kidnapping young working-class girls to fuel the noble slave market.

## Volume 2: The Faceless & The Great Smog

Sherlock Moriarty frequents East Borough, hiring **Old Kohler** as his street informant and investigating factory pollution and kidnappings. Klein executes the corrupt slave-trafficker Capim and his Beyonder guards, leaving the calling card of **The Fool**.

During the climax of Volume 2, the **Demoness Sect** detonates biological plague cauldrons across East Borough. The resulting **Great Smog of Backlund** chokes tens of thousands of defenseless laborers to death, including Old Kohler, searing the tragedy permanently into Klein's heart.
