## Overview

The **Mysterious Space Above the Gray Fog (Sefirah Castle / The Origin of Mysteries)** is the supreme **Sefirah** governing the **Fool (Seer)**, **Door (Apprentice)**, and **Error (Marauder)** pathways. Hovering infinitely above the Astral Realm and Spirit World, wrapped in an eternal sea of churning, grayish-white divine fog, Sefirah Castle is the foundational transcendent domain and source of power for **Lord of the Mysteries (The King of Space-Time / The Dominator of the Spirit World)**.

Throughout the series, Sefirah Castle serves as the impenetrable divine sanctuary where **Klein Moretti** hosts the weekly gatherings of the **Tarot Club**, conducts infallible divinations, purges lethal mental contaminations, and prepares for his cosmic apotheosis.

## Structure & Divine Features

- **The Grand Divine Palace**: A majestic, ancient stone hall supported by hundreds of towering Romanesque pillars, featuring a colossal mottled bronze table with twenty-two high-backed chairs carved with the Major Arcana symbols.
- **The Churning Gray Fog**: An ocean of divine consciousness that shields the castle from the perception and divination of True Gods, Outer Deities, and cosmic corruption.
- **The Transmigrator Cocoon Cluster**: Suspended beneath the dome of the gray fog, a cluster of translucent, glowing spiritual cocoons containing preserved souls from modern Earth (including Zhou Mingrui and Roselle Gustav), placed there in the Pre-Epoch by the **Celestial Worthy**.
- **The Crimson Stars**: Countless floating crimson points of light within the fog corresponding to the prayers, spiritual resonances, and souls of mortals across the universe.
