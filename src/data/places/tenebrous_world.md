## Overview

The **Tenebrous World (The Abyss of Corrupt Desires / The Shadow Depths)** is the supreme **Sefirah** governing the **Chained (Prisoner/Mutant)** and **Abyss (Criminal/Devil)** pathways, originally ripped away from the Outer Deity **Mother Tree of Desire (Father of Devils / Tree of Depravity)** during the Pre-Epoch.

Manifesting as a bottomless, pitch-black chasm of pulsating demonic flesh, rusted iron chains, sticky black tar, and endless screams of spiritual torment sealed beneath the Western Continent, the Tenebrous World embodies the Great Old One authority of **Father of Devils (The Heart of Darkness / The Source of Sin)**.

:::reveal at=7

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & The Rose School Schism

The deep metaphysical origin of the **Rose School of Thought** is revealed: the Indulgence Faction draws directly on the corrupted aura of the Mother Tree of Desire attempting to reclaim the Tenebrous World, while **Miss Messenger (Reinette Tinekerr)** and the Temperance Faction fight to maintain spiritual restraint.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Containment of the Abyss

Following the stabilization of the world barrier, the Church of the Fool and Chained Angel Reinette Tinekerr maintain strict surveillance over the sealed Tenebrous World, suppressing the descent of the Mother Tree of Desire.
