## Overview

The **River of Eternal Darkness (The Styx of Rest / The Flow of Eternal Night)** is the supreme **Sefirah** governing the **Darkness (Sleepless)**, **Death (Corpse Collector)**, and **Twilight Giant (Warrior)** pathways. Manifesting as an immense, silent subterranean torrent of pitch-black water flowing beneath the deepest strata of the **Underworld** and Calderón City, the River of Eternal Darkness embodies the supreme Great Old One authority of **Eternal Darkness (Calamity of Destruction / Singularity of Calamity)**.

Possessing the absolute divine power of eternal slumber, soul pacification, and final termination, the River's waters can silence any spiritual consciousness—including the residual wills of Pillar-level deities.

:::reveal at=7

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & The Pale Springs

Klein Moretti infiltrates Calderón City to harvest a flask of water from the River of Eternal Darkness to fulfill his agreement with the **Evernight Goddess**.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Great Old One of Darkness

The **Evernight Goddess (Amanises)** accommodates the Death and Twilight Giant Uniquenesses, drawing upon the River of Eternal Darkness to achieve partial Great Old One status. Amanises uses the river's water to lull the Celestial Worthy's awakening to sleep, ensuring Klein's soul survives his apotheosis.
