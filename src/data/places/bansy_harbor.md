## Overview

**Bansy Harbor** was an eerie, fog-shrouded transit port located on an isolated island along the trade routes between Loen and the Rorsted Archipelago. Known for its quiet fishing community, bizarre roasted meat culinary specialties, and odd local dialect, Bansy harbored a horrifying supernatural secret: it was the ancient sacrificial sanctuary of the Fourth Epoch **Medici Family** and the **Red Angel Evil Spirit**.

:::reveal at=3

## Volume 3: Legend of the Pirate Hunter & The Destruction of Bansy

Klein Moretti docks at Bansy aboard the *White Agate*, narrowly escaping an ambush by corrupted cannibalistic cultists. The **Church of the Lord of Storms** responds with overwhelming divine wrath, deploying Archbishop Jahn Kottman and naval fleets to bombard the island and purge the heresy with lightning.

:::

:::spoiler volume=4

## Volume 4: Undoing the Past & The Medici Altars

Investigations reveal that Bansy's sacrificial altars were designed to assist the resurrection of the **Red Angel Evil Spirit (Medici)**, linking the island's ruins directly to the conspiracies of the Tudor Dynasty.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Sealed Sea Route

In Volume 8, Bansy Harbor remains an uninhabited, sealed maritime monument, monitored by the Church of Storms and Tarot Club scouts to ensure no ancient blood rituals reignite.

:::
