## Overview

**Bansy Harbor** was an ancient, isolated colonial port town situated on a rugged island in the northern **Sonia Sea**, serving as a mandatory maritime coaling and resupply station for passenger liners sailing between Pritz Harbor and Bayam (including the passenger ship *White Agate*). 

Beneath its seemingly quiet, Victorian fishing-town facade lay a horrifying Fourth Epoch dark heritage: the entire island was originally a sacred sacrificial settlement founded by descendants of the Fourth Epoch's **Medici Family (The Red Angel)**, where cannibalistic flesh rituals, weather curses, and human sacrifices were secretly preserved across centuries.

## The Bansy Custom & The Green-Haired Abominations

- **The Cursed Custom**: When dark storms descended over the harbor, the residents sealed their doors, roasted human flesh with secret spices, and chanted ancient incantations to invoke the blood of the Red Angel.
- **Physical Mutations**: Prolonged consumption of cursed sacrificial flesh caused residents to develop thick, green seaweed-like hair, bloodshot eyes, and extreme resistance to physical trauma.

## Volume 3: Legend of the Pirate Hunter & The White Agate Incident

When the *White Agate* is forced to anchor in Bansy Harbor during an eerie unnatural storm, Gehrman Sparrow (Klein Moretti) and Danitz discover the cannibalistic rituals. Klein slays the mutated innkeeper and uncovers the lingering spiritual aura of the **Red Angel Evil Spirit (Medici)**.

Following Klein's escape, the **Church of the Lord of Storms** recognizes the revival of the ancient evil spirit and mobilizes an ironclad fleet, subjecting the entire island of Bansy Harbor to apocalyptic celestial lightning bombardments, incinerating the port to pure ash.
