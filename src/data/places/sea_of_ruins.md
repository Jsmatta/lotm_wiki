## Overview

The **Sea of Ruins (The Sunken Graveyard of Empires)** is a perilous, uncharted marine zone situated in the deep reaches of the **Sonia Sea**, shrouded in eternal gray fog, localized spatial distortions, sudden magnetic anomalies, and ancient oceanic relics. Formed during the cataclysms of the Second and Third Epochs when ancient elf kingdoms, giant outposts, and divine islands sank beneath the waves, the Sea of Ruins is avoided by ordinary merchant ships and navigated only by the boldest pirate admirals.

Home to legendary mythical aquatic beasts, ghost galleons, and the elusive **Mermaids**, the Sea of Ruins serves as the primary setting for the latter half of Volume 3 (Legend of the Pirate Hunter).

## Mystical Flora & Fauna

- **The Mermaids**: Mythical aquatic beings whose melodious, ethereal songs soothe the spiritual sea of high-sequence Beyonders; their singing is the mandatory ritual condition to safely consume the **Sequence 5: Marionettist** potion.
- **Sunken Cathedrals & Relics**: Submerged obsidian palaces from the Second Epoch, filled with lingering divine curses, corrupted sea serpents, and ancient treasures.

## Volume 3: Legend of the Pirate Hunter & The Voyage of the Future

Gehrman Sparrow boards **Admiral of Stars Cattleya's** flagship, **The Future**, navigating past temporal eddies, ghost ships, and Frank Lee's bizarre botanical experiments. 

Deep within the Sea of Ruins, Gehrman locates a pod of singing mermaids, safely ingesting the Marionettist potion to advance to Sequence 5 while discovering the ancient grimoire **Groselle's Travels**.
