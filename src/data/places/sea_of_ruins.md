## Overview

The **Sea of Ruins** is an enigmatic, supernatural maritime expanse located in the southern waters of the **Sonia Sea**. Littered with bizarre spatial anomalies, submerged Fourth Epoch cities, distorted currents, and ancient battlefields from the Cataclysm, the region is shunned by ordinary sailors and navigated only by the most daring pirate admirals.

It is home to the elusive **Mermaids**, whose singing is essential for Seer pathway Beyonders digesting the **Sequence 5: Marionettist** potion.

:::reveal at=3

## Volume 3: Legend of the Pirate Hunter & The Mermaid Singing

Aboard the *Future*, Klein Moretti journeys into the Sea of Ruins. After locating a pod of singing mermaids, Klein successfully drinks the Marionettist potion and completes his advancement amidst ancient ruins.

:::

:::spoiler volume=6

## Volume 6: Clues to the Cataclysm

Archaeological explorations in the Sea of Ruins reveal connections to the ancient Second Epoch Elven Kingdom and the sinking of ancient continental shelves during the Original Creator's awakening.

## Volume 8: The Boundary of the Western Continent

In Volume 8, the Sea of Ruins serves as the eastern staging ground where **Alger Wilson** and the Tarot Club navigate mystical spatial barriers to establish contact with the enigmatic **Western Continent**.
