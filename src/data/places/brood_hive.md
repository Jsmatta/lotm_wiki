## Overview

The **Brood Hive (The Primordial Womb / The Source of Life and Proliferation)** is the supreme **Sefirah** governing the **Moon (Apothecary/Vampire)** and **Mother (Planter)** pathways, originally ripped away from the Outer Deity **Mother Goddess of Depravity (Origin of Evil / The Indestructible)** by the Original Creator's awakening during the Pre-Epoch.

Manifesting as a colossal, pulsating biological hive of embryonic sacs, organic tendrils, and primordial amniotic oceans sealed beneath the western barrier of Earth, the Brood Hive represents the Pillar authority of supreme biological fertility, life proliferation, and genetic mutation.

:::reveal at=7

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & The Outer Corruption

The nature of the Brood Hive is revealed to the Tarot Club: because a portion of her Pillar Sefirah was ripped away and sealed on Earth, the Mother Goddess of Depravity relentlessly assaults Earth's barrier to reclaim it.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: Lilith & The Defense of the Barrier

The Earth Mother (**Lilith**) accommodates the Moon and Mother Uniquenesses, tapping into the sealed Brood Hive to resist the extraterrestrial corruption of the Mother Goddess of Depravity, defending Earth's biosphere ahead of the coming apocalypse.
