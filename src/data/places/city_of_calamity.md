## Overview

The **City of Calamity (The City of Fire and Ruin / The Incinerated Necropolis)** is the supreme **Sefirah** governing the **Red Priest (Hunter)** and **Demoness (Assassin)** pathways. Sealed deep beneath the Northern Continent in the dimensional substratum, the City of Calamity manifests as an immense, burning iron fortress-city engulfed in eternal crimson hellfire, molten sulfur, and primordial plagues.

Representing the Great Old One authority of **Calamity of Destruction (The Origin of Catastrophe / The Primordial Fire)**, the City of Calamity is the ultimate prize contested by the **Primordial Demoness (Cheek)**, **Medici (Red Angel)**, and high-sequence Hunter demigods.

:::reveal at=7

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & The Ancient Sealing

The City of Calamity's sealing marks and influence are traced back to the Fourth Epoch's War of the Four Emperors and the Blood Emperor Alista Tudor's catastrophic collapse.
