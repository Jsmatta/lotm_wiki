## Overview

**Chernobyl** is the earth-shattering pre-epoch historical revelation discovered by **Klein Moretti** in the northern reaches of the **Forsaken Land of the Gods**. Deep beneath ancient radioactive ruins, Klein uncovers an underground fallout shelter containing Cyrillic inscriptions, nuclear warning symbols, and the rusted sign: **"ЧЕРНОБЫЛЬ" (Chernobyl)**.

This discovery obliterates Klein's long-held assumption that he transmigrated to an alien fantasy world: **the world of Lord of the Mysteries is Earth in the distant future**, having survived a cataclysmic pre-epoch apocalypse caused by the awakening of the **Original Creator**.

:::reveal at=6

## Volume 6: Light of the City of Silver & The Truth of Earth

Standing before the rusted Chernobyl shelter, Klein confronts the horrifying truth: there is no path "home" to modern Earth—modern civilization collapsed millennia ago, and his transmigrated soul was merely awakened from an ancient sleep in Sefirah Castle.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: The Pre-Epoch Legacy

Klein pieces together the origins of the Ancient Sun God, who walked out of the Chernobyl shelter in the Second Epoch carrying the First Blasphemy Slate to rebuild human civilization.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Liberation of the Cocooned

Above Sefirah Castle, Klein examines the cocooned souls suspended by the Celestial Worthy since the pre-epoch era. Before entering his divine slumber, Klein severs their bindings, releasing these fellow modern humans into the peaceful post-war world.
