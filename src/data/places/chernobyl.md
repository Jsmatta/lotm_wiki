## Overview

**Chernobyl (The Cradle of the Sun God / The Pre-Epoch Ruins)** is a buried subterranean ruins complex situated deep beneath the northern mountains of the **Forsaken Land of the Gods**. Marked by rusted iron fences, decaying reinforced concrete domes, radioactive warning trefoils, and yellowed Cyrillic signs reading **ЧЕРНОБЫЛЬ**, this site is the monumental location where **Klein Moretti (Zhou Mingrui)** discovers the horrifying truth of his existence: the mystical world of the Northern and Southern Continents is not an alien fantasy planet, but **Earth itself, countless millennia after an apocalyptic pre-epoch cataclysm**.

## The Rebirth of the Ancient Sun God

- **The Underground Research Facility**: A pre-epoch underground nuclear and biological shelter containing cryogenic cocoon chambers and the subterranean gateway to the **Chaos Sea**.
- **The Awakening**: Millennia after the Original Creator's awakening destroyed human civilization, the modern human scientist transmigrated and crawled out of the Chernobyl shelter, holding the first Blasphemy Slate to become the **Ancient Sun God (The Creator)**.

## Volume 6: Light of the City of Silver & The Shattering Truth

Amon captures Klein Moretti and escorts him across the Forsaken Land toward Chernobyl. Standing before the cracked concrete reactor domes and reading the ancient Russian Cyrillic signage, Klein realizes with profound grief that his hometown, modern civilization, and loved ones were obliterated thousands of years ago.

This shattering revelation transforms Klein's motivation from a nostalgic search for a way home into an unyielding vow to protect the living humans of this new era.
