## Overview

**Belltaine City** was a peaceful, prosperous commercial city in the south-central **Loen Kingdom**, surrounded by rolling pastoral hills, granaries, and railway networks. 

The city became the apocalyptic focal point of the **Sealed Artifact 0-02 Crisis** in Volume 7 (Secrets of the Cosmos), when the renegade Church of Evernight Archbishop **Larrion** brought the terrifying Grade 0 Sealed Artifact **0-02: The Trunsoest Brass Book** into the municipality, subjecting all tens of thousands of citizens to an inescapable, lethal legal enclosure.

## The Law Domain of Sealed Artifact 0-02

Upon activation within Belltaine City, the Trunsoest Brass Book autonomously manifested ancient divine imperial statutes from the Fourth Epoch:
- **Absurd Prohibitions**: Forbidding leaving municipal boundaries, walking faster than a set pace, speaking above whisper volume, or using unauthorized heat sources—with automatic death penalties for any infraction.
- **Inescapable Enclosure**: All spatial teleportation, spirit-world travel, and telepathic communications were completely severed.

## Volume 7: Secrets of the Cosmos & The Belltaine Containment

**Madam Arianna (Servant of Concealment)** and **Klein Moretti (The World)** enter Belltaine City to rescue the trapped populace. Navigating the deadly, evolving statutes through legal loopholes and miracle wishes, Klein and Arianna neutralize Larrion, safely containing Sealed Artifact 0-02 and lifting the death sentences over Belltaine City.
