## Overview

**Belltaine City** is a strategic border fortress city located on the mountainous frontier between the **Loen Kingdom** and the **Feysac Empire**. Boasting heavy stone ramparts and massive garrison barracks, it served as a critical operational staging ground during the World War.

It is infamous as the location where the terrifying Grade 0 Sealed Artifact **Trunsoest Brass Book** awakened, turning the city into a living prison of arbitrary executions.

:::reveal at=7

## Volume 7: The Brass Book Crisis

The Trunsoest Brass Book writes tyrannical laws across Belltaine City, executing anyone who speaks, moves, or casts spells. Klein Moretti (as Merlin Hermes) and the Church of Evernight intervene, using concealment and clever legal loopholes to seal the book and liberate the surviving citizens.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: Post-War Rebuilding

In Volume 8, Belltaine City is demilitarized and rebuilt as a symbol of Loen-Feysac reconciliation, with joint administrative councils overseeing peace along the northern border.

:::
