## Overview

The **Key of Light (The Origin of Destiny / The Axis of Probability)** is the supreme **Sefirah** governing the **Wheel of Fortune (Monster)** pathway. Sealed in the deepest cosmic folds of the universe beyond the spatial fabric of Earth, the Key of Light manifests as an ever-shifting, kaleidoscopic stream of silver mercury light, celestial gears, and infinite branching probability vectors representing the **River of Fate**.

Embodying the Great Old One authority of **Key of Light (The Endless Loop / The Arbiter of Destiny)**, it is the singular celestial source of power sought by the two rival Snakes of Mercury: **Will Auceptin (The Snake of Fate)** and **Ouroboros (The Tail-Devourer)**.
