## Overview

The **Forsaken Land of the Gods (The Cursed Continent / The Land of Eternal Night)** is a vast, desolate realm in the eastern half of the world that was violently sundered from the Northern and Southern Continents during the Third Epoch's Cataclysm following the fall of the **Ancient Sun God**. Severed from the Astral Realm and encased in an impenetrable divine barrier, the region was abandoned by the Orthodox Deities and plunged into perpetual pitch-black darkness.

Deprived of sunlight, clean water, and normal flora for over 2,500 years, the land is illuminated only by frequent, violent lightning storms, and is inhabited by horrifying Second Epoch abominations, shadow wraiths, and the last surviving human enclaves: the **City of Silver** and **Moon City**.

## The Harsh Realities of Survival

- **Eternal Night & The Darkness Curse**: Living beings that linger in darkness without flame or lightning are quickly devoured or mutated into mindless shadow wraiths.
- **The Dead's Curse**: Deceased mortals do not rest in peace; their corpses spontaneously reanimate as vengeful evil spirits unless properly purified by holy light or consumed.
- **Black-Patterned Grass**: The sole surviving resilient crop capable of absorbing toxic soil minerals to sustain mortal life.
