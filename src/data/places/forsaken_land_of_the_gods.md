## Overview

The **Forsaken Land of the Gods** (ancient eastern portion of the pre-Cataclysm continent) is a desolate, nightmarish wasteland severed from the rest of the world since the end of the Third Epoch. Cursed by the catastrophic fallout of the Cataclysm and abandoned by sunlight, the region has endured over 2,500 years of perpetual supernatural darkness punctuated only by erratic crimson lightning storms.

Humanity survives here in isolated, besieged enclaves—most famously the **City of Silver**—subsisting on black-patterned grass and facing monstrous abominations whose corpses turn into evil spirits upon death.

:::reveal at=2

## Volume 2: The Reports from the City of Silver

Through the Tarot Club, **Derrick Berg (The Sun)** shares grim reports of expeditions across the dark wasteland, hunting deformed monsters and seeking an exit to the outside world.

:::

:::spoiler volume=6

## Volume 6: Light of the City of Silver & The Trek with Amon

Amon kidnaps Klein Moretti into the Forsaken Land of the Gods, forcing him on a harrowing trek across its desolate ruins. Klein discovers **Chernobyl**, uncovers the truth of ancient Earth, and coordinates with Chief **Colin Iliad** to open the doorway in the **Giant King's Court**.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: The Great Exodus

The City of Silver and Moon City complete their historic exodus through the Giant King's Court into the outside world, setting eyes upon the real sun for the first time in millennia. The cursed wasteland is left largely empty, save for the subterranean roars of the True Creator and the Chaos Sea.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Sealed Homeland

In Volume 8, the Forsaken Land of the Gods remains sealed behind the astral boundary. Its ancient history is revered by the New City of Silver as a sacred crucible that forged the courage of their people under the guidance of **The Fool**.

:::
