## Overview

The **Rorsted Archipelago (The Islands of the Sea God)** is a strategically vital chain of tropical islands situated in the northern **Sonia Sea**, colonized by the **Loen Kingdom** during the Fourth and Fifth Epoch maritime expansions. Centered around the capital island of **Bayam**, the archipelago is world-renowned for its lucrative spice plantations, rubber trees, gold mines, and deep maritime trade channels.

Deeply divided between wealthy colonial plantation owners, the dominating presence of the **Church of the Lord of Storms**, the native **Resistance**, and roving pirate armadas, the archipelago is a hotbed of geopolitical tension.

## Culture, Beliefs & Indigenous Resistance

- **The Faith of Sea God Kalvetua**: For centuries, the indigenous population revered the ancient sea serpent **Kalvetua** as their supreme deity, offering blood sacrifices and prayers for safe voyages.
- **The Colonial Resistance**: Underground native guerrillas fighting against Loen military garrisons to end forced labor on sugar and spice plantations.
- **The Seven Pirate Admirals**: Using the archipelago's secluded coves and uncharted inlets as resupply bases and black market trading ports.
