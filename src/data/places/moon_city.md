## Overview

**Moon City** is a tragic, isolated enclave of mutated human survivors enduring the perpetual horrors of the **Forsaken Land of the Gods**. Unlike the City of Silver which preserved substantial Second Epoch martial knowledge, Moon City was founded by worshippers of an ancient lunar deity, suffering severe physical deformities, ocular mutations, and genetic degeneration over thousands of years of consuming toxic mutated monsters.

Led by High Priest Nim, the citizens lived in squalid stone caverns under the faint glow of the Crimson Moon, on the brink of total extinction.

:::reveal at=6

## Volume 6: Light of the City of Silver & The Fool's Salvation

Klein Moretti discovers Moon City during his travels in the Forsaken Land of the Gods. Acting as The Fool, Klein bestows pure spring water and food, curing their spiritual poisoning and granting them hope of salvation.

:::

:::spoiler volume=7

## Volume 7: Exodus to Sunlight

The citizens of Moon City follow the City of Silver through the doorway of the Giant King's Court, stepping out into the sunlight of the outside world.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: Cured Under The Fool

In Volume 8, Moon City establishes a new settlement on the coastal plains near the New City of Silver. Blessed by the miraculous healing of **The Fool**, their children are born completely free of mutations, thriving as devoted members of the Church of the Fool.

:::
