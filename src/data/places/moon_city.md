## Overview

**Moon City** is a tragic, desperate human settlement situated deep within the eastern mountains of the **Forsaken Land of the Gods**. Suffering under severe genetic degradation, environmental radiation, and high-concentration mystical pollution for millennia, the citizens of Moon City were born with severe physical deformities, extra limbs, warped eyes, and short lifespans.

Despite their horrifying physical mutations and the despair of surviving on cursed monster meat and toxic moss, the people of Moon City maintained an unyielding, desperate faith in the prophecy of a coming savior who would bring true sunlight.

## Society & The High Priest's Devotion

- **High Priest Nim**: The wise, deformed leader of Moon City who preserved ancient stone tablets prophesying the return of the Sun God.
- **Physical Mutations**: Deformities passed down through generations due to consuming contaminated mythical beast flesh in the absence of normal crops.

## Volume 6: Light of the City of Silver & The Miracle of The Fool

Klein Moretti (as Gehrman Sparrow / Merlin Hermes) encounters the scouting team of Moon City in the darkness. Klein displays the miracle of pure white bread and healing potions, performing the ritual of Mr. Fool to purify their corruptions.

The High Priest leads the entire city in bowing before the holy emblem of The Fool, embracing the church as their sole salvation.

:::

## Volume 7 & Volume 8: The Exodus to the Sea

Alongside the City of Silver, the surviving citizens of Moon City pass through the bronze gates of the Giant King's Court. Resettled in the coastal warmth of **Bayam**, their corruptions are healed by the **Church of the Fool**, allowing newborn generations to be born whole and healthy.
