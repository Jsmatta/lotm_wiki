## Overview

The **Chaos Sea (The Primordial Ocean / The Source of Light and Wisdom)** is the supreme **Sefirah** governing the **Sun**, **Tyrant (Sailor)**, **White Tower (Reader)**, **Hanged Man (Secrets Suppliant)**, and **Visionary (Spectator)** pathways. Situated beneath the physical crust of the Earth and manifesting as a boundless, boiling ocean of incandescent golden-black liquid chaos, the Chaos Sea is the source of omnipotence and omniscience for **God Almighty (The Creator / The Maker of All Things)**.

Containing the original **First Blasphemy Slate**, the Chaos Sea is the divine engine that fueled the Third Epoch reign of the **Ancient Sun God** and the Fifth Epoch apotheosis of **Adam**.

:::reveal at=6

## Volume 6: Light of the City of Silver & The Chernobyl Descent

The subterranean entrance to the Chaos Sea is discovered beneath the ruins of **Chernobyl** in the Forsaken Land of the Gods, guarded by the residual spirit of Dark Angel Sasrir.

:::

:::spoiler volume=8

## Volume 8: The Dual God Almighty Apotheosis

**Adam** merges with the **True Creator**, establishing complete control over the Chaos Sea. Drawing on the boundless authority of the Primordial Ocean, Adam ascends as the **Half-God Almighty**, holding back the Orthodox Gods and assisting Klein Moretti in resisting Amon above the gray fog.

:::
